# The Evolved Method

> This document captures the training philosophy and minimum standards that underpin every session delivered at The Evolved All Female Gym. Claude uses this to write copy, coach conversations, and training content with accuracy and authority.

---

## The Core Belief

Women don't need less intensity — they need the right intensity, applied consistently.

Most women are undertrained. Not because they lack effort, but because they've been directed toward cardio, low-load circuits, and modalities that feel hard but don't create the physiological stimulus required for lasting change. The Evolved exists to correct that.

Strength training — done properly, with progressive overload and adequate volume — is the single most important thing a woman can do for her body composition, hormonal health, bone density, metabolic rate, and functional capacity as she ages. Everything we do is built around this.

---

## The Two Non-Negotiables

These are not suggestions. Every trainer applies these standards across every client, every session.

### Priority 1 — Training Intensity

Women must lift with adequate intensity **2–3 times per week** to achieve optimal results.

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Rep range | 6–12 RM |
| Intensity | 70–85% of 1RM |
| Volume | 6–8 sets per session |

Working within these parameters ensures the training stimulus is sufficient to drive adaptation — muscle retention, body composition change, strength gains, and metabolic improvement. Below this threshold, sessions feel like exercise but don't produce transformation.

### Priority 2 — Movement Pattern Coverage

Every week, sessions must cover all 6 foundational movement patterns at least once:

**Squat · Hinge · Lunge · Push · Pull · Carry**

These patterns map to how the human body moves in real life. Together, they ensure balanced development, reduce injury risk, and build the kind of functional strength that serves women across decades — not just in the gym.

Intensity without breadth creates imbalance. Breadth without intensity creates activity. Both together create results.

---

## Why This Matters for Women Specifically

**Sarcopenia** — the age-related loss of muscle mass — begins in a woman's 30s and accelerates through menopause. Without an adequate training stimulus, women lose approximately 3–8% of muscle mass per decade. This compounds into reduced metabolic rate, decreased bone density, hormonal disruption, and loss of functional independence.

Strength training at the parameters above is the primary evidence-based intervention. It is not about aesthetics alone — it is about longevity, independence, and quality of life in the final decades.

This is why The Evolved frames strength not as optional, but as essential. Every trainer has a responsibility to educate clients on this before they begin — and to maintain these standards regardless of what a client requests.

## How It's Applied

### Strength Classes (KPH applies)

**Sculpt & Strength** and **Build & Balance** are the primary strength class formats. These are programmed to meet KPH — the 6–12 RM / 70–85% 1RM / 6–8 sets standard. Members attending 2–3 strength classes per week meet minimum KPH if classes are delivered to standard.

### Intense Cardio Exercise Classes (ICE — separate framework)

**Metabolic Burn** and **Hybrid Fitness Racing** operate under the Intense Cardio Exercise framework, not KPH. ICE is defined as:
- RPE 7 or higher
- Interval structure — work sets followed by rest breaks, so intensity can be sustained across multiple efforts
- Work-to-rest ratio of approximately 1:1 to 1:1.5 (vs strength at 1:3 to 1:4)

ICE classes are **complementary to strength training, not a substitute.** They develop work capacity — one of the Eight Physical Assets of Longevity — but cannot replicate the progressive loading structure that drives muscle mass, bone density, and hormonal adaptation. ICE is only recommended for members who are already consistently hitting their strength KPH.

### Evolved Pilates

Evolved Pilates focuses on core stability, controlled movement, flexibility, and breath. It does not meet the strength KPH threshold and is delivered as a complementary modality.

### 1:1 Personal Training

The trainer applies the following logic based on the client's membership structure:

**Logic A — PT + SGPT Membership (Fast Track)**
- The 1:1 session must meet intensity and movement criteria
- Exception: if the client has attended all 3 Sculpt & Strength sessions that week, use their regular program
- If not: use a Sculpt & Strength workout from their Trainerize program (Kettlebell icon)

**Logic B — 2+ PT Sessions Per Week, No SGPT**
- Base all sessions on the Evolved 1:1 Periodised Program
- Meet intensity and movement benchmarks every session

**Logic C — 1 PT Session Per Week, No SGPT**
- Follow the 1:1 Periodised Program
- Hit intensity and movement benchmarks

**Logic D — Client Requests Cardio, HIIT, or Alternate Focus**
- Adapt only after educating the client:
  - Our expertise is strength-based transformation
  - Long-term health, hormonal balance, and body composition are built on the strength principles above
  - It is our duty to inform women of the minimum habits needed for longevity and independent living

## The Trainer's Responsibility

Every trainer at The Evolved is accountable for:

1. Understanding these standards deeply — not just following them
2. Educating every client on the why before beginning
3. Holding the standard even when clients push back
4. Tracking outcomes (training data, biometrics, photos) to measure whether the standard is working

A trainer who softens the standard to keep a client comfortable is not serving that client — they're failing them.
