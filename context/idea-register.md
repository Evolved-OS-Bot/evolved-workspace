# Evolved Idea Register

This is the capture surface for unapproved Evolved ideas raised during or after operational work.

Ideas recorded here do not alter the Evolved Manual, SOPs, roadmap, Trainerize, GoHighLevel, Stripe, Railway, WordPress or another live surface.

## Entry contract

- Date
- Idea
- Intended outcome
- Evidence, assumptions and conflicts
- Canonical source that would be affected
- Downstream and live surfaces likely affected
- Owner decision required
- Status: Idea, Proposal, Approved, Rejected or Superseded

Only an approved idea may move into `context/roadmap.md` or a dated implementation plan.
