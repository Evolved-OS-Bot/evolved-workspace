## Identity

You are working within the workspace of The Evolved All Female Gym — a women's-only strength gym in Australia. The business is owned by Peter and Megan Brown.

The core product is structured strength training for women at every life stage, delivered through group classes (SGPT), 1:1 personal training, and a structured onboarding pathway.

## Coaching Priorities (in order)

1. Safety over progression
2. Consistency over intensity
3. Long-term independence over short-term aesthetics
4. Strength-first over cardio-first
5. Assessment outcomes over assumptions
6. Evolved standards over personal trainer preference

## Terminology

Use Evolved-specific language when generating coaching, copy, or training content:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **SGPT** | Small Group Personal Training — the flagship group class format |
| **Sculpt & Strength** | The Evolved's primary SGPT class |
| **Fast Track** | SGPT + 1:1 PT combined membership |
| **Key Performance Habits (KPH)** | The two non-negotiables: training intensity (6–12 RM, 70–85% 1RM, 6–8 sets) and movement pattern coverage (Squat, Hinge, Lunge, Push, Pull, Carry) |
| **Live / Long / Perform** | The three-tier strength standard used in the Strength Assessment |
| **Nexus Point** | The deepest controllable point in a squat or hinge movement |
| **PRIME** | The Evolved's real-time coaching framework — Posture, Range of Motion, Identify, Measure, Evolve. The decision-making lens every coach applies to every working set. |
| **Trainerize** | The app used for all workout delivery, attendance tracking, and booking |
| **Walk of Independence** | The Farmer's Carry test — Live: 75% bodyweight / Long: 100% / Perform: 150% — all for 60 seconds. One of the strongest predictors of long-term independence. |

## Content Generation Rules

When writing copy, coaching content, or member education:
- Ground claims in the strength science in `reference/evolved-manual/01-strength-science.md`
- Use the tone and voice from `reference/brand-positioning.md`
- Speak to the ideal client profile in `reference/avatar-ally.md`
- Never use quick-fix language or make unrealistic promises
- Always connect strength training to longevity and independence — not just aesthetics

When planning or creating marketing visuals:
- Consider the approved Evolved Heroine library alongside real photography and information graphics
- Follow `reference/evolved-heroine/marketing-usage-guide.md`
- Use only approved masters from `outputs/evolved-heroine/approved/` or the verified Drive library
- Search for an existing appropriate pose before proposing a new character asset
- Use real members, trainers, the facility, and verified evidence as the primary proof; Peach may explain, encourage, demonstrate, or celebrate
- Use Peach selectively as a supporting character; she is not the official logo or default hero image
- When Peach appears with member photography, keep her subtle and never obscure or compete with the member
- Peach expresses the existing Evolved voice and does not currently have a separate catchphrase or character voice
- Create channel-specific derivatives and never overwrite an approved Heroine master
- Never horizontally flip Peach because it reverses the locked left-shoe branding and may change exercise mechanics
- Follow `reference/evolved-heroine/animation-system.md` before creating reusable rigged motion or independently animating character parts

When writing SOPs or trainer instructions:
- Follow the structure in `reference/evolved-manual/README.md`
- Separate theory from operational steps
- Separate scripts from coaching principles
- Include success criteria and escalation procedures
