# The Evolved — Member Policies
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-25

These policies apply to all members and clients. They exist to protect trainer time and commitment, and to support member accountability and results.

---

## SGPT Membership Policies

### Cancellation
- Minimum **30 days paid notice** required from date of form submission
- Submitted via The Evolved Membership Cancellation Form
- No downgrades permitted during cancellation period
- Holds and cancellations cannot run simultaneously — the 30-day period must always be paid in full
- 30-day notice may only be waived if a medical professional explicitly recommends cessation of all training (supported by medical report)
- All sales final — no refunds for payments already taken

### Membership Holds
- Minimum **14 days notice** required (waived if medical certificate supplied)
- Standard holds: 1–4 weeks per calendar year
- Extended holds: 5–12 weeks, require manager approval
- Cannot hold and cancel simultaneously
- Submit via The Evolved Membership Hold Form

### Billing
- Direct debits processed **one week in advance**
- Failed debits may incur additional fees
- Arrears may be recovered without further notice

### Cooling Off Period
- **48 hours** from signing date
- Cancellation within 48 hours: refund issued less admin fee (up to $75) and cost of any sessions provided or booked (onboarding sessions valued at $149 each, including those forfeited with <24hrs notice)
- After 48 hours: all standard cancellation terms apply, no refunds

### Session Booking
- SGPT sessions are 60 minutes, booked via the booking app
- Late arrivals do not extend session times

### Onboarding Sessions (SGPT)
- Treated as Personal Training
- Minimum **24 hours notice** required to reschedule
- Cancelling within 24 hours = session forfeited
- Replacement onboarding sessions charged at **$149**

## Personal Training (PT) Policies

### Rescheduling
- Use the reschedule link in the booking confirmation email or calendar invite
- Reschedule link **expires 24 hours before** the scheduled session
- Once expired: rescheduling is no longer possible and the session is forfeited
- All reschedule questions directed to gym phone: **0483 968 880**

### Cancellations (PT Sessions)
- Cancelling a session at any time for any reason = **session forfeited**
- Strictly non-refundable and non-creditable
- Exception only at The Evolved's discretion under extraordinary circumstances
- All questions directed to gym phone: **0483 968 880**

### PT Contract Cancellation
- Minimum **30 days notice** via The Evolved Personal Training Cancellation Form
- If on hold and choose to cancel: must complete 30-day paid cancellation period and return to training during that period
- No downgrades during cancellation period
- 30-day notice waived only if physician explicitly recommends avoiding all physical activity (medical report required)

### PT Holds / Pauses
- Minimum **14 days notice** before the debit date
- Submit via The Evolved Personal Training Hold Form

### Billing (PT)
- Payments taken via Stripe (card/direct debit) or approved upfront session packs
- Payments made **at least one week in advance**
- Unpaid sessions may be cancelled 24 hours prior to session start
- Failed debits: 3 consecutive weeks = plan cancelled and 30-day notice payment requested

### Session Packs
- Must be used within **6 months** of purchase date
- Minimum pack size: 20 sessions
- Remaining sessions after expiry are forfeited

### Cooling Off Period (PT)
- **48 hours** from signing date
- Same terms as SGPT cooling off above

### Session Timing
- Sessions begin and end at scheduled times
- Late arrivals do not extend session time
- Trainer unavailability: replacement trainer provided where possible; if not, session rescheduled at client's convenience

## Policy Decision Rules (for bot use)

### PT Reschedule — Within Policy
- Request made **more than 24 hours** before the session
- Draft response: confirm the reschedule is within policy, direct them to use the reschedule link in their booking confirmation

### PT Reschedule — Outside Policy (decline)
- Request made **within 24 hours** of the session, OR reschedule link has expired
- Draft response: advise session is forfeited per their signed agreement, express empathy, do not offer credit or refund

### SGPT Cancellation — Within Policy
- 30+ days notice from submission date
- Draft response: confirm cancellation notice received, advise last day and final payment date

### SGPT Cancellation — Outside Policy
- Less than 30 days notice, no medical certificate
- Draft response: advise 30-day notice period applies, confirm when their last payment will be

### Hold Request — Within Policy
- 14+ days notice before hold start date, hold duration 1–4 weeks, no active cancellation
- Draft response: direct to hold form if not yet submitted, confirm dates

### Hold Request — Outside Policy
- Less than 14 days notice (without medical certificate), or hold requested during cancellation period
- Draft response: advise minimum notice requirement or hold/cancel conflict as applicable
