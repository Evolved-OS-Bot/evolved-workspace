### Social Copy

**LinkedIn**

Most of the work I do at The Evolved never gets seen by members. Today was one of those days.

We've had a cancellation system running for months — a structured form that walks a member through their reason for leaving and presents relevant retention offers at each step. Financial pressure? Here's a fee waiver. Health issue? Here's a hold. Want to train somewhere else? Let's talk about what was missing.

The problem: by the time a member submits that form, they've already said no to everything. The automated retention logic ends there.

Today I built the human layer — 9 parallel workflows that fire the moment a cancellation form is submitted. One for each cancellation reason. Each one gives Piper (our retention coach) the exact context she needs from the form: what the member said, what they declined, and a scripted call framework for that specific situation.

The sequence: call within 24 hours, twice daily until reached, reason-specific re-offer, and as a last resort — a complimentary farewell 1:1 PT session before their membership ends. Not to guilt them. To show them how far they've come.

The average lifetime value of a cancelling member at The Evolved: $2,677. Every save matters.

The thing about retention systems is that the form is the easy part. The conversation is where it actually happens.
