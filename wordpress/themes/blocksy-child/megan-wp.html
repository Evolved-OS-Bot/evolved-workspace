From Dancer to Women's Strength Coach

Megan's journey into strength coaching began long before she took ownership of The Evolved. As a former dancer, she had always been connected to movement — but life had other plans.

Between the demands of running a business and becoming a mum twice over, Megan felt the same struggles many women face. Fatigue, injuries, and the never-ending juggle of responsibilities left her wondering how to prioritise her own body without burning out.

She knows what it feels like to push through pain, to doubt whether you have the time or energy, and to want to feel like yourself again.

“True strength isn't about doing more. It's about finding the right balance to support long-term health and energy.”

The Diagnosis That Changed Everything

In 2017, just 18 months after giving birth to her second child, Megan was diagnosed with chronic fatigue. Suddenly, everything she thought she knew about training and resilience was challenged.

She had to relearn how to move, how to fuel her body properly, and most importantly, how to leave enough energy in the tank for the demands of motherhood and running a business.

This difficult chapter reshaped her entire approach to health. Instead of chasing exhaustion, Megan discovered the power of training smarter and prioritising recovery. It became clear that true strength was not about doing more — but about finding the right balance to support long-term health and energy.

Growth, Recognition & The FitMum Method

Megan has been part of the fitness industry since 2013 and has owned and run body transformation businesses since 2014. Along the way, she qualified as a Metabolic Nutritionist and earned certifications in personal training and body transformation.

She sought out world-class mentorship, learning under Dr Paul Cribb PhD and Sharlene Sault, who helped refine her scientific approach to training and nutrition.

Drawing on her recovery from chronic fatigue, Megan created the FitMum Method — a program designed to help mums rebuild their strength and confidence postpartum without sacrificing their health.

Her work began to stand out across the fitness industry. In 2019, she was awarded Female Transformation Specialist of the Year — recognition of the results she was delivering and the compassion she brought to her coaching.

“Strength training should fit into your life — not the other way around.”

Megan's Coaching Philosophy

Megan believes strength training should fit into your life, not the other way around. Her philosophy is rooted in efficiency, injury prevention, and helping women feel in control of their bodies.

She understands the realities of fatigue, time pressures, and fluctuating energy — because she has lived them herself.

Having worked with hundreds of women across Brisbane, Megan has guided clients through every stage of life: from teenage years to post-menopause. Her coaching proves that strength training is not about punishment or extremes — it is about building a body that supports you in every season of life.

For Strong Women, Inside and Out

Megan's passion for strong women extends beyond the gym. She is a proud supporter of the arts, donating regularly to the Queensland Ballet in West End, and she loyally backs Women's Legal Service QLD — taking part in their annual River to Rooftop fundraiser.

For Megan, supporting these causes is part of the same mission she lives out every day: empowering women to stand taller, move with confidence, and live stronger lives.

How Megan Coaches at The Evolved

At The Evolved, Megan leads by example. She coaches women to build the strength they need — not only for the gym, but for the rest of their lives.

Whether it is helping a new mum regain confidence, guiding a woman through perimenopause, or coaching someone toward their first personal best, Megan brings empathy, expertise, and lived experience to every session.

She knows the challenges. She has walked the path. And she is here to make sure no woman has to do it alone.
