Meet Leisa

If you've ever felt stuck in a cycle of wanting change but not knowing how to get there, you're not alone. Leisa's journey to becoming a coach wasn't straightforward — it was shaped by years of struggle, self-doubt, and finally, breakthrough. And because she's walked that path herself, she has the wisdom and compassion to guide women toward the strength they've been searching for.

From Chaotic Kitchens to the Gym Floor

Leisa's story didn't begin with barbells and coaching sessions. For more than a decade she worked as a chef in Brisbane, grinding through 12–15 hour days in high-pressure kitchens. On paper it looked like a career filled with creativity and skill — but behind the scenes it was draining.

Long hours, weekend shifts, late nights, and the drinking culture that often comes with the industry began to chip away at her health. She used fitness as an escape, sneaking in workouts when she could — but her lifestyle and her goals were in constant conflict.

“I had a lack of real purpose. Life felt mundane. The job was chaotic, the industry was toxic, and it didn't align with the person I wanted to become.”

The Turning Point

Even in those years, Leisa always found herself drawn to training. She picked up her first barbell at 17, and for years lifted on her own — not really knowing if she was making progress. Eventually the frustration of running in circles pushed her to hire a coach. That decision was a game-changer.

With guidance, structure, and accountability, she finally began to see the strength gains and confidence she had been chasing for so long. At first it was about looking better. But as the process unfolded, her motivation shifted toward performance, health, and energy. That shift laid the foundation for everything that came next.

The Struggles and the Lessons

Of course, the journey wasn't easy. Leisa faced the same struggles many women know too well — body image battles, binge eating, drinking habits, overtraining, and the endless pressure to do more. There were countless times she wanted to quit.

What changed wasn't sheer willpower alone. It was learning that progress comes from being intentional, consistent, and patient. She discovered the importance of nutrition, recovery, stress management, and mindset — not just sets and reps.

“Even last week I felt like giving up. But I've learned that breakdowns always come with breakthroughs. You just have to keep going.”

The Breakthrough

Through years of trial, coaching, and reflection, Leisa transformed. Strength training gave her more than just physical results. It gave her the energy to wake up at 4:30 every morning, the confidence to run a marathon, and the resilience to handle life's challenges without falling back into old habits.

Her body composition changed, her mindset evolved, and she began to trust the process instead of chasing quick fixes. That trust and consistency became her superpower.

“Strength training makes me want to be a better human every single day. From the way I feel, to how I move, to the energy I bring — it shapes everything about my life.”

Why Leisa Coaches

For Leisa, becoming a personal trainer wasn't just about changing careers. It was about sharing what she had learned with other women who felt stuck — like she once did.

“I feel called to help women who don't see their own value, who doubt themselves. I'll hold your hand for a while if that's what you need — but my goal is to build you up so you can carry yourself. I want you to feel proud, capable, and confident.”

Leisa's Most Rewarding Coaching Moment

One of Leisa's most rewarding experiences has been working with a client named Renee, who faced breast cancer. Together they navigated her highs and lows, focusing on consistency and health above all else.

She celebrates every woman who comes in nervous to touch a barbell and leaves, months later, lifting weights they never thought possible.

“It's not about fully recovering in a straight line. It's about staying as strong and healthy as possible through each wave. Seeing Renee regain her energy, move with confidence, and look in the mirror with pride — that's why I do this.”

How Leisa Coaches Today

At The Evolved All Female Gym, Leisa offers 1:1 personal training tailored to women of all ages. Whether you're brand-new to strength training or have years of experience, she focuses on building consistency, confidence, and results that actually last.

Her approach goes beyond sets and reps. She helps clients align training with nutrition, stress management, and recovery — creating an environment where women feel safe, supported, and excited to train. A far cry from the intimidating atmosphere of commercial gyms.

When you train with Leisa, you can expect a safe, welcoming environment designed for women, personalised guidance to move past plateaus, support through setbacks — and consistent progress toward strength, confidence, and energy.
