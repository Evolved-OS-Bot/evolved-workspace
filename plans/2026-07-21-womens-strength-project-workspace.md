# Women's Strength Project: Sibling Workspace Plan

**Created:** 21 July 2026  
**Status:** Completed

## Objective

Create a standalone sibling workspace for the Women's Strength Project so research, data governance, product development and future partnerships can develop independently from day-to-day gym operations.

## Boundary

- The Evolved Gym remains the operating business and first assessment site.
- The Women's Strength Project owns the V2 assessment specification, research framework, analytical model and future platform intellectual property.
- The historical identified V1 dataset remains in the private Evolved workspace.
- Only the final de-identified V1 workbook is copied into the new workspace as seed material.
- Trainerize and GHL receive operational summaries only. They are not the canonical V2 research database.

## Initial structure

1. Workspace operating instructions and project charter
2. Research and evidence directories
3. Versioned assessment protocols
4. Protected raw and identified data zones
5. De-identified analytical data zone
6. Product and integration specifications
7. Plans, scripts and generated outputs

## Completion checks

- No identified V1 client data exists in the new workspace.
- Private and raw data directories are excluded from version control.
- The de-identified V1 workbook is copied without modification.
- The new workspace roadmap identifies the V2 data blueprint as the immediate next deliverable.
- The Evolved roadmap records the new sibling-workspace boundary.
