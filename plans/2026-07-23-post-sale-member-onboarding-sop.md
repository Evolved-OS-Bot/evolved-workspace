# Post-Sale Member Onboarding SOP Implementation Plan

**Status:** Complete
**Created:** 2026-07-23
**Owner:** Peter Brown

## Objective

Create one current post-sale onboarding procedure that joins the GHL sale handoff, Trainerize fulfilment, package-specific inclusions, first-session booking and independent quality control.

## Source Decisions

- A completed sale requires a signed membership agreement, successful upfront payment and a recorded membership start date.
- The consultant completes Trainerize fulfilment during the same appointment, before the member leaves.
- The existing Strength Assessment Trainerize account is reused. No duplicate client or second invitation is created.
- GHL assigns the consultant a next-day fallback task. Admin Eve receives an independent day-two quality-control task.
- The current membership products, Product Starts settings, 999 non-expiring class-credit model and package inclusions are authoritative.
- Historical Drive material that references ACR, PT Minder or old package names is superseded.
