## Objective

Build the first reusable, approved Apple Motion animation system for Peach, the Evolved Heroine.

The outcome is not a one-off animated clip. It is a controlled production system containing a layered character construction, reusable Apple Motion rig, approved expression and movement library, channel templates, transparent exports, QA evidence, documentation, and a clean handoff for continued marketing production.

## Non-Negotiable Character Controls

- Peach remains the complete body, never a peach head on a human torso.
- Preserve the approved pointed peach silhouette, vintage colour separation, deep-plum ink, cream highlights, coral shadows, facial identity, gloves, apparel, shoes, and print texture.
- Arms and legs remain simple mascot tubes attached directly to the fruit.
- Never introduce a human chest, waist, hips, pelvis, buttocks, groin, or anatomical limbs.
- Never horizontally flip Peach.
- `Evolved` remains only on the outward-facing side of the anatomical left shoe.
- Do not move, duplicate, misspell, mirror, or invent shoe text.
- Do not invent or approximate the official Evolved logo.
- Do not overwrite approved static masters.
- Animation is a deployment system and does not become a new static style authority.
