## Executive Decision

For the next 30 days, revenue work will concentrate on three outcomes:

1. Approve and launch a five-class SGPT capacity expansion.
2. Create enough verified PT delivery capacity to complete up to 20 Strong-to-Fast Track upgrades.
3. Establish and operate an early-retention system capable of preventing at least one avoidable cancellation per month.

These are one programme, not three independent campaigns. Class expansion and Fast Track both consume trainer time and floor capacity. Retention affects how much new capacity is required and must therefore be measured alongside acquisition and upgrades.

General systems cleanup, speculative platform work, new content engines and broad paid acquisition will not compete with this programme during the 30-day window.

| Lever | Calculation | Annualised gross value |
|---|---|---:|
| Five added SGPT classes, fully filled | 5 classes × 4 member places × $99 × 52 | $102,960 |

The combined figure is a strategic ceiling, not a forecast. It assumes all five classes are filled, all 20 upgrades remain active and one additional client is retained each month for a full-year run-rate effect.

It must not be used to double-count members. Retained members still occupy class capacity, and Fast Track members require both group access and a weekly 30-minute PT appointment.

### Outcome 2: Fast Track Upgrade Pilot

By 22 August:

- the active Strong and Fast Track rosters are reconciled across GHL, Stripe and Trainerize;
- ten weekly trainer hours are reserved for 20 additional 30-minute Fast Track sessions;
- existing Fast Track delivery is confirmed healthy before more members are added;
- eligible members are ranked using coaching need, assessment gaps, attendance, goals and schedule compatibility;
- a manual first cohort is offered Fast Track without discounting;
- the first ten upgrades are reviewed before the second cohort is released; and
- up to 20 completed upgrades are accepted only where a real recurring PT slot is assigned.

The commercial target is $52,000 annualised gross revenue. Ten completed upgrades represent the minimum validated result and $26,000 annualised; 20 is the stretch result for the 30-day window.

### Outcome 3: Early Retention

By 22 August:

- a rolling 13-week cancellation baseline exists by tier, tenure, reason and recurring revenue lost;
- the current monthly check-in SOP is reconciled with the audited member lifecycle;
- one accountable 30/60/90-day member-care process is operating;
- attendance decline, future-booking gaps, unresolved negative replies and hold-return risk generate a visible staff action;
- every red flag has an owner, due date, outcome and revenue-at-risk value;
- cancellation saves, holds, downgrades and genuine cancellations are reported separately; and
- at least one verified save is documented if one occurs.

A verified save requires evidence that the member was at material risk of cancelling, the intervention changed the outcome, and the member remained active. Routine positive check-ins must not be counted as saves.

One retained Strong member protects $5,148 annualised. One retained Fast Track member protects $7,748 annualised. Building to one additional retained client per month is worth approximately $58,962 in annualised run rate at the current blended value.

## Operating Guardrails

1. Do not launch broad paid acquisition until new SGPT capacity has an approved date and trainer.
2. Do not sell a Fast Track upgrade without an assigned recurring PT slot.
3. Do not place new peak PT demand into a time that compromises SGPT delivery.
4. Do not discount the $149 Fast Track membership to generate uptake.
5. Do not count a pipeline stage as an active paying member without GHL, Stripe and Trainerize reconciliation.
6. Do not count an offered hold or downgrade as a retention save until the member accepts it and the resulting state is verified.
7. Do not publish the unfinished Day 8–365 workflow shells individually.
8. Keep the first retention and upgrade pilots manual or staff-approved until the member response, task ownership and delivery capacity are proven.
9. No new automation may obscure which staff member owns a member conversation.
10. Coaching quality and member safety override the 30-day commercial target.

## Stop / Continue / Expand Rules

### Class Expansion

- **Stop:** no demand evidence, no qualified trainer or unacceptable floor conflict.
- **Continue:** bookings support the proposed time and coverage is stable.
- **Expand:** launched classes approach the agreed occupancy threshold without degrading existing sessions.

### Fast Track

- **Stop:** recurring slots cannot be assigned, onboarding is missed or trainer utilisation becomes unsafe.
- **Continue:** members accept at full price and weekly PT delivery is reliable.
- **Expand:** the first ten upgrades are fulfilled cleanly and the second ten have verified capacity.

### Retention

- **Stop:** generic high-frequency messaging produces noise without staff ownership.
- **Continue:** risks are identified earlier and assigned actions are completed.
- **Expand:** verified interventions reduce revenue loss without relying on blanket financial concessions.
