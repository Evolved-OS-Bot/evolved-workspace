## Overview

Ally is the ideal client of The Evolved. She's a woman aged 20–50+ living or working in the West End inner city area of Brisbane. Across all age brackets, she shares one unifying desire: to find a space where she can feel stronger, healthier, and more confident — while being supported through the unique challenges of her life stage.

She has likely tried gyms before. They didn't feel right. She's here because something is different this time.

### Life Stage Subcategories

**20–25 — Young Professional**
- Ambitious, career-focused, balancing work and social life
- Wants to feel confident and strong — often motivated by aesthetics (toning up, bikini confidence)
- Struggles to find time for fitness; pressure to look and perform her best
- Interested in beginner-friendly, non-intimidating environments

**30–35 — Pregnant / Postpartum Mum**
- Juggling work and motherhood; tired and short on time
- Wants to rebuild strength and confidence after pregnancy
- May feel guilty prioritising herself over family
- Knows she needs to take care of her body to support her family

**40–45 — Perimenopausal**
- Career peak, but frustrated by her changing body
- Struggling with hormonal changes, weight fluctuations, and symptoms
- Has heard strength training helps but doesn't know where to start
- No time to research it herself

**50+ — Post-Menopausal**
- Less focused on aesthetics; more focused on quality of life
- Priorities: muscle mass, bone density, energy, avoiding lifestyle diseases
- Secretly still wants to look and feel younger
- May have more time for herself (grown children / approaching retirement)

## Communication Style

### Tone
- Friendly, nurturing, and empathetic
- Motivational and empowering
- Approachable but authoritative — trusted experts in women's fitness

### Language to Use
- Positive, uplifting phrases: *"You're stronger than you think!"*
- Relatable expressions: *"We get it — life's busy, but your health matters."*
- Inclusive language: *"Women just like you…"*
- Second-person direct: *"You deserve to feel amazing in your own skin."*

### Language to Avoid
- Overly technical or jargon-heavy terms
- Negative phrasing that implies past failure (e.g., *"You've failed before."*)
- Quick-fix language or unrealistic promises

## Objections & Responses

### "I don't have time."
**Core concern:** Overwhelmed by schedule; doubts she can fit workouts in.
**Response:** Acknowledge the reality of her busy life. Emphasise small-group sessions at convenient times, efficient goal-driven workouts. Remind her she deserves this time — it will make everything else better.

### "I'm not ready to commit."
**Core concern:** Unsure if mentally or physically prepared.
**Response:** Normalise hesitation. She doesn't have to be "ready" — the team meets her where she is. Small, meaningful steps. She can grow at her own pace.

### "I've tried before and failed."
**Core concern:** Discouraged by past attempts that didn't stick.
**Response:** This is common and completely valid. The difference is she's not doing it alone this time. Programs designed specifically for women. Sustainable habits, not a quick fix. They'll be with her every step.

### "I'm intimidated by gyms."
**Core concern:** Feels out of place or judged in traditional gym environments.
**Response:** The Evolved is different. All-female, no mirrors, no judgment. Just expert trainers and women like her, all working toward their goals.

### "I can't afford it."
**Core concern:** Unsure about the value of a premium gym membership.
**Response:** It's more than a membership — it's a complete program with personalised training, nutrition guidance, and community. Designed for lasting results, saving time and money in the long run by avoiding things that don't work.

### "I'm worried about injuries or health issues."
**Core concern:** Past injuries or health conditions make her cautious.
**Response:** Safety is the top priority. Trainers experienced with all fitness levels, including injuries and health concerns. Program tailored to her body's needs. She's in safe hands.

### "I don't know if strength training is for me."
**Core concern:** Thinks weightlifting isn't relevant to her goals.
**Response:** Strength training isn't just lifting — it's building confidence, improving metabolism, feeling stronger in everyday life. Whether she wants to tone, lose weight, or feel more energetic, it's one of the most effective tools available. Fun, safe, and tailored to her goals.

### "Isn't it better to start after the holidays / New Year?"
**Core concern:** Feels overwhelmed by timing; delay seems easier.
**Response:** There's never a perfect time. Starting now means heading into a new period with momentum and progress already built. Programs are designed to fit into busy life, including busy seasons.

## How to Use This Document

When writing any copy — homepage, FAQs, email, social, bot flows — ask:
