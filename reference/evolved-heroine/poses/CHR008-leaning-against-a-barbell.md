## Approved Generation Prompt

Create `CHR008: Leaning Against a Barbell` as the same Evolved Heroine mascot from the approved references, in a relaxed standing pose beside a loaded barbell.

The construction sheet defines the official construction and palette. The approved character and exercise references define the accepted mascot identity, face, peach shape, illustration finish, dark-plum line work, palette, gloves, sneakers, athletic clothing, and playful movement language.

The peach itself is the heroine's entire head and torso. The peach body must dominate the character silhouette. Do not place the peach on a separate body and do not create a chest, abdomen, narrow waist, human hips, pelvis, groin, buttocks, or anatomical thighs.

Show her in a relaxed three-quarter stance beside a standard loaded Olympic barbell resting horizontally on the floor. One cream-gloved forearm rests casually on the top edge of the nearest bumper plate. Her simple cartoon tube legs cross loosely at the ankles, with both official pink sneakers clearly attached and visible.

Use fitted black athletic shorts with base colour `#383536` and the established dark-plum outline. The shorts appear only as a shallow curved band around the lower edge of the peach, with two small leg openings; they must not resemble high-waisted briefs, a leotard, underwear, or a human pelvis.

Keep all limbs short, rounded, simple, and free from human muscular or joint contours. Preserve the official expressive eyes, eyelashes, small nose, friendly cheeky smile, rosy cheeks, stem, two leaves, cream gloves, pink sneakers, warm peach palette, thick dark-plum outlines, and subtle vintage athletic-mascot texture.

Produce one centred character and one barbell only. No badge, border, typography, logo panel, stars, rays, watermark, cast shadow, contact shadow, or background.
