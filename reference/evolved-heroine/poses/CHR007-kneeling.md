## Approved Generation Prompt

Create `CHR007: Kneeling` as the same Evolved Heroine mascot from the approved references, in a compact neutral two-knee kneeling pose.

The construction sheet defines the official construction and palette. The approved character and exercise references define the accepted mascot identity, face, peach shape, illustration finish, dark-plum line work, palette, gloves, sneakers, and playful movement language.

The peach itself is the heroine's entire head and torso. The peach body must dominate the character silhouette. Do not place the peach on a separate body and do not create a chest, abdomen, narrow waist, human hips, pelvis, groin, buttocks, or anatomical thighs.

Show a compact three-quarter kneel on both knees. The lower edge of the peach sits directly above two short bent cartoon tube legs; each leg folds backward naturally, with both official pink sneakers clearly attached and visible.

Use fitted black athletic shorts with base colour `#383536` and the established dark-plum outline. The shorts appear only as a shallow curved band around the lower edge of the peach, with two small leg openings; they must not resemble high-waisted briefs, a leotard, underwear, or a human pelvis.

Both gloved hands rest lightly near the lower front of the peach or on the small tube-leg knees. Keep all limbs short, rounded, simple, and free from human muscular or joint contours.

Preserve the official expressive eyes, eyelashes, small nose, friendly cheeky smile, rosy cheeks, stem, two leaves, cream gloves, pink sneakers, warm peach palette, thick dark-plum outlines, and subtle vintage athletic-mascot texture.

Produce one centred character only. No equipment, badge, border, typography, logo panel, stars, rays, watermark, props, cast shadow, contact shadow, or background.
