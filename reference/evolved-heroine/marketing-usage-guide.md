# Evolved Heroine Marketing Usage Guide

**Status:** Operational v1; owner preferences confirmed and embedded across the workspace  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-04  
**Character:** Peach, the Evolved Heroine  

This guide governs how approved Evolved Heroine artwork is selected, adapted, and used in marketing. It complements:

- `reference/brand-positioning.md` for business positioning, voice, colours, typography, and photography
- `reference/marketing-playbook.md` for campaign strategy, content pillars, channels, and calls to action
- `reference/evolved-heroine/README.md` for character identity, approved-master, production, versioning, and technical rules

If a marketing treatment conflicts with the Heroine character rules, the approved master and the Heroine asset system win. If character usage conflicts with a member's dignity, privacy, or truthful evidence, the member and evidence rules win.

## Brand Role

Peach is The Evolved's visual guide, coach, encourager, educator, and celebrator. She makes strength, science, healthy habits, and progress feel approachable without weakening the brand's expertise.

Peach can:

- Introduce or explain an idea
- Demonstrate a movement or healthy habit
- Direct attention to a headline, fact, call to action, or next step
- Encourage a prospective or current member
- Celebrate progress, milestones, events, and community moments
- Create continuity across campaigns and recurring content series

Peach cannot:

- Replace real members as evidence of real results
- Pretend to be a member, trainer, clinician, or scientific authority
- Give unqualified medical advice or imply a health guarantee
- Manufacture a testimonial, transformation, result, or emotional experience
- Become a childish novelty that makes women's strength training feel trivial

## Visual Hierarchy

Choose the visual lead according to the marketing job:

| Marketing job | Primary visual | Peach's role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member story, testimonial, transformation, community proof | Authentic member or trainer photography | Optional supporting accent; never the evidence |
| Education, myth-busting, exercise explanation, healthy habit | Peach or a clear information graphic | Guide, demonstrator, or attention anchor |
| Community event or behind-the-scenes content | Authentic photography | Optional host, label, or celebratory accent |
| Milestone or achievement | Member evidence when available | Celebrator or badge-like supporting figure |
| Offer, trial, membership, or conversion campaign | Clear offer and appropriate real-world imagery | Supporting attention and CTA device, not proof |
| Seasonal, announcement, reminder, or lightweight engagement | Peach may lead | Branded host or messenger |
| Website trust and sales sections | Real people, facility, coaching, and evidence | Supporting navigation, explanation, or emphasis |

Default rule: real people prove the promise; Peach explains, encourages, and celebrates it.

## Composition Rules

- Keep Peach recognisable and preserve the complete intended silhouette.
- Do not stretch, squash, warp, redraw, or change facial features, clothing, props, colours, outlines, shadows, or highlights.
- Do not horizontally flip an asset. Flipping reverses the locked anatomical-left-shoe branding and can make exercise mechanics incorrect.
- Cropping is allowed only in a channel-specific derivative and only when it does not remove a meaningful gesture, prop, face, or required exercise position.
- Preserve adequate clear space around the face, gloves, shoes, and active prop.
- Keep text off the face and away from fine silhouette details.
- Use the approved transparent PNG master when building a derivative.
- Use Evolved brand colours behind Peach where contrast remains clear. Prefer neutral beige, light pink, soft plum, or restrained campaign colour fields over busy photography.
- When Peach appears over a photo, place her in a deliberately composed clear area. Do not cover a member's face, body, result, equipment interaction, or important environmental evidence.
- A subtle design shadow may be added to a derivative for separation, but it must not be baked into or mistaken for a new approved master.
- Do not place Peach inside an unrelated badge, sticker, costume, logo lock-up, or branded product without an approved campaign design.

## Logo, Text, and Copy

- Never alter or approximate The Evolved logo inside a character asset.
- Do not add pseudo-text or unapproved writing to shoes, clothing, equipment, flags, signs, certificates, books, phones, or screens.
- Marketing headlines, captions, and speech devices must follow `reference/brand-positioning.md`, `reference/avatar-ally.md`, and the evidence rules in the Evolved manual.
- Peach may point to, hold space for, or visually support marketing copy. The copy remains The Evolved's voice unless a separate Peach voice is explicitly approved.
- Do not make Peach the apparent source of a medical, clinical, research, or member-result claim.
- Pair physical outcomes with confidence, capability, longevity, or independence; never use shame, failure, fear, or quick-fix framing.

## Channel Rules

### Social posts and carousels

- Peach may lead educational, reminder, seasonal, celebration, and lightweight engagement posts.
- For member stories, use member photography as the hero visual; Peach may appear on a supporting or CTA slide.
- Use one primary Peach pose per slide unless the concept explicitly needs a sequence.
- Keep carousel pose changes purposeful and visually consistent.

### Stories and short-form video

- Peach may be used as a static overlay, cutaway, transition, pointer, reaction, or end-card figure.
- Until the proper animation system is built, keep movement limited to whole-character position, scale, opacity, and simple entrance/exit effects.
- Do not puppet, bend, or independently animate body parts from a static master.
- New reusable character motion must follow `animation-system.md`.

### Email

- Use Peach as a header accent, section guide, educational illustration, reminder, or CTA-supporting image.
- Keep member proof and offer details dominant.
- Use optimised derivatives rather than embedding the full-resolution master.

### Website and landing pages

- Real members, trainers, the facility, coaching, and evidence remain the principal trust builders.
- Peach may support education, FAQs, onboarding steps, programme explanations, empty states, calls to action, and seasonal campaigns.
- Never serve an image directly from Google Drive. Create and upload an optimised WebP or AVIF deployment derivative.
- Record recurring or strategically important deployments in the local asset record.

### Paid advertising

- Peach may support an ad concept, education hook, offer reminder, or retargeting creative.
- She must not be the sole proof for a results claim.
- Claims, urgency, offer terms, and member evidence require the same review as any other paid creative.

### Print and in-gym materials

- Use a print-ready derivative at the final physical dimensions.
- Preserve transparency or use an intentional approved background.
- Check small text, outlines, contrast, crop, bleed, and final resolution before print submission.

## Accessibility

- Add concise alt text describing Peach's action and the marketing purpose, not every visual detail.
- Do not place essential information only inside the image.
- Maintain readable text contrast against the background and character.
- Do not use rapid flashing, aggressive movement, or animation that distracts from the message.
- Decorative appearances may use empty alt text when the surrounding copy already communicates the full meaning.

## Derivative and File Rules

Never overwrite an approved master.

Create derivatives in the appropriate workspace or campaign folder using:

```text
[ASSET-ID]-[short-description]-[channel]-[dimensions]-v[version].[ext]
```

Examples:

```text
COA005-explaining-instagram-carousel-1080x1350-v1.png
HLT002-holding-apple-email-header-1200w-v1.webp
ACH003-double-arm-victory-website-800w-v1.webp
```

Use:

- PNG for editable transparency where needed
- WebP or AVIF for websites and most lightweight digital delivery
- An appropriate high-resolution print format for physical production

Campaign backgrounds, copy, crops, animation, effects, and layout belong in derivatives, not in the approved master.

## Marketing Workflow

For every creative that may use Peach:

1. Define the objective, audience, content pillar, and channel.
2. Decide whether the message requires real photography, Peach, or both.
3. Search the master spreadsheet or local approved folder by category and pose.
4. Confirm the selected file is an approved master.
5. Create a channel-specific derivative; never edit the master.
6. Apply the brand voice, evidence, CTA, composition, and accessibility rules.
7. Verify that Peach supports the message without replacing real proof.
8. Publish through the normal channel workflow.
9. Record strategically important recurring deployments or newly created reusable derivatives.

## Confirmed Owner Decisions

Peter Brown confirmed these rules on 2026-08-04:

1. **Prominence:** Peach is a supporting brand character, not the primary logo or default hero image.
2. **Voice:** Peach expresses the existing Evolved voice. She does not currently have a separate first-person voice or catchphrase.
3. **Frequency:** Use Peach selectively so she adds recognition without flooding the marketing.
4. **Member imagery:** Peach may appear subtly alongside real members but never replaces, competes with, or obscures them.
5. **Paid campaigns:** Peach may support paid advertising provided real evidence remains the proof.
6. **Animation:** A proper reusable animation system is an approved direction. Until it is built, only simple whole-character motion is permitted. The system specification is `animation-system.md`.
7. **Logo relationship:** Peach remains separate from the official Evolved logo and must not be turned into a logo lock-up without a future explicit brand decision.
