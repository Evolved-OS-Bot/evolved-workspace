## Content Pillars

Four recurring themes that all content should map to:

### Pillar 1 — Empowerment Through Strength
**Focus:** Strength training builds more than physical power — it builds confidence and independence.
- Success stories (deadlift milestones, weight loss, improved confidence)
- Educational posts: benefits of strength training for women
- Inspirational quotes and imagery

### Pillar 2 — Education & Expertise
**Focus:** Establish authority as the go-to gym for women's fitness and health.
- Nutrition tips (protein-packed meals, portion guides)
- Strength training tips and form corrections
- Myth-busting posts ("Will lifting weights make me bulky?")
- Condition-specific posts (perimenopause, PCOS, bone health)

### Pillar 3 — Community & Connection
**Focus:** Showcase the supportive, all-female environment.
- Client spotlights and testimonials
- Behind-the-scenes (group classes, events, Max Out Party)
- Milestone celebrations (first chin-up, weight loss achievements)
- "Meet the Trainer" features

### Pillar 4 — Action & Results
**Focus:** Drive conversions through real results and program promotion.
- Before-and-after transformations
- Promotional posts for challenges ("6-Week Challenge Starting Soon!")
- CTAs for trial memberships
- Posts addressing objections (time, cost, intimidation)

## Instagram Post Framework

Every post should include: **Hook → Story/Value → Visual Appeal → CTA**

### Post Types

**1. Client Success Stories**
- Hook: "Meet [Name] — She Lost 14kg and Gained Confidence in Just 6 Months!"
- Story: Starting point, challenges, how The Evolved helped, emotional wins
- Visual: Before/after, workout shots, milestone stats
- CTA: "Inspired by [Name]'s story? DM us 'READY' to start your own journey!"

**2. Educational Posts**
- Hook: "Why Women Over 40 Should Lift Weights"
- Story: 3–5 key points on a topic (carousel or infographic)
- Visual: Bold graphics with clear headers per point
- CTA: "Save this post!" / "Comment below if you have questions!"

**3. Community Highlights**
- Hook: "Our All-Female Community is Like No Other"
- Story: Behind-the-scenes, member quotes, group moments
- Visual: Smiling group shots, action photos, trainer team photos
- CTA: "Tag your workout buddy!" / "DM us 'TOGETHER' to join our community!"

**4. Actionable Tips**
- Hook: "3 Tips for Managing Perimenopause with Strength Training"
- Story: Immediate, actionable advice (one tip per carousel slide)
- Visual: Carousel ending with summary/CTA slide
- CTA: "Share this with a friend who needs this!" / "DM us for a tailored program!"

**5. Promotional Posts**
- Hook: Bold offer headline ("28 Days for $1!")
- Story: What's included + urgency ("Only 5 spots left!")
- Visual: Eye-catching graphics with bold fonts
- CTA: "DM us 'YES' to claim your spot before it's gone!"

## Success Story Template

For emails, social, and website testimonials.

1. **Headline** — Results-driven, specific: *"Lost 15kg in 6 Months: Meet Jane's Transformation"*
2. **Introduction** — Relatable hook: starting point, key challenge, who she is
3. **Goals & Challenges** — Her specific goal and what was in the way
4. **The Evolved Journey** — Program used, sessions attended, nutrition support, milestones
5. **Results** — Measurable outcomes + emotional wins
   > *"In just 6 months, Jane lost 15kg, built the strength to squat 70kg, and — most importantly — found her confidence again."*
6. **Closing Message** — What's possible when she commits: *"Jane's story is proof that with the right support, anyone can achieve incredible results. Your journey could be next."*
7. **CTA** — *"Inspired by Jane's story? Reply 'EVOLVE' to see how we can help you achieve your goals!"*

**Key language principle:** Always pair the physical result with the emotional/life outcome. The body result is proof; the life change is the story she wants for herself.

## Marketing Campaign Workflow (7 Steps)

1. **Goal** — Define the objective (membership sign-ups, challenge promotion, brand awareness). Set success metrics.
2. **Audience** — Reference Ally's avatar. What pain point does this solve? What result will she achieve?
3. **Plan** — Core message, which content pillars to activate, deliverables (email sequence, social posts, landing page, ads)
4. **Create** — Email (3–5 sequence: intro → objections → success story → offer reminder), Instagram posts, landing page
5. **Distribute** — Email at peak times (mornings or early evenings), schedule social via Buffer/Later, stories daily during campaign, Facebook Ads targeting women in West End Brisbane
6. **Engage** — Respond promptly to DMs, comments, email inquiries. Use objection handling scripts.
7. **Analyze** — Track email open/click rates, social engagement/reach, landing page conversion. Document what worked.

**Internal CTA check:** *"Will this campaign speak directly to Ally? Does it solve her problem, inspire her goals, and guide her to action?"*

## Sales Objections Quick Reference

| Objection | Core Concern | Key Response Direction |
|---|---|---|
| "I don't have time." | Schedule overwhelm | Programs fit your life; small-group at convenient times; you deserve this time |
| "I'm not ready to commit." | Fear of starting | You don't have to be ready — we meet you where you are |
| "I've tried and failed." | Past discouragement | Not doing it alone this time; sustainable habits, not quick fix |
| "I'm intimidated by gyms." | Judgment / out of place | All-female, no mirrors, no judgment — safe and comfortable |
| "I can't afford it." | Value uncertainty | Complete program not just a membership; saves money vs quick fixes; flexible options |
| "I'm worried about injuries." | Safety | Expert trainers work with injuries and health concerns; tailored to your body |
| "I don't know if strength training is for me." | Relevance | Builds confidence, metabolism, everyday strength — fun, safe, tailored to goals |
| "Isn't it better to start later?" | Timing hesitation | No perfect time; starting now means momentum heading into any new period |
| "I'm not fit enough to join." | Self-doubt | Programs meet you where you are; beginner-friendly; step-by-step guidance |
