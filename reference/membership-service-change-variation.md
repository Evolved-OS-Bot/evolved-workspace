## Slide 3: Evolved Anywhere Variation

### Your New Service

Evolved Anywhere costs A$69 per week. It includes personalised Trainerize programming, normal program modifications and one 30-minute personal training session every four weeks.

Your included session may be delivered in person or virtually by mutual agreement. Evolved Anywhere does not include routine group training or general facility access.

### Effective Date and Current Access

Your service change takes effect after 30 paid days. Your existing service, access and billing continue until the effective date confirmed by The Evolved.

### Monthly Personal Training Session

Your session must be used within its four-week service period and does not roll over. It cannot be transferred, exchanged for cash or credited against another service.

You may reschedule with at least 24 hours notice. A cancellation or reschedule with less than 24 hours notice, or a no-show, forfeits the session.

If The Evolved cancels your session and cannot provide a replacement trainer, the session will be rescheduled and will not be forfeited.

### Programming and Support

Your coach will maintain your personalised program in Trainerize and provide normal coaching support. Coaching support does not include unlimited or immediate access to a coach.

### Holds, Cancellation and Later Changes

The Evolved's standard membership hold and cancellation policies apply after this variation takes effect. Any later service change requires a new signed variation.

### Continuing Agreement

This variation changes only the service, price, access and inclusions stated above. All unchanged terms of your original membership agreement continue to apply.

## Strong 12-Month Commitment Variation

**Agreement version:** `strong-12-month-commitment-variation-v1`

### Your price and term

You are keeping your Strong, Fit & Flexible membership and its existing
services. Your four-week upfront payment does not change.

Your regular weekly membership price will reduce from A$99 to A$89 for 12
calendar months. The term starts when your first successful A$89 regular
weekly payment is collected. If your weekly billing has already started, the
discount begins on your next normal weekly payment. The discount is not
backdated and does not create a refund.

The maximum discount over the term is A$520.

### What happens at the end

Your membership is an ongoing agreement. At the first normal weekly billing
date on or after the 12-month anniversary, your weekly price automatically
returns to your originally agreed A$99. The A$89 price does not automatically
renew. Another discounted term requires a new signed variation.

The Evolved will send you a written reminder at least two months before the
initial term ends, stating the end date, that the membership continues at
A$99 per week and how to end the agreement.

Initials field:

> I understand that this is an ongoing membership, the A$89 term ends after 12
> calendar months, and my original A$99 weekly price then resumes.

### Holds, early cancellation and service changes

A hold does not extend the 12-month term.

If you cancel early or downgrade from Strong, The Evolved may recover only the
discount you actually received: A$10 for each successful A$89 weekly payment,
less any refunded discounted payments, up to a maximum of A$520. We will give
you the calculation before any amount is collected.

There is no discount recovery if you upgrade to Fast Track. There is also no
discount recovery where The Evolved cannot supply the service, where your
rights under the Australian Consumer Law apply, or where The Evolved approves
documented medical or severe hardship.

Initials field:

> I understand when discount recovery may apply, how it is calculated, the
> A$520 maximum, and the circumstances where it does not apply.

### Cooling-off and continuing agreement

You may cancel this variation in writing within 48 hours after signing it.
If you do, The Evolved will restore the original A$99 weekly price and will
not charge a variation administration fee. Any refund required by law will be
processed within the required period.

This variation changes only the weekly price and the commitments stated
above. All unchanged terms of your original membership agreement continue to
apply, including your existing cancellation interaction and notice process.
Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law cannot be excluded.

> I understand that replying COMMIT did not change my membership and that the
> change is complete only after The Evolved confirms it.
