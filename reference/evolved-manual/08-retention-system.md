## First-Week Member Reply Follow-Up

The live `Membership: First 7 Days` workflow checks for member replies on Day 7, Day 8 and Day 9. This is a role-restricted process for the designated Retention Manager, with Admin Eve handling the written response and review-workflow state.

For a positive reply, the Retention Manager calls the member promptly to thank her, build rapport and check whether she has left a review. When it feels natural, the Retention Manager can mention that a new-member spot has opened and ask whether a friend or woman in her life may benefit from getting stronger too.

The Retention Manager asks for a warm introduction or confirms that the referred woman has agreed to be contacted before recording or using her details. Once consent or the introduction is in place, the Retention Manager records the referral and gets in touch promptly.

For a negative reply, the Retention Manager calls the member as soon as possible. The call, concern, support discussed, outcome, next action and any owner escalation must be recorded as an internal note on the member's GHL contact record.

The task is complete only after the required follow-up, internal note and any escalation are recorded in GHL. Full procedure: `reference/sops/first-week-member-reply-follow-up.md`.
