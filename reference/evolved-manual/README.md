## Purpose

This manual defines the complete coaching, assessment, programming, retention, and delivery framework used by The Evolved All Female Gym.

It is structured for both human readability and AI parseability — designed to serve as the source of truth for trainers, the trainer onboarding portal, AI assistants, and future certification products.

**Core Outcome:** Help women become stronger, healthier, and more independent at every stage of life.

## AI Instruction Block

When referencing this manual:

1. Follow coaching standards before personal preference.
2. Follow assessment outcomes before assumptions.
3. Use Evolved terminology — PRIME, Live/Long/Perform, Key Performance Habits, Nexus Point.
4. Prioritise safety over progression.
5. Prioritise consistency over intensity.
6. Prioritise long-term independence over short-term aesthetics.
7. When writing copy or coaching content, ground it in the strength science in Section 01.
8. When writing member-facing content, use the tone and language from `reference/brand-positioning.md` and `reference/avatar-ally.md`.
