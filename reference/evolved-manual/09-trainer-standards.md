## Purpose

Define the professional, cultural, and delivery standards every trainer at The Evolved must uphold. These are not guidelines — they are the baseline. Coaches who consistently fall below these standards are failing their members.

---

## Learning Outcomes

After reading this section coaches should be able to:
- Articulate The Evolved's non-negotiable delivery standards
- Apply correct conduct in client, team, and public-facing situations
- Understand their employment obligations and accountability expectations
- Follow the correct escalation and reporting procedures

---

## Delivery Standards

### Appearance & Presentation

- Arrive on time, appropriately dressed, and prepared for all scheduled sessions
- Attire must be clean, professional, and appropriate for a training environment

### Punctuality

- Arrive **15 minutes early** for all sessions — equipment set up, space prepared, member details reviewed
- Notify management of any schedule changes or cancellations at least **24 hours in advance**
  - Phone Peter: **0420 863 721**
  - Phone Megan: **0402 840 247**

### Client Interaction

- Treat all members, staff, and team with respect and courtesy
- Respect members' personal space, boundaries, and fitness goals
- Obtain explicit consent before providing physical adjustments
- Ask — then highlight what they are already doing well — then deliver the cue or correction

### Phone Policy

- Personal phones are **not permitted during class**
- **Exception:** Filming Instagram story content for @the_evolved_allfemalegym — post after class, not during
- **Exception:** Life-threatening emergency
- All other personal phone use during class is prohibited

### Session Delivery

- Do not sit down during a session
- Do not perform an unrelated exercise during a session
- Remain on your feet at all times while delivering a workout
- Your back must never be turned to a member — maintain full visual on all moving clients at all times
- Provide at least **one touch point per client per working set** — cue, adjustment, or acknowledgement — minimum 8 times per workout

---

## Programming Obligations

- The SGPT program must be delivered as structured — no major changes to sets, reps, or movement patterns without management approval
- Trainers may progress or regress movements based on individual ability (strength, fitness, injury)
- When unsure of a progression or regression: deliver what is prescribed, apologise, and ask management as soon as possible — provide client name and the movement pattern requiring change
- 1:1 clients must always have **12 weeks of structured training** outlined in Trainerize — random compliance checks will be conducted

## Safety Obligations

- Inspect all equipment before each session for damage or wear — report any faults to management immediately
- Confirm walkways and workout areas are clear of obstructions before every session
- Know the location of first aid kits and AEDs — confirm they are accessible and stocked
- Familiarise yourself with emergency procedures, including evacuation plans
- Report all injuries, incidents, or unsafe practices to management promptly
- Ensure a first aid kit is always accessible during training

---

## Cultural Standards

### Inclusivity

- Promote an environment where women feel empowered, supported, and motivated
- Celebrate members' progress and milestones — foster a positive community culture
- Never engage in judgment, gossip, or comparison involving members

### Community

- Your job is to create a safe, exciting training environment
- Do not field member complaints — redirect all complaints to **info@theevolvedgym.com.au**
- Never discuss member complaints with other members or staff outside management

### Collaboration

- Work effectively with the team to provide seamless services
- Share constructive feedback through appropriate channels — directly to management, not laterally

---

## Confidentiality

- Maintain strict confidentiality regarding member health and personal information at all times
- Personal information must not be discussed outside the team and only for the purpose of delivering training

---

## Accountability & Conduct

### Standards of Conduct

- Foster an inclusive and supportive environment, free from discrimination, harassment, or bullying
- Abide by all operational policies, safety standards, and professional codes in this document

### Professional Development

- Stay updated with industry standards
- Attend training or workshops as required by management

### Reporting Misconduct

- Address unprofessional conduct through appropriate channels
- Escalate serious matters to management immediately

### Disciplinary Process

- Violations of these standards may result in disciplinary action, including warnings, suspension, or termination
- Employees will have the opportunity to respond to any allegations before disciplinary measures are enacted

---

## Post-Session Responsibilities

**Member farewell:**
- Acknowledge what a great job everyone did
- Congratulate 2–3 members individually — identify 1 win or 1 improvement
- Ask members when they're coming in next

**Clean-up:**
- Wipe down and sanitise all equipment used
- Return all items to designated storage areas
- Check for forgotten personal items — store in lost and found
- Wash cool towels in bacterial wash, roll and place back in the fridge

**Environment reset:**
- Restore the workout space to its original condition for the next session
- Inspect for damage or hazards — report to management if necessary
- Ensure the toilet is closed after the session

**Record-keeping:**
- Record attendance in Trainerize
- Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with any issues encountered during the session

---

## Prerequisite: Session Delivery Qualification

All trainers must complete **36 workouts recorded in their own Trainerize account** before being permitted to deliver Sculpt & Strength or Build & Balance sessions. Sculpt & Strength and Build & Balance sessions both count toward this total.

The purpose is to experience The Evolved product as a client, understand the member journey from the inside, and become a genuine raving fan of what we offer. The reflection therefore focuses on the trainer's growth as a woman, not her growth as a trainer.
