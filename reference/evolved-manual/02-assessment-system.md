## Purpose

Assessments provide direction, data, motivation, and retention. Every member enters The Evolved through a structured assessment process. There are no exceptions.

The assessment is not just a sales tool — it is the diagnostic foundation for everything that follows. A well-run assessment eliminates guesswork, creates genuine commitment, and sets up months of meaningful coaching.

---

## Learning Outcomes

After reading this section coaches should be able to:
- Deliver the full 3-part Strength Assessment
- Deliver all 4 Intro Sessions to standard
- Apply the correct exercise prescription based on assessment outcomes
- Use assessment data to drive programming decisions and member conversations

---

## Assessment Framework

```
Diagnosis → Direction → Data → Retention
```

- **Diagnosis:** Understand where the member is starting from
- **Direction:** Prescribe the right program based on what is found
- **Data:** Record and track results so progress is measurable
- **Retention:** Members who understand their starting point stay longer

## The Strength Assessment (New Members)

> Verbatim scripts: `scripts/assessments/strength-assessment-script.md`

The Strength Assessment is the entry point into The Evolved for all new members. It has three parts and runs approximately 60–90 minutes.

### Part 1 — Initial Consultation

**Before the assessment:**
- Review the shared pre-qualification summary and the complete conversation history in Lead Connector: 5–10 minutes minimum
- Pre-qualification is a shared team process. The owner, admin or a coach may have contributed, so the conversation itself is the source of truth
- An injury branch closes once the information is trainer-actionable and no unresolved safety issue requires another pre-assessment question. Do not restart the injury interview simply because more questions could be asked
- Treat observations from a trusted allied-health or manual-therapy provider as useful context, not as a diagnosis independently confirmed by The Evolved
- The trainer purchases the `Initial Consultation` Trainerize product for the prospect, then sets up that Trainerize account on the Sales iPad before they arrive
- Be outside to greet them — at the top of the stairs or ramp, ready to call/text if needed

**Purpose of the consultation:**
The consultation is an information-gathering exercise, not a coaching session. Do not offer advice, make recommendations, or get into extended discussions. Listen and feel for the emotion behind what they want.

**The 9 consultation questions:**

| # | Question | What you're listening for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is your main fitness goal? | The gap to be closed |
| 2 | Why are these goals important to you? | Emotional depth — the real driver |
| 3 | When would you like to achieve your goal by? | Urgency, realism, commitment level |
| 4 | What have you tried in the past? | What didn't work and why |
| 5 | What do you do for work? | Capacity to invest time and money |
| 6 | Lifestyle questions (smoking, alcohol) | Competing habits |
| 7 | What do you need from us to succeed? | Their version of support |
| 8 | Any existing injuries or conditions? | Safety and programming constraints |
| 9 | Any roadblocks in the next 6 months? | Pre-empt and handle objections now |

**Key principles:**
- Reference what you already know — show you've done your homework
- Preserve the prospect's own language, especially aspirational goals, injury context and attempted returns to training
- Build on the pre-qualification handoff, confirm only what safety requires and make the assessment focus visible to the prospect
- Sit in silence after each question; women often need space to fully open up
- Question 2 (the why) is the most important — go deep, let them run
- Questions 3 and 9 are where you handle objections before the close

**Transition to physical assessment:**
> *"Excellent — thank you for taking the time to go through that with me. Let's move on to the physical assessment. Are you ready?"*

---

### Part 2 — Physical Assessment

**Why the physical assessment matters:**

Most fitness sales processes never leave the chair. A prospect can nod along for 45 minutes and still feel uncertain. The physical assessment changes that — it holds up a mirror and shows them, in real time, exactly where they are. This is what turns a hesitant prospect into a committed member.

**Before beginning:**
Prepare the **Strength Assessment Card** — this card contains the three exercises, the three strength levels (Live / Long / Perform), and a longevity timeline. Record results in shorthand on both the digital form and the physical card. The card goes home with them.

The PAR-Q must be complete and reviewed before physical testing begins. Any Yes answer must be clarified; current, uncontrolled, recent, unexplained, materially changed, or unclear issues require written medical or allied-health clearance.

Modify or omit a test when pain, apprehension, restriction, poor control, or a managed condition makes the standard test unsuitable. Stop immediately for chest pain or pressure, fainting or near-fainting, severe unusual breathlessness, sudden neurological symptoms, acute or rapidly increasing pain, loss of function, or any new unsafe symptom.

Trainerize is the in-session source of truth for results. After the session, the coach submits **SA: Coach Consultation Feedback** so GHL receives the assessment and outcome data needed for marketing and remarketing until the two systems are integrated.

**Closing the appointment record:**

- If the assessment was delivered, set the GHL appointment to **Showed** and submit **SA: Coach Consultation Feedback** before finishing the shift.
- In the feedback form, select the person who actually delivered the assessment. If an approved cover coach delivered it and is not listed, select **Approved cover / other** so Admin Eve can resolve the exception.
- If the prospect did not attend, set the appointment to **No show** immediately. Missing feedback is not proof of a no-show.
- If the prospect cancelled, leave the appointment as **Cancelled**. Use **Invalid** only for an approved duplicate, test or bad booking.
- An appointment left Confirmed more than 60 minutes after its end becomes an Admin Eve exception. The coach still owns the first correction.

**The Three Tests:**

#### Test 1 — Front Foot Elevated Split Squat

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **What it reveals** | Ankle mobility, knee strength, hip mobility, overall leg function |
| **Why it matters** | Translates to glute strength, running, carrying children, returning from injury, and reducing chronic pain in the back, knees, and hips |
| **Live standard** | Heel flat and grounded, knee tracking over toe, hamstring and calf in full contact at the bottom, 10 clean reps each side |
| **How to run** | Start with a higher box, progress downward until form breaks down. Record height achieved and rep count |

#### Test 2 — Farmer's Carry (Walk of Independence)

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **What it reveals** | Grip strength and full-body carrying capacity |
| **Why it matters** | Grip strength is one of the strongest predictors of independence in the final decade of life |
| **Live standard** | 75% of bodyweight for 60 seconds walking. Example: 60kg client = 45kg total = 22.5kg per hand |
| **How to run** | Tell them the target upfront. Start at 75% — if they hesitate, try 50%. Stop the set the moment form breaks down |

**Coaching note for younger clients (<30):** Connect grip strength to pulling movements, glute development, and the lifts that produce the aesthetics they're after.

#### Test 3 — Full Plank

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **What it reveals** | Core stability and foundational strength |
| **Why it matters** | Clients who can hold a full plank for 2 minutes are significantly less likely to suffer chronic back pain |
| **Live standard** | Hands and toes (not forearms), shoulders protracted, hips in line — no sagging or tenting, 2 minutes continuous |
| **How to run** | Demonstrate the standard. Call the set the moment form breaks down — no exceptions |

**Presenting the results:**

Once all three tests are complete, bring their attention to the longevity scale on the card.

If they fell short of Live on any test:
> *"[Name], right now your results show me that you don't yet have the strength needed to live pain-free. That explains why you're [tired / have back pain / can't shake that old injury]. The fix is 3–6 months working with us. We regularly see members go from this level to Live — and close to Long — in that timeframe. If you stuck around for 12 months, you'd be well into Long and getting close to Perform. How does that feel? ... Do you want help closing that gap?"*

Wait in silence. Let them answer. When they say yes — move into the close.

### Part 3 — The Close

**Present the packages:**

> *"This is our most popular membership. It's designed for the woman who wants support, structure, and a community, all focused on building real strength. It includes everything you need to get started: one KickStart onboarding session, a custom meal plan, and access to our full group training program."*

Then introduce Fast Track for clients who mention injuries, medical conditions, or wanting faster progress.

**The close question (say this, and nothing else):**
> *"Is there anything on the screen that jumps out as something you can do — something you'd like to do?"*

Then go silent. Wait for them to speak first.

**On sign-up:**
1. Open the membership agreement form
2. Enter contact details, select package, double-check upfront + weekly combination
3. Walk them through T&Cs; have them sign
4. Complete payment via checkout redirect
5. Before the member leaves, complete `reference/sops/post-sale-member-onboarding.md`

## Intro Sessions (Exercise Prescription)

Intro Sessions establish the member's exercise levels across the three primary movement patterns: **Squat, Deadlift, and Bench Press.**

**Session allocation:**
- **Fit & Flexible members:** no Intro Session
- **Strong, Fit & Flexible Membership members:** 1 Intro Session
- **New Fast Track members:** 4 Intro Sessions
- **Existing-member Fast Track conversions:** the assessment-prescribed 13-session specialised series, not Intro Sessions

Use the end of Session 1 to upsell 1:1 PT — explain that additional sessions allow you to continue the assessment and progression process together.

---

### Session 1 — Movement Assessment

**Purpose:** Assign the appropriate exercise for each movement pattern.

**Approach:** Move quickly. Avoid failure — work to a 7 RPE. The goal is to identify where the client demonstrates control at a working load, not to push limits.

**Preparation:**
- Send a friendly intro text when the calendar invite is received
- Send a reminder text the morning of the session
- Arrive 15 minutes early, review client details

**Greeting and app setup:**
- Welcome the member, explain the purpose of Intro Sessions
- Help them download Trainerize, book 2–3 classes, walk through key features
- Take photos and measurements (ask permission, explain it's the best progress-tracking method)
- Create 1–3 main goals in Trainerize
- Assign 1–2 Level 1 Habits aligned to their main goal

**Movement Assessment — Progression Rules:**
- Apply PRIME to every rep: Posture, Range, Identify, Measure, then Evaluate & Evolve
- Advance only when the client completes 10 pain-free, repeatable reps to PRIME standard and the exercise remains below 7 RPE
- Stop at and assign the highest exercise the client can perform proficiently at about 7 RPE
- If posture, range, control, comfort or safety breaks down, stop the progression and assign the previous proficient exercise
- Never skip a stage or progress solely because 10 reps were completed
- Before progressing beyond Barbell Romanian Deadlift, the client must complete 12 reps at 35 kg total barbell load. Every rep must include a controlled one-second hold with the bar just below mid-knee while the client maintains a PRIME postural position
- Record all reps and load; leave exercises not completed blank
- Rest: 30 seconds between sets
- Teach breathing and bracing in all exercises — begin lying, transfer to standing and loaded

#### Squat Pattern

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Glute Bridge | 1 × 10 | No load. Move to next if form maintained |
| Bodyweight Box Squat | 1 × 10 | Adjust box height with plates — find point of best control. No load if form maintained at nexus point |
| Bodyweight Squat | 1 × 10 | No load if form maintained at nexus point |
| Kettlebell Goblet Squat | 1 × 10 | Record reps and weight. If 7 RPE not achieved above 10kg, move to Nexus Point Squat |
| Nexus Point Squat | 1 × 10 | Record only if upright torso maintained at nexus point |

#### Hinge Pattern

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Broomstick Hinge | 1 × 10 | Establish the hinge pattern. Advance only when all reps meet PRIME and remain below 7 RPE |
| Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift | 1 × 10 | Record reps and load. Advance only when all reps meet PRIME and remain below 7 RPE |
| Barbell Romanian Deadlift | 1 × 12 at 35 kg | Hold one second just below mid-knee on every rep. Progress only when all 12 reps maintain a PRIME postural position |
| Trap Bar Deadlift | 1 × 10 | Record reps and load. Advance only when all reps meet PRIME and remain below 7 RPE |
| Barbell Deadlift | 1 × 10 | Record reps and load. Assign only when all reps meet PRIME at about 7 RPE |

#### Bench Pattern

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Dumbbell Bench Press | 1 × 10 | Record reps and weight. If scapula retraction + arch + active hips maintained above 15kg total, move to Barbell |
| Barbell Bench Press | 1 × 10 | Record only if scapula retraction + arch + active hips maintained throughout |

**Post-session:**
- Upsell 1:1 PT — explain that additional sessions continue this assessment and progression process
- Send a post-session text with encouragement — highlight something they did well

**If not continuing PT:**
- Let them know you'll check in monthly — follow the monthly check-in process
- Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with client status
- Send a warm final encouragement text

---

### Session 2 — First Working Sets

**Purpose:** Complete the 3 exercises assigned in Session 1 — 1 working set each, 3 total. Focus on progressing posture and range of motion; teach the member how to measure and track reps and load.

**RM Education:**
Teach the client what rep range and RM means:
> *"10–12RM means pick a weight you can lift for 10, 11, or 12 reps — but not 13. You should reach rep 12 and not be able to complete rep 13 with good form. If you can, add 1–2kg. If you can only lift 6 reps, remove weight until you're within the rep range."*

**Progressive overload logic:**
- Complete 12 reps across all working sets → increase weight by 1–2kg next week
- Complete 10 or 11 reps → aim for one more rep at the same weight next week

**Session structure:**

| Pattern | Progression Options | Sets × Reps | RPE |
|---|---|---|---|
| Squat | Glute Bridge / BW Box Squat / BW Squat / KB Goblet Squat / Nexus Point Squat | 1 × 10–12RM | 7–8 |
| Hinge | Broomstick Hinge / Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift / Barbell Romanian Deadlift / Trap Bar Deadlift / Barbell Deadlift | 1 × 10–12RM | 7–8 |
| Bench | DB Bench Press / Barbell Bench Press | 1 × 10–12RM | 7–8 |

Rest: 2 minutes between sets. Use Performance Warm Up and Acclimation Sets in Trainerize. Record all reps and load.

Retain the Session 1 hinge assignment unless the member can demonstrate the next exercise to PRIME standard. If reassessing, use the same sequence and stop rule from Session 1.

The 35 kg Barbell Romanian Deadlift gate remains mandatory in every reassessment: 12 reps with a one-second hold just below mid-knee on every rep in a PRIME postural position.

---

### Session 3 — Progressing Volume

**Purpose:** 2 working sets per exercise (6 total). Reassess — can the client progress to the next exercise in any pattern?

**Session structure:**

| Pattern | Progression Options | Sets × Reps | RPE |
|---|---|---|---|
| Squat | Glute Bridge / BW Box Squat / BW Squat / KB Goblet Squat / Nexus Point Squat | 2 × 10–12RM | 7–8 |
| Hinge | Broomstick Hinge / Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift / Barbell Romanian Deadlift / Trap Bar Deadlift / Barbell Deadlift | 2 × 10–12RM | 7–8 |
| Bench | DB Bench Press / Barbell Bench Press | 2 × 10–12RM | 7–8 |

Reinforce RM education and progressive overload logic from Session 2. Rest: 2 minutes between sets.

Only progress an assigned hinge exercise when the member demonstrates the next exercise to PRIME standard. Otherwise consolidate the current assignment.

**Post-session:** Ask if the member is interested in continuing 1:1 PT after intros.

---

### Session 4 — Consolidation

**Purpose:** Retain the two-set structure from Session 3. Consolidate the highest proficient exercise assignment in each pattern and close for ongoing PT.

The Barbell Romanian Deadlift cannot be progressed until the member passes the 35 kg, 12-rep, one-second-hold PRIME gate.

**Opening check-in:**
- *"Imagine you've been consistent with 3–4 sessions per week for 6 months — how would you feel?"*
- *"What benefits do you think you'll experience?"*

**Post-session:**
- Let them know you'll check in monthly — follow the monthly check-in process
- Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with client status
- Send a final post-session text with encouragement

## Decision Trees

### Squat Pattern Prescription

```
IF Goblet Squat < 7 RPE at 10kg
THEN progress to Nexus Point Squat
```

### Hinge Pattern Prescription

```
START Broomstick Hinge
THEN Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift
THEN Barbell Romanian Deadlift
THEN Trap Bar Deadlift
THEN Barbell Deadlift

Advance one stage only after 10 pain-free, repeatable reps meet PRIME and remain below 7 RPE.
The Barbell Romanian Deadlift is a mandatory load-and-position gate: 12 reps at 35 kg total barbell load, with a controlled one-second hold just below mid-knee on every rep while maintaining a PRIME postural position.
Stop at the highest exercise completed proficiently at about 7 RPE.
If PRIME, comfort or safety breaks down, assign the previous proficient exercise.
```

### Bench Pattern Prescription

```
IF DB Bench Press: scapula retraction + arch + active hips maintained at 15kg total
THEN progress to Barbell Bench Press
```
