## Purpose

Map the complete member journey from first contact to long-term retention — so every trainer understands their role at each stage and can move members forward intentionally.

---

## Learning Outcomes

After reading this section coaches should be able to:
- Describe the full member journey from inquiry to retention
- Identify which stage each member is in
- Know the correct action to take at each stage
- Plant the seed for upgrades at the right moments

---

## The Member Journey Map

```
Inquiry
→ Strength Assessment (Part 1–3)
→ Membership Start
→ Post-Sale Onboarding
→ Intro Sessions (0, 1, or 4 depending on membership)
→ Regular Timetable (SGPT)
→ Monthly Check-Ins
→ PT Upgrade Conversation
→ Fast Track / 1:1 PT
→ Long-Term Retention
```

## Stage 3 — Post-Sale Onboarding

The assigned consultant completes the member setup during the same post-sale consultation, before the member leaves. The consultant must reuse the existing Trainerize assessment account, provision the correct membership and timetable access, complete package inclusions and book the first required coaching appointment.

The GHL task due the next day is a fallback control, not a handoff to Admin. Admin Eve performs an independent quality-control check by day two.

Full procedure: `reference/sops/post-sale-member-onboarding.md`.

## Stage 4: Intro Sessions

### Membership Allocation
- Fit & Flexible members: no Intro Session
- Strong, Fit & Flexible Membership members: 1 Intro Session
- Fast Track members: 4 Intro Sessions

### Session 1 Baseline

Session 1 establishes the member's starting point in Trainerize. The trainer records six circumference measurements in this order: shoulders, right bicep, waist, hips, right quad and right calf.

Measurements are recorded in centimetres to the nearest 0.5 cm. The trainer asks permission before every measurement, respects a no without pressure and uses consistent landmarks and positioning so future measurements are comparable.

With separate permission, the trainer takes front, side and rear progress photos using the phone camera, then uploads them to Trainerize. Photos are never shared without explicit permission for that separate use.

Full measurement, photography and consent procedure: `reference/sops/intro-sessions.md`.

## Stage 6 — Early Follow-Up, Monthly Check-Ins & Retention

The designated Retention Manager owns the personal follow-up when a new member replies to the Day 7, Day 8 or Day 9 check-in in the live `Membership: First 7 Days` workflow.

Admin Eve owns the written reply, contact notes and review-workflow state. For a positive reply, the Retention Manager calls the member promptly to thank her, build rapport, check her review status and offer a natural, consent-based referral invitation.

If the member has someone in mind, the Retention Manager asks for a warm introduction or confirms that the referred woman has agreed to be contacted. Referral details are recorded and used only after that consent is confirmed.

For a negative reply, the Retention Manager calls the member as soon as possible. The Retention Manager records the call, outcome, next action and any owner escalation as an internal note on the member's GHL contact record before completing the task.

Full procedure: `reference/sops/first-week-member-reply-follow-up.md`.

The separate monthly check-in system begins one month after joining and is governed by Section 08.

## Stage 8 — Cancellation & Retention

When a member submits a cancellation form, 8 reason-specific GHL workflows fire in parallel with the core cancellation workflow. Each assigns a retention task to Piper within 10 minutes of form submission.

**The 8 reason workflows:**

| Reason | Primary Re-offer |
|---|---|
| Financial | Financial Relief Form (50% reduced rate, up to 12 weeks) |
| Health/Injury | Complimentary Injury Triage Session + Membership Hold |
| Moving/Travel | Membership Hold (standard or extended) + remote options |
| Schedule/Time | 1:1 PT (flexible) / Hybrid / Online Only |
| Results/Value | PT Reset Block ($90) → free direction session |
| New Gym | PT Reset Block ($90) + re-education on women's strength standards |
| New Style | PT Reset Block ($90) + 1:1 PT hybrid |
| Other | Listen first — match re-offer to real reason once identified |

**Retention call sequence:**
1. Piper calls within 24 hours, twice daily until contact is made
2. Re-offer per reason (see above and `outputs/systems/mc-reason-workflows-build-guide.md`)
3. If all offers declined: book a complimentary farewell session (30-min 1:1 PT) — this is the last opportunity
4. Wave 2: mid-notice check-in SMS at Day 14, owner escalation if no contact after 14 days

**PT Reset Block** ($90, one-time) is the primary re-offer for Results/Value, New Gym, and New Style cancellations. Full product details in `reference/product-offerings.md`.
