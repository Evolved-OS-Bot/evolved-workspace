# Emerging Science Horizon Watchlist

**Owner:** Peter Brown  
**Status:** Canonical research-surveillance register  
**Version:** 1.7  
**Evidence cutoff:** 19 August 2026

## Purpose

Track genuinely promising women's strength, athletic-health and cognitive-performance research before it is mature enough to enter Evolved doctrine or support a public outcome claim.

This register protects Evolved from two opposite errors: ignoring important new work until it becomes old, and treating novelty, registration or mechanism as proof.

## Authority boundary

An entry here is a research signal, not an approved study. It may influence future search priorities, measurements to watch and questions to ask, but it cannot by itself:

- change Evolved doctrine, programming standards or service scope;
- support a public efficacy, prevention, treatment or superiority claim;
- establish an individual supplement, clinical or return-to-play instruction;
- validate a biomarker as a member outcome; or
- justify collection of reproductive, health or wearable data from members.

Peer-reviewed records that have completed claim-use review remain governed by `approved-studies-bank.md`. When a watchlisted trial publishes, the publication receives a new or existing `STU-` record only after identity, methods, outcomes, limitations and transferability are checked.

## Admission classes

| Class | Meaning | Permitted use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `REGISTERED-TRIAL` | Prospectively registered intervention or observational study with a stable registry record | Monitor design, completion and publication |
| `PROTOCOL` | Peer-reviewed or registry protocol without mature outcome evidence | Assess whether it addresses a known evidence gap |
| `EARLY-RESULT` | Peer-reviewed pilot, feasibility or exploratory study | Generate a bounded hypothesis; no doctrine change without reconciliation |
| `MECHANISTIC` | Biomarker, imaging, molecular or physiological signal without direct outcome proof | Explain a possible pathway only when clearly labelled |
| `METHODS-FRONTIER` | New measurement, validation or research-quality development | Improve appraisal and future data design, not outcome claims |

Conference abstracts and preprints may be recorded for discovery but must be labelled explicitly. Neither is eligible for promotion until a stable peer-reviewed publication is verified.

## Promotion gate

A Horizon record is promoted only when all applicable gates pass:

1. A stable peer-reviewed publication is verified against the journal or PubMed record.
2. Registration, protocol, final sample, analysis and reported outcomes are reconciled.
3. Primary outcomes, multiplicity, attrition, adverse events and conflicts or funding are reviewed.
4. Population, intervention, comparator and timeframe transfer to the proposed Evolved use.
5. Biomarkers and intermediate measures are kept distinct from member-important outcomes.
6. The exact approved wording and prohibited overreach are written in the study bank.
7. Conflicting or stronger evidence is included rather than selecting only a favourable new result.
8. Any material doctrine implication passes doctrine regression and owner acceptance.

## Surveillance priorities

The next search refresh should prioritise:

- perimenopause and menopause resistance-training trials that report power, tendon, bone and cognitive outcomes;
- replicated creatine-monohydrate trials in women using clinically meaningful cognitive outcomes;
- female-specific rate-of-force-development, eccentric and deceleration research;
- pregnancy and postpartum return-to-performance cohorts with clear obstetric and pelvic-health boundaries;
- female REDs, iron and bone-stress work using validated clinical definitions;
- cognitive-motor and dual-task outcomes tied to independence rather than biomarker changes alone;
- prospective validation of wearables and autoregulation decisions, not agreement statistics alone; and
- registered-study results that challenge rather than merely confirm current Evolved positions.
- results from HZN-0092–0100 and HZN-0104 that report skeletal-muscle-specific measures, strength, function, bone, nutrition adequacy, adverse events and post-treatment durability rather than bodyweight alone.
- female-specific long-term resistance-training studies comparing repeated cold, heat and passive recovery, because the present adaptation evidence is still predominantly male.
- omega-3 trial publications that test sex/genotype response, the clinical utility of index targets, formulation-specific CNS delivery, clinically meaningful recovery, pregnancy baseline-status targeting and long-term offspring outcomes.
- cross-board supplement trial publications at HZN-0033–0057, with special attention to female directness, formulation comparisons, baseline status, commercial funding, adverse events and whether functional outcomes move with biomarkers.
- new safety and analytical surveillance for botanicals, proprietary blends, contamination, label accuracy, dose stacking and medicine interactions.
- vitamin D trials that separate baseline status, achieved status, dose and clinical outcome rather than treating a blood concentration as proof of benefit.
- strain-, substrate- and formulation-specific probiotic, prebiotic, synbiotic and postbiotic results, including null or adverse findings.
- microbial-metabolite and FMT research that tests muscle, cognition, metabolic response, pain and mood while preserving biological-product, infection, donor and long-term ecosystem risks.

## Review control

- Routine review: quarterly identity-and-status surveillance, with no automatic workspace scheduler.
- Immediate review: results posted, peer-reviewed publication, protocol deviation, retraction or correction, safety signal, or proposed downstream use.
- Manual surveillance is recorded here. Any future automation must use the governed Railway scheduling architecture and requires separate authority.
