# Evolved Mindset and Behaviour Doctrine

**Owner:** Peter Brown  
**Status:** Canonical; completed Mindset and Behaviour doctrine with eleven bounded positions  
**Version:** 2.1  
**As of:** 17 August 2026

This is the canonical authority for accepted Evolved mindset and behaviour principles. Acceptance as an `Evolved position` records how Evolved chooses to coach; it does not convert the source material into scientific proof or guarantee outcomes.

## Protected coaching and mental-health boundary

Standard Evolved coaching may clarify goals, explore what matters to the member, select controllable behaviours, support confidence through practice, review setbacks without shame and help the member re-enter training or habits.

Trainers do not diagnose motivation, personality, trauma, anxiety, depression, an eating disorder or another mental-health condition. They do not provide psychotherapy, crisis counselling or treatment. Concerning distress, risk, disordered eating, coercive relationships or matters outside qualification follow the appropriate qualified or urgent-care route.

## Framework relationship

```text
Understand the present reality
  -> choose the next useful behaviour
  -> make it easier to perform
  -> practise it in ordinary life
  -> observe the response
  -> keep, adjust or progress
  -> build independent skill
```

## DOC-MND-0001 — The Evolved Transformation Loop

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | Mindset and Behaviour |
| Statement | Sustainable coaching proceeds through a repeatable loop: understand the member's current reality; select one safe, useful and manageable behaviour; reduce friction; practise it in ordinary life; review the response; then keep, adjust or progress it. |
| Primary status | Evolved position |
| Owner | Peter Brown |
| Accepted | 14 August 2026 |
| Review due | 14 August 2027 |
| Immediate-review triggers | Evidence of harm or monitoring burden; conflict with a clinical restriction; change to service scope; repeated failure across materially different member contexts. |

### Why Evolved holds this position

- **Source teaching:** Multiple PDF and video units converge on skills, one-change-at-a-time progression, preparation, observation and application during normal life.
- **Evidence position:** Current systematic reviews support autonomy support, specific goals and self-monitoring as potentially useful components, generally with modest or heterogeneous effects. They do not validate this exact loop, prove that one change at a time is universally superior or guarantee durable change.
- **Evolved judgement:** Peter has selected the loop as the standard way to organise Evolved habit coaching because it is practical, autonomy-supportive and compatible with the existing member journey and professional boundaries.

### Coaching application

1. Establish the member's goal, existing capability, constraints and current support.
2. Select one next behaviour using expected usefulness, required effort, safety, readiness and member preference.
3. Make the behaviour specific enough to practise.
4. Reduce avoidable environmental or logistical friction.
5. Practise during ordinary conditions. Stabilisation takes priority during crisis, acute illness, overload or safety concerns.
6. Review what happened without shame or moral judgement.
7. Keep the behaviour, make it easier, change it or progress it.
8. Repeat only when the member has enough capacity for the next step.

### Scope and exclusions

- Applies to general habit coaching, member check-ins and skills development.
- Does not diagnose why a behaviour occurred, require tracking, define one universal “highest-impact” action or guarantee an outcome.
- Clinical symptoms, suspected eating disorders, medication questions and matters outside trainer qualification follow the appropriate referral route.

## DOC-MND-0002 — Knowledge-to-Autonomy Ladder

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | Mindset and Behaviour |
| Statement | Evolved develops capability through four coaching stages: Know, Practise, Apply and Own. Progress means increasing the member's ability to make useful decisions across situations, not increasing dependence on the coach. |
| Primary status | Evolved position |
| Owner | Peter Brown |
| Accepted | 14 August 2026 |
| Review due | 14 August 2027 |
| Immediate-review triggers | The ladder is used as a rigid competence test; access or disability concerns emerge; service design rewards dependence; a stage is represented as a validated psychological diagnosis. |

### Why Evolved holds this position

- **Source teaching:** The source corpus distinguishes knowing information from practising, applying and internalising a skill, and repeatedly favours transferable decision-making over rigid plan dependence.
- **Evidence position:** Autonomy-supportive health interventions have modest average support. The named four-stage ladder remains Evolved's operating synthesis, not a validated universal learning-stage model.
- **Evolved judgement:** Member independence is the intended direction of coaching even when ongoing support remains valuable.

### Coaching application

| Stage | Member capability | Coach's job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Know | Can explain the relevant principle or intended behaviour. | Teach only the information needed for the next action. |
| Practise | Can perform it with prompts, examples or structure. | Provide rehearsal, feedback and appropriate support. |
| Apply | Can adapt it across common situations and disruptions. | Ask questions, test transfer and remove unnecessary prompts. |
| Own | Can make sound decisions independently and seek help when context exceeds her capability. | Reinforce autonomy and remain available without manufacturing dependence. |

Stages are domain-specific. A member may own one skill while only beginning another, and may move back to more support when circumstances change.

### Scope and exclusions

- Applies to coaching skills, education and member-development design.
- Does not determine intelligence, motivation, worth, clinical capacity or membership eligibility.
- Does not mean support should be withdrawn when a member benefits from continued coaching.

## DOC-MND-0003 — Food Environment and Preparation Ladder

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | Mindset and Behaviour |
| Statement | Food preparation is progressed through the smallest level that creates useful reliability: make one choice easier, establish short-range availability, then build a flexible personal system when capacity and circumstances support it. |
| Primary status | Evolved position |
| Owner | Peter Brown |
| Accepted | 14 August 2026 |
| Review due | 14 August 2027 |
| Immediate-review triggers | Eating-disorder or restriction concerns; food insecurity; household conflict; accessibility or disability exclusion; food-safety incident; service promise changes. |

### Why Evolved holds this position

- **Source teaching:** The corpus repeatedly uses availability, visibility, leftovers, shopping rhythms, component preparation and batch cooking to reduce decision and preparation friction.
- **Evidence position:** Choice-architecture reviews support the ability of availability, position and related environmental changes to influence food choice in some real-world settings. Home transfer and durable outcomes remain incomplete; this doctrine accepts a service-design method, not a guaranteed adherence or weight outcome.
- **Evolved judgement:** Preparation should scale to a member's life rather than become another rigid standard she can fail.

### Coaching application

| Level | Aim | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Make one choice easier | Improve one immediate decision. | Put useful ingredients within reach, keep one fallback option or cook one extra serving. |
| 2 — Create short-range reliability | Cover the next several demanding periods. | Prepare components once or twice weekly, use leftovers or frozen items and maintain portable options. |
| 3 — Build a personal system | Make useful meals repeatable with less daily decision load. | Use a flexible shopping, restocking, preparation and fallback rhythm suited to the household. |

The coach starts at the lowest sufficient level and progresses only when the member wants and can use more structure.

### Scope and exclusions

- Must adapt to culture, preferences, allergies, budget, storage, food safety, disability, caring responsibilities and the needs of other household members.
- No compulsory kitchen purge, moral food labels, coercive disposal, surveillance or deprivation.
- Tracking or food photography is optional, consented and stopped if burdensome or harmful.

## DOC-MND-0004 — Member-Owned Direction

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | Mindset and Behaviour |
| Statement | Coaching direction begins with what matters to the member. The coach helps translate that direction into a specific next action without manufacturing urgency, imposing a personal value or using vulnerability as sales pressure. |
| Primary status | Evolved position with partial values and goal-setting evidence |
| Owner | Peter Brown |
| Accepted | 14 August 2026 |
| Review due | 14 August 2027 |
| Immediate-review triggers | Goal work becomes coercive; sensitive information is used in sales; member values conflict with safety or clinical direction; application to minors. |

### Coaching application

1. Ask what the member wants to be different and why it matters to her.
2. Clarify the desired capability or experience without forcing disclosure.
3. Identify the smallest safe action that expresses that direction now.
4. Confirm that the action belongs to the member and fits current capacity.
5. Revisit the direction when circumstances or priorities change.

### Scope and exclusions

- A meaningful reason may help direction, but it does not guarantee adherence.
- The member may decline to disclose private emotional material.
- The coach does not decide which life goal should matter most.

## DOC-MND-0005 — Compassionate Realism and Controllables

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | Mindset and Behaviour |
| Statement | Evolved names reality accurately, including constraints the member cannot simply think away, then directs attention toward the safest useful action she can influence. Compassion and accountability operate together; neither blame nor denial is required. |
| Primary status | Evolved position with supportive self-compassion evidence |
| Owner | Peter Brown |
| Accepted | 14 August 2026 |
| Review due | 14 August 2027 |
| Immediate-review triggers | Structural or clinical barriers are labelled excuses; compassion is used to avoid safety action; a member reports shame or blame. |

### Coaching application

- Separate facts, interpretations, constraints and controllable next actions.
- Acknowledge injury, age, genetics, disability, money, caring, culture, work and health context without minimising them.
- Set accountability around an agreed behaviour, not the member's worth.
- Change the plan when the plan is the problem.

### Scope and exclusions

- “Focus on controllables” must not deny discrimination, trauma, medical reality or resource limits.
- Non-shaming coaching is not psychotherapy or self-compassion treatment.
- Outcomes outside the member's control are not used as moral scores.

## DOC-MND-0006 — Motivation Is Information, Not a Moral Verdict

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | Mindset and Behaviour |
| Statement | Motivation naturally varies. A low-motivation period prompts curiosity about value, capability, friction, fatigue, support and safety; it does not prove laziness, weak character or lack of commitment. |
| Primary status | Evolved position with autonomy-support evidence |
| Owner | Peter Brown |
| Accepted | 14 August 2026 |
| Review due | 14 August 2027 |
| Immediate-review triggers | Trainers diagnose mental state; declining engagement is treated as misconduct; persistent low mood or distress is coached around rather than referred. |

### Coaching application

Ask what changed. Check whether the action still matters, is understood, feels possible and fits present capacity.

Then reduce friction, shrink the action, change the support, reconnect it to the member's direction or pause and refer. Do not demand emotional enthusiasm before useful action can occur.

### Scope and exclusions

- This position does not claim motivation is irrelevant.
- Persistent loss of interest, distress or functional impairment may require qualified assessment.
- Coaches do not use personality labels or diagnose “self-sabotage.”

## DOC-MND-0007 — Confidence Follows Evidence and Practice

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | Mindset and Behaviour |
| Statement | Evolved builds confidence from safe practice, useful feedback, truthful examples and personally meaningful evidence of capability. Encouragement supports the process but does not replace competence or create a result guarantee. |

| Owner | Peter Brown |
| Accepted | 14 August 2026 |
| Review due | 14 August 2027 |
| Immediate-review triggers | Social proof becomes misleading; pressure replaces skill development; assessment results are overstated; fear or trauma requires clinical care. |

### Coaching application

- Start with a safely attainable challenge.
- Give specific feedback about what the member did.
- Record real progress without inflating it.
- Use relatable stories only within their governed factual match.
- Progress challenge as competence and safety permit.

### Scope and exclusions

- Confidence is not a prerequisite for starting.
- One successful session does not prove all future capability.
- Member stories illustrate possibility; they do not establish typical outcomes or treatment efficacy.

## DOC-MND-0008 — Setback Review and Safe Re-entry

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | Mindset and Behaviour |
| Statement | A setback is reviewed as information: establish what changed, protect safety, reduce the next demand if needed, restore one useful action and rebuild from current capacity. Re-entry matters more than punishment or compensation. |
| Primary status | Evolved position with supportive behaviour and self-compassion evidence |
| Owner | Peter Brown |
| Accepted | 14 August 2026 |
| Review due | 14 August 2027 |
| Immediate-review triggers | Re-entry pressure conflicts with a hold or clinical restriction; exercise is used to compensate for eating; relapse or distress requires specialist care. |

### Coaching application

1. Establish the interruption and its safety implications.
2. Identify what remains possible and wanted.
3. Remove unnecessary difficulty.
4. Select one useful re-entry action.
5. Review before restoring the previous demand.

### Scope and exclusions

- Re-entry is not automatic when clinical clearance or recovery is required.
- Missed training or changed eating is not punished with extra exercise or restriction.
- Crisis, acute illness and safety take priority over habit progression.

## DOC-MND-0009 — Coaching Support Is Not Psychological Treatment

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | Mindset and Behaviour |
| Statement | Evolved trainers support goals, confidence, habits and ordinary emotional context only within fitness-coaching competence. Diagnosis, psychotherapy, crisis response and treatment of a mental-health or eating disorder belong to appropriately qualified care. |
| Primary status | Professional-scope and safety position |
| Owner | Peter Brown |
| Accepted | 14 August 2026 |
| Review due | 14 August 2027 |
| Immediate-review triggers | New mental-health service or qualification; incident or disclosure; referral-network change; trainer role change. |

### Coaching application

Listen without diagnosing. Acknowledge what the member shared, establish immediate safety when relevant, stay within the trainer's role and use the approved escalation or referral pathway.

Do not promise confidentiality beyond Evolved's privacy and safety duties. Record only necessary, factual and appropriately protected information.

### Scope and exclusions

- Trainers do not treat anxiety, depression, trauma, body dysmorphia, disordered eating or addiction.
- Trainers do not tell a member to stop prescribed medication or replace clinical care with exercise, nutrition or mindset work.
- General encouragement remains available while qualified care is involved, subject to clinician restrictions and the member's consent.

## DOC-MND-0010 — Support and Tracking Must Earn Their Place

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | Mindset and Behaviour |
| Statement | Goals, plans, prompts, tracking, feedback, supervision and social support are optional coaching tools. Evolved selects the smallest useful support with the member, reviews whether it helps and reduces or removes it when it becomes inaccurate, burdensome, coercive or unnecessary. |
| Primary status | Evolved position with bounded behaviour-intervention evidence |
| Owner | Peter Brown |
| Accepted | 17 August 2026 |
| Review due | 17 August 2027 |
| Immediate-review triggers | Tracking distress or eating-risk signal; surveillance or privacy concern; a tool becomes compulsory; a digital-service or trainer-scope change. |

### Why Evolved holds this position

- Planning, activity monitors and selected feedback systems can produce small average behaviour effects.
- Effects vary, and no technique consistently works for every population or context.
- Monitoring-app research also identifies associations with body-image concern, compulsive exercise and disordered eating in some users, without establishing simple causality.
- Evolved therefore treats support intensity as a practical experiment, not as a test of commitment.

### Coaching application

1. Define what the support is intended to make easier or clearer.
2. Ask whether the member wants it and can use it safely.
3. Use the minimum information and contact needed for that purpose.
4. Review usefulness, burden, accuracy and emotional response.
5. Keep, alter, taper or remove the support from real feedback.

### Scope and exclusions

- Tracking is never required as proof that a member is truthful, motivated or compliant.
- A plan does not solve pain, fatigue, childcare, access, distress or clinical contraindication by itself.
- Trainers do not use app data to diagnose an eating disorder, compulsive exercise, mood disorder or readiness state.
- Necessary Evolved operational records remain governed separately by privacy, safety and service requirements.

## DOC-MND-0011 — Participation Is Shaped by Person, Environment and Relationship

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | Mindset and Behaviour |
| Statement | Participation is shaped by the interaction of capability, opportunity, current demand, physical and social environment, prior experience and what matters to the member. Evolved improves the conditions it can influence and does not reduce attendance or adherence to willpower or character. |
| Primary status | Evolved position with women-specific qualitative and adherence evidence |
| Owner | Peter Brown |
| Accepted | 17 August 2026 |
| Review due | 17 August 2027 |
| Immediate-review triggers | Access or safeguarding complaint; discriminatory or coercive coaching; a community or supervision outcome guarantee; material service-design change. |

### Why Evolved holds this position

- Reviews of women's strength training identify knowledge, stigma, accessibility, supervision, routine, work and caring demands alongside personal factors.
- Pregnancy, postpartum, disability, younger-adult and older-adult evidence shows that barriers and enablers change with context rather than reflecting one female motivation profile.
- Autonomy-supportive coaching, appropriate supervision and social connection may assist participation, but community does not guarantee adherence and supervision should build capability rather than dependence.

### Coaching application

- Ask what is making participation easier or harder now.
- Separate capability, opportunity, competing demand, environment, support and preference rather than labelling the member.
- Improve clarity, welcome, accessibility, predictability and appropriate support within Evolved's control.
- Adapt the next action to current capacity while preserving the member's direction and meaningful training standard.
- Escalate discrimination, safeguarding, disability-access, clinical and severe-distress issues through the appropriate route.

### Scope and exclusions

- Context does not remove all personal agency; it changes the plan and the support required.
- Community, accountability and a women-only environment are service features, not guaranteed mechanisms of adherence or psychological treatment.
- Life stage, body size, disability or attendance history must not be used to infer personality, ambition or motivation.
