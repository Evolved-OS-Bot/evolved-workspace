Starting now means building something worth keeping.

*Goals: recomp | Voice: update*

Strength training is safe during pregnancy and postpartum when done with proper guidance. At The Evolved, we specialise in safe, effective exercises that support your changing body and prepare you for the demands of motherhood.

**P02 — How can I strengthen my core after childbirth?**
*Goals: recomp | Voice: update*

Postpartum core strengthening should focus on reconnecting with deep core muscles — the transverse abdominis and pelvic floor. We recommend starting with breathing exercises, pelvic tilts, and gentle strength movements, then gradually progressing as core strength returns.

### Perimenopause & Menopause

**M01 — Can strength training help with menopause symptoms?**
*Goals: bone-density, recomp, lose-weight | Voice: update*

Yes. Strength training helps balance hormones, maintain bone density, and reduce the risk of weight gain, hot flashes, and other symptoms. It also boosts energy and mood, helping women navigate menopause more comfortably.

---

**M02 — How can I lose weight during perimenopause?**
*Goals: lose-weight | Voice: update*

Strength training and a nutrient-dense, high-protein diet are the two most effective tools for managing weight during perimenopause. Balancing hormones and building muscle helps you maintain a healthy metabolism even as your body changes.

---

**M03 — How do I stay consistent when I'm always tired?**
*Goals: recomp | Voice: update*

Start small. Short, consistent workouts can help increase your energy levels. Prioritising sleep, eating enough to fuel your body, and managing stress all play crucial roles. At The Evolved, we help you find balance and make fitness sustainable even on low-energy days.

---

**M04 — Will exercise help balance my hormones?**
*Goals: recomp, bone-density | Voice: update*

Yes. Strength training helps regulate hormones like cortisol and insulin, which are key to managing weight, energy levels, and mood. It also helps your body produce beneficial hormones like testosterone and growth hormone — both critical for women as they age.

---

**M05 — What types of workouts are best for perimenopausal women?**
*Goals: recomp, lose-weight | Voice: update*

Strength training is the highest-value modality for perimenopausal women — it helps maintain muscle mass, balance hormones, and alleviate symptoms. Pair it with mobility work and moderate cardio to support overall health and wellness.

---

**M06 — How can I maintain muscle mass as I age?**
*Goals: bone-density, get-stronger | Voice: update*

Strength training is essential. Regular resistance training 2–4 times per week combined with adequate protein intake will help preserve muscle and keep you strong and mobile. Women lose roughly 250g of muscle per year from their 30s without intervention — training is how you fight back.

**B01 — What exercises are best for bone health?**
*Goals: bone-density | Voice: update*

Weight-bearing exercises like squats, lunges, and deadlifts are excellent for bone health. They promote bone density and reduce the risk of osteoporosis. Adding resistance training to your routine is a highly effective way to protect your bones.

---

**B02 — How can I stay active with joint pain or arthritis?**
*Goals: bone-density, get-stronger | Voice: current | Homepage: live as "I've had injuries..."*

Low-impact strength training, focusing on form and gradual progression, can help strengthen the muscles around your joints and reduce pain over time. Our coaches specialise in form correction, injury prevention, and training around pain or limitations. We work closely with physios and allied health professionals — if you have a treatment team, we're happy to collaborate.

**B03 — Is it too late to start strength training in my 50s?**
*Goals: bone-density, get-stronger | Voice: update*

---

**B04 — Is weightlifting safe for someone with osteoporosis?**

---

**B05 — What exercises help improve bone density?**
