**Positioning:** For women who want to move more, feel good, and stay lightly active. NOT for muscle building, toning, or bone density. Be honest about this on the page and direct those goals to the Strong, Fit & Flexible Membership.

**Upgrade path:** Strong, Fit & Flexible Membership or Fast Track.

### Strong, Fit & Flexible Membership (Core)

**Positioning:** The flagship strength membership. Built to global NSCA standards. For every woman — 21 wanting to shape her body or 61 wanting to stay strong and independent. Sessions ~12 women, paired training (one works/one rests), trainer coaches every lift.

**Includes:**
- Sculpt & Strength sessions (3+ per week) — progressive overload, female-specific

**Science basis:** NSCA, Dr Gabrielle Lyon, Dr Stacy Sims, Dr Vonda Wright, Dr Paul Cribb. Programmed for hardest-to-serve demographic (postmenopausal women) so all others benefit even more.

**Upgrade path:** Fast Track.

## 1. Small Group Personal Training (SGPT)

**Overview:** Intimate, supervised sessions capped at a small number of participants. Tailored to individual fitness levels and goals, with expert guidance from female personal trainers.

**USPs:**
- Personalized coaching in a supportive, small-group environment
- Focus on strength training, toning, and functional fitness
- Regular progress check-ins to ensure results

### SGPT Session Types

**Sculpt & Strength**
Focused strength-training class designed to tone muscles, enhance body composition, and build functional strength. Combines targeted sculpting movements with heavier lifts for a balanced, full-body workout.
- Perfect for women seeking to tone, define, and strengthen without bulking up
- Incorporates progressive overload for continuous improvement
- Suitable for all levels (modifications for beginners, challenges for advanced)
- Emphasis on form, control, and muscle engagement

**Power Moves**
High-intensity circuit classes blending strength and cardio for explosive power and fat burn.
- Low and high-impact options for all fitness levels
- Dynamic, full-body workouts designed for strength and endurance

**Pilates Evolved**
A dynamic, full-body Pilates workout to build core strength, flexibility, and posture.
- Focus on injury prevention, mobility, and toning
- Combines controlled movements with relaxing breathwork

**Hybrid Fitness Racing**
Strength training meets cardio bursts for the ultimate fitness challenge.
- Ideal for women who love to push their limits in a supportive environment
- Builds power, stamina, and metabolic conditioning

## 5. Downgrade Options (Internal — Not Public-Facing)

Used in retention calls when a member cannot continue their current membership. Not advertised. Two options depending on how much coaching support they need.

Both services are active retention options. They require the governed Membership Service Change Variation before the change is fulfilled. Current control gaps are recorded in `plans/2026-07-30-membership-service-change-control.md`.

### Online Only — $27/week

**Who it's for:** Members who need a downgrade and are self-sufficient enough to train without modifications or coaching.

**Includes:**

- Training data retained

**What it does not include:** Modifications, coach check-ins, or any communication with the coaching team.

**Use case:** Members who know how to train and just need the program.

**Price control:** `$27/week` is the approved retention price and is published in the Membership Service Change Variation.

**Who it's for:** Members who need a downgrade but want to retain personalised coaching.

**Includes:**
- 1 x 30-minute 1:1 PT session every 4 weeks

These offers are made during retention calls only. They are not advertised, not available on request, and require manager awareness when applied.

## Sales FAQs — Product-Specific

**How many times a week do I need to train?**
Programs are flexible to suit your lifestyle, but most women train 2–4 times per week. We'll help you find the right balance to achieve your goals without feeling overwhelmed.

**What does membership include?**
Small-group personal training, access to expert female trainers, personalized nutrition guidance, and a supportive community. 1:1 PT sessions and educational resources also available.

**What's the cancellation policy?**
No lock-in contracts — just a 30-day notice period. The goal is to keep members because they're seeing results and loving the experience, not because of a contract.

**What results can I expect?**
Results vary depending on starting point and commitment. Many members see significant improvements in strength, energy, and body composition within the first few weeks. The focus is on sustainable, long-term progress.
