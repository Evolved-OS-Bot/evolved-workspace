## Megan Brown — Co-Owner & Head Coach

**Headline:** Female Personal Trainer Brisbane | Co-Owner, The Evolved All Female Gym

### Short Bio (1000 characters)

Megan Brown is your head coach and co-owner at The Evolved All Female Gym. She has been coaching women through strength training since 2013 and holds qualifications in personal training, body transformation, and metabolic nutrition. In 2019 she was awarded Female Transformation Specialist of the Year.

Megan's coaching philosophy is built on lived experience. As a mum of two who has navigated her own health challenges, she understands firsthand what it takes to build a body that supports real life — and she brings that understanding to every trainer she works with.

When she took co-ownership of The Evolved in 2023, her mission was clear: build a space where women of every age and life stage discover what their bodies are truly capable of. As a trainer here, you are part of delivering that mission every single day.

Megan sets the standard. She is here to support you, challenge you, and make sure every woman who walks through this door gets your very best.

### Long Bio

When Megan Brown took over The Evolved All Female Gym in 2023, it was more than a business decision. It was about keeping a dream alive for the women of Brisbane. She had seen firsthand how much this space meant to women searching for strength, confidence, and community.

Megan refused to let that vision die with the old owners. Instead, she poured her heart into completely renewing the energy of the gym, inside and out, creating a place where women of every age could thrive.

"Strength isn't just about lifting weights. It's about building a body that keeps up with your life, your ambitions, and the people who rely on you." — Megan Brown

### From Dancer to Women's Strength Coach

Megan's journey into strength coaching began long before she took ownership of The Evolved. As a former dancer, she had always been connected to movement, but life had other plans.

Between the demands of running a business and becoming a mum twice over, Megan felt the same struggles many women face. Fatigue, injuries, and the never-ending juggle of responsibilities left her wondering how to prioritise her own body without burning out.

She knows what it feels like to push through pain, to doubt whether you have the time or energy, and to want to feel like yourself again.

### The Diagnosis That Changed Everything

In 2017, just 18 months after giving birth to her second child, Megan was diagnosed with chronic fatigue. Suddenly, everything she thought she knew about training and resilience was challenged.

She had to relearn how to move, how to fuel her body properly, and most importantly, how to leave enough energy in the tank for the demands of motherhood and running a business.

This difficult chapter reshaped her entire approach to health. Instead of chasing exhaustion, Megan discovered the power of training smarter and prioritising recovery. It became clear that true strength was not about doing more but about finding the right balance to support long-term health and energy.

### Qualifications and Recognition

Megan has been part of the fitness industry since 2013 and has owned and run body transformation businesses since 2014. She is a qualified Metabolic Nutritionist and holds certifications in personal training and body transformation.

She has trained under Dr Paul Cribb PhD and Sharlene Sault, refining her scientific approach to training and nutrition.

Drawing on her recovery from chronic fatigue, Megan created the FitMum Method: a program designed to help mums rebuild their strength and confidence postpartum without sacrificing their health.

In 2019, she was awarded Female Transformation Specialist of the Year.

### Coaching Philosophy

Megan believes strength training should fit into your life, not the other way around. Her philosophy is rooted in efficiency, injury prevention, and helping women feel in control of their bodies.

She understands the realities of fatigue, time pressures, and fluctuating energy because she has lived them herself. Having worked with hundreds of women across Brisbane, Megan has guided clients through every stage of life: from teenage years to post-menopause.

### Beyond the Gym

Megan is a proud supporter of the arts, donating regularly to the Queensland Ballet in West End, and loyally backs Women's Legal Service QLD, taking part in their annual River to Rooftop fundraiser.

### At The Evolved

At The Evolved, Megan leads by example. Whether it is helping a new mum regain confidence, guiding a woman through perimenopause, or coaching someone toward their first personal best, Megan brings empathy, expertise, and lived experience to every session.

She knows the challenges. She has walked the path. And she is here to make sure no woman has to do it alone.

## Peter Brown — Co-Founder & Director

**Headline:** Co-Founder & Director, The Evolved All Female Gym | Strength Coach | Nutrition Specialist | Business Strategist

### Short Bio (1000 characters)

Peter Brown is the co-founder and director of The Evolved All Female Gym, responsible for the strategy, systems, and infrastructure that drive the business. He has been in the fitness industry since 2008 and holds qualifications in personal training, metabolic nutrition, body transformation, and sports nutrition.

Peter's career began on the gym floor, where he coached hundreds of women and families through evidence-based strength and body composition programs. Over time his focus shifted to building the systems behind exceptional coaching delivery.

Today he leads the business strategy, marketing, technology, automation, and staff development at The Evolved. Working alongside Megan, who leads coaching excellence, Peter is the architect behind the member experience from first contact through to long-term results.

His passion for women's strength training is personal. Raised by a single mother, with two younger sisters, and now raising two daughters alongside his wife Megan, Peter has spent his career building something he genuinely believes in: a place where women grow stronger at every stage of life.

### Long Bio

Peter Brown is the Co-Founder and Director of The Evolved All Female Gym, where he leads the strategy, systems and innovation behind one of Brisbane's leading female-focused strength training facilities.

A strength coach, nutrition specialist, entrepreneur, author and business strategist, Peter has dedicated his career to understanding what truly drives long-term health, body composition change and human performance. Since entering the fitness industry in 2008, he has helped transform the lives of hundreds of women and families through evidence-based strength training, nutrition coaching and behaviour change systems.

Peter holds qualifications as a Fully Qualified Personal Trainer (Cert III & IV), Metabolic Nutritionist, Body Transformation Specialist, Athletic Conditioning Specialist and Sports Nutrition Specialist. He was recognised as Metabolic Precision's Business Person of the Year in 2016 and later became a Mentor of Trainers within the organisation.

Between 2009 and 2019, Peter competed successfully in state and national-level bodybuilding competitions through the World Fitness Federation (WFF), International Natural Bodybuilding Association (INBA) and other recognised federations. During this period he also coached competitive physique athletes, helping clients prepare for competition and achieve high-level body composition outcomes.

His entrepreneurial journey began with Lean Body Personal Training in 2009 before founding Fisique Health & Fitness Professionals in 2012. Starting from outdoor group sessions in a local park, Peter grew the business into a commercial gym before expanding into a private studio with his wife Megan in 2014.

Following the success of Fisique, Peter and Megan went on to create The FitMum Method and FitDad Method, helping hundreds of parents navigate strength, body composition and lifestyle change through some of life's busiest seasons.

In 2023, Peter co-authored the FitFam Cookbook alongside Megan, providing practical, family-friendly nutrition strategies designed to help busy parents simplify healthy eating without sacrificing results.

Today, Peter serves as Co-Founder and Director of The Evolved All Female Gym. While his career began as a coach and body transformation specialist, his role today extends far beyond the gym floor. He leads business strategy, marketing, sales, technology, automation, product development and operational systems — building the frameworks that support member acquisition, retention, staff development, service delivery and long-term growth.

Working alongside Megan, who leads coaching excellence and program delivery, Peter has helped create a business model that combines evidence-based strength training with exceptional member experience, operational efficiency and scalable systems.

His passion for women's strength training is personal. Raised by a single mother, with two younger sisters, and now raising two daughters alongside his wife Megan, Peter has spent his career building something he genuinely believes in: a place where women grow stronger at every stage of life.

His philosophy is simple: strength is one of the most powerful predictors of health, confidence, resilience and independence. Through The Evolved, Peter's mission is to help women live stronger, longer and more independently.
