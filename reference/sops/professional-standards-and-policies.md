# Professional Standards and Policies

**Organisation:** The Evolved All Female Gym
**Applies to:** All employees

---

## Purpose

This document outlines the professional standards, policies, and code of conduct for employees at The Evolved All Female Gym. It ensures a safe, professional, and empowering environment for staff, members, and employees.

---

## 1. Professional Standards

### 1.1 Conduct and Behaviour

- Treat all members, staff, and employees with respect and courtesy
- Foster an inclusive and supportive environment, free from discrimination, harassment, or bullying
- Arrive on time, appropriately dressed, and prepared for all scheduled sessions

### 1.2 Boundaries and Respect

- Respect members' personal space, boundaries, and fitness goals
- Obtain explicit consent before providing physical adjustments or personal advice

## 2. Operational Policies

### 2.1 Programming & Deliverability

- The Small Group Personal Training program is to be delivered as structured — without major changes to sets, reps, or movement patterns
- Trainers may progress or regress a movement based on an individual's ability (strength, fitness, and/or injury)
- 1:1 clients must always have **12 weeks of structured training** outlined in their training app — random compliance checks will be conducted

### 2.2 Scheduling and Availability

- Notify members and management of any schedule changes or cancellations at least **24 hours in advance**

### 2.3 Facility Use

- Keep training areas clean, tidy, and organised during and after sessions
- Report any damage to equipment or unsafe conditions to management immediately

### 2.4 Timesheets & Pay

### 2.6 Injury, Health Conditions, Surgery & Pregnancy

**The position:** Training modification is always the starting point. Cessation is a last resort.

Evidence shows that modified training produces faster recovery and better outcomes in the majority of injuries, health conditions, surgical recovery, and pregnancy. Trainers must operate from this position and communicate it with confidence.

**Protocol when a member presents with a health or injury concern:**

1. Ask questions first. Understand the nature and impact of the condition before offering any recommendation.

2. Explore what is still possible. In almost every case, some form of modified training is achievable.

3. Offer allied health collaboration. With the member's explicit consent, The Evolved can communicate with their GP, physiotherapist, obstetrician, or specialist to align on a safe training plan. This is a standard offer, not an exceptional one.

4. If modification is not immediately feasible, offer an Injury Triage Session: a complimentary 1:1 session to assess capability, design a modified program, and map a return plan.

5. Membership hold is the next option only if modified training is genuinely not possible.

6. Cancellation should only be reached when a member is medically advised to cease all physical activity for an extended, unknown period.

**What to say:**

"Evidence shows that training modifications have faster recovery times and better outcomes in the majority of injuries and health conditions, even in cases of pregnancy or surgery. We can be very flexible during this time."

"We regularly work with allied health professionals to get the best outcome for our members. With your permission, we can work alongside your practitioner. Are you open to that?"

**Documentation:** Notify management of any member with a significant injury, health condition, surgery, or pregnancy. Management will update the member's profile in GHL and brief the relevant coach.

**Escalation:** Any uncertainty about what is safe for a specific condition must be escalated to management before proceeding. Do not guess.

## 3. Safety Policies

### 3.1 Health and Safety Compliance

- Maintain current certifications in **First Aid and CPR** — provide copies to management upon renewal
- Follow all gym safety protocols and Queensland health and safety regulations
- Familiarise yourself with emergency procedures, including evacuation plans

### 3.2 Equipment Use

- Ensure equipment is used safely and for its intended purpose
- Supervise members closely to prevent misuse or injury

### 3.3 Incident Reporting

- Report all injuries, incidents, or unsafe practices to management promptly
