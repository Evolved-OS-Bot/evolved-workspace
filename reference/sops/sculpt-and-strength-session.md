# SOP: Sculpt & Strength Session

**Class:** Sculpt & Strength Session
**Gym:** The Evolved All Female Gym
**Effective Date:** 20/01/2025
**Prepared By:** Peter & Megan Brown
**Version:** 1.1

---

## 0. Prerequisite

All trainers must complete **36 workouts recorded in their own Trainerize account** before being permitted to deliver Sculpt & Strength Sessions.

The trainer completes these workouts as a client to experience The Evolved product and member journey firsthand. This builds personal belief in the offer so she can become a genuine raving fan of what we provide.

---

## 1. Purpose

Outline procedures for conducting Sculpt & Strength Sessions — combining strength training and body-sculpting exercises in a safe, supportive environment.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all employees and instructors delivering Sculpt & Strength Sessions at The Evolved All Female Gym.

---

## 3. Objectives

- Improve members' muscular strength, endurance, and body composition
- Ensure proper form and technique during all exercises
- Foster a positive and empowering class environment

---

## 4. Class Structure

- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Capacity:** 15 members

---

## 4.1 Pre-Session Checklist

**Cleanliness & Hygiene**
- Ensure all equipment is clean and sanitised
- Check floors, mirrors, and workout areas are free of dust, debris, and clutter

**Equipment Setup**
- Arrange equipment according to the session's layout
- Confirm all necessary items are available and in good condition
- Test any machines or devices for functionality — report if faulty

**Environment**
- Adjust room temperature (fans as needed)
- Set up music/audio to appropriate volume and playlist
- Ensure adequate lighting — no glare or overly dim conditions

**Safety**
- Inspect equipment for damage or wear — replace if necessary
- Confirm walkways and workout areas are clear of obstructions
- Ensure first aid kits and AEDs are accessible and stocked

---

## 4.2 Member Comfort & Experience

**Welcoming Atmosphere**
- Greet members warmly as they arrive and direct them to the workout area
- Provide an overview of the session and address any initial questions

**Accessibility**
- Set up to accommodate varying fitness levels — modifications readily available
- Arrange additional space or equipment for members requiring alternative setups

**Safety Communication**
- Brief members on equipment usage and safety protocols before the session
- Identify emergency exits and highlight the location of first aid kits

---

## 4.3 Warm-Up (5–10 minutes)

**Objective:** Prepare the body for strength exercises, increase mobility, and reduce injury risk.

- Use the prescribed warm-up exercises in Trainerize
- Within your scope, give alternatives where needed to address individual mobility needs
- If unsure of an appropriate alternative: deliver what is prescribed, apologise, and tell the member you'll ask management — then do so as soon as possible (provide client name and area/type of immobility)
- Gather clients as a group — highlight the focus for the day and build rapport
- Ask clients to open their Trainerize app and determine their working weight for the first exercise
- Group 2–3 people together with similar working weights to minimise racking/unracking
- Explain the Performance Warm-Up — break down working weight into the prescribed percentages, have them perform the assigned reps and sets, finishing with 1 rep at working weight before their first working set

---

## 4.4 Main Session (30–40 minutes)

**Format:** Station-based workout focusing on strength and sculpting.

- Use the prescribed workout in Trainerize — adhere to sets, reps, and movement patterns outlined
- Ensure everyone has a spotter — clients spot each other in most situations
- Have all clients completing a working set in synchronisation to make form correction and feedback easier
- Position yourself so your back is never turned to a member and you can clearly see all moving clients at all times
- **Provide at least one touch point per client per working set** — each member must receive a cue, adjustment, or acknowledgement from you at least 8 times per workout
- When giving cues or adjustments: always ask for consent, highlight what they are already doing well, then frame the cue or adjustment constructively
- **Do not sit down.** Do not perform an unrelated exercise. Remain on your feet at all times while delivering a workout
- **No personal phones during class** except to film and upload Instagram stories — tag @the_evolved_allfemalegym. All other personal phone use is prohibited during class
- Actively preempt working space setup as the workout progresses (refer to the laminated diagram in the gym) — clients should not set up their own working space unless it has been checked by the supervising trainer
- Within your scope, give progressions or regressions to individualise the workout to each member's needs
- If unsure of an appropriate progression or regression: deliver what is prescribed, apologise, and tell the member you'll ask management — then do so as soon as possible (provide client name and movement pattern requiring progression/regression)

**Equipment required:** Dumbbells, kettlebells, barbells, resistance bands, benches, mats

---

## 4.5 Cool-Down (5–10 minutes)

**Objective:** Lower heart rate, reduce muscle tension, and enhance recovery.

- Use the prescribed cool-down provided in Trainerize

---

## 4.6 Post-Session Responsibilities

**Member Farewell**
- Acknowledge what a great job everyone did
- Congratulate 2–3 members individually — identify 1 win or 1 improvement and offer a class recommendation or complimentary 1:1 PT session
- Ask members when they're coming in next

**Clean-Up**
- Wipe down and sanitise all equipment used
- Return all items to their designated storage areas
- Check for forgotten personal items — store in lost and found
- Wash cool towels in bacterial wash, then roll and place back in the fridge

**Environment Reset**
- Restore the workout space to its original condition for the next session
- Inspect for damage or hazards — report to management if necessary
- Ensure the toilet is closed after the session

---

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities

**Instructor:**
- Arrive 15 minutes early to set up equipment and greet members
- Conduct a quick check-in to identify any injuries or concerns
- Provide clear instructions and demonstrate exercises
- Offer modifications or progressions for various fitness levels
- Monitor participant safety and encourage proper form throughout

**Participant:**
- Arrive on time and inform the instructor of any injuries or concerns
- Wear appropriate workout attire and footwear
- Wipe down equipment after use

---

## 6. Safety Guidelines

- Inspect all equipment before the session for damage or wear
- Ensure the training area is clear of hazards
- Keep a first aid kit nearby and be familiar with its location
- Encourage hydration breaks during the session
- Modify or replace exercises that may cause discomfort or pain

---

## 7. Session Feedback

- Gather feedback from participants after the session to assess satisfaction and areas for improvement
- Record attendance in Trainerize
- Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with any issues encountered during the session

---

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Issue | Response |
|---|---|
| Low attendance | Reach out to regular members who were absent to check in |
| Injuries | Follow gym emergency procedures and document the incident |
| Equipment malfunction | Substitute exercises or notify management immediately |
| No clients show | To be paid, you must be present at the premises completing an approved alternative activity |
