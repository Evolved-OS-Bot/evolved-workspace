# SOP — Monthly Member Check-In

**Gym:** The Evolved All Female Gym

---

## Purpose

Monthly check-ins are an opportunity to highlight member success, identify the next area for improvement, and — for members not currently on 1:1 PT — give them time to recognise the value in it and reassess.

---

## Follow-Up Process

Call the member one month after joining, then every month after that.

**Best times to call:** 7–8am, 12–1pm, or 5–6pm

**If they answer:**
- Ask how their training is going
- Are they still enjoying it?
- Do they feel like they're progressing toward their goals?
- Is there any extra help they're not currently getting?
- Record notes in Trainerize

**If no answer after 3 attempts, send this text:**

> *"Hey [name], I tried calling a few times but didn't hear back. Just wanted to see how you were going with your training and if you need any extra help reaching your goals."*

---

## Outcome Triage

**They want more help** — walk them through the PT sign-up:
- Use the PT Agreement Form
- Confirm their first session date and first debit date (debit must occur before the first session)

**They're doing fine** — keep the relationship warm. A "no" is a "not right now." Stay the friendly, engaged trainer they love and follow up again next month.

**Red flag** — any intention to cancel, disgruntled member, or anything you can't handle:
- Email **info@theevolvedgym.com.au**
- Subject line: `RED FLAG — [Member Name]`
- Include full details of the situation in the body
- Management will follow up directly
