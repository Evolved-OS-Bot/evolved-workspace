# SOP: Street Outreach — Mini Strength Assessment

**Version:** 1.0
**Created:** 2026-07-06
**Owner:** Head Trainer / All Trainers
**Purpose:** Capture attention in public spaces, demonstrate real strength deficits to women on the spot, and book them directly into a full Strength Assessment.

---

## Overview

This is a field outreach activity. A trainer approaches women in a public space, runs a 3-minute mini strength test, reveals the result against an age-matched standard, and books them into a Strength Assessment via the trainer's phone before they walk away.

The goal is not to educate. The goal is to create a felt moment of awareness and convert it to a booked appointment.

---

## Equipment Required

| Item | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Hand dynamometer | Grip strength test — clinical credibility, instant number |
| Phone with camera (on tripod or held by second trainer) | Social media capture |
| Phone with SA booking page loaded | On-the-spot booking |
| Branded t-shirt / gym gear | Visual authority and brand recognition |
| Optional: small printed card with strength standards table | Leave-behind if they don't book on the spot |

**No additional equipment is needed.** The single-leg balance test requires nothing beyond a flat surface.

---

## The Test Options

Always run both in sequence. The dynamometer delivers the clinical credibility and the number. The single-leg balance delivers the physical "felt" moment that makes it real.

### Primary — Hand Dynamometer (Grip Strength)

**Why:** Grip strength is one of the strongest independent predictors of all-cause mortality and muscle mass. A number on a screen lands differently than a body sensation. It is also fast, objective, and requires no setup.

**Setup:** Dominant hand, elbow at 90 degrees, squeeze maximally for 3 seconds. Take best of two attempts.

**Age-matched benchmarks for women (minimum standard):**

| Age | Minimum Pass (kg) |
|---|---|
| 20–29 | 28 kg |
| 30–39 | 27 kg |
| 40–49 | 25 kg |
| 50–59 | 22 kg |
| 60+ | 19 kg |

**Note:** Standards above reflect minimum functional strength. Many research references use higher cutoffs. Frame these as the floor, not the goal.

### Secondary — Single-Leg Stand with Eyes Closed

**Why:** Zero equipment, takes under 30 seconds, and most women fail faster than they expect. The wobble and the look on their face when they realise they cannot hold it is the moment that makes this memorable. Neuromuscular control and balance decline faster than most women know, and this makes it visible.

**Setup:** Stand on one leg, eyes closed, hands on hips. Time from eyes closing to when they open their eyes, grab something, or put the foot down. Swap legs and repeat. Take the better of the two sides.

**Age-matched benchmarks for women (minimum standard):**

| Age | Minimum Pass |
|---|---|
| 20–39 | 20 seconds |
| 40–49 | 15 seconds |
| 50–59 | 8 seconds |
| 60+ | 4 seconds |

Most sedentary women under-perform their age bracket. This is the felt moment.

### Recommended Sequence

Run the dynamometer first (30 seconds, creates curiosity with a number). Then offer the single-leg balance as the physical follow-up. Tell them it is harder than it looks. This gives you two data points, extends the engagement, and the balance test creates the body-level awareness that converts to a booking.

## The Script

Work through each phase in order. Do not skip the consent phase.

---

### Phase 1 — Attention Hook

Approach with open body language and a smile. Speak clearly and confidently.

> "Hey, quick question for you — we are doing a free strength test with women today. It takes about 2 minutes and we can tell you exactly how strong you are for your age. Are you up for it?"

**If hesitant:**

> "There is no gym involved, no equipment — it is literally just one physical test and I will show you where you sit against the standard for your age. Totally free."

**Do not pitch the gym yet.** The test is the product right now.

---

### Phase 2 — Frame the Stakes

Once they agree, frame the test before you run it. This primes the reveal.

> "The reason we are doing this is that muscle strength is one of the biggest predictors of how well women age. Most women have no idea how weak they are until it actually starts affecting their daily life. We want to change that."

Pause briefly. Let that land.

> "I am going to do two quick tests: a grip strength test with this device, then a balance test that sounds easy but is not. I will show you exactly what your results mean for your age. Ready?"

---

### Phase 3 — The Social Media Ask

Ask this before the test starts, not after. Asking after feels like a setup.

> "One more thing: we are raising awareness around women's strength and we are capturing some of this today for social media. Would you be okay if we filmed the test? You do not have to say anything on camera — it is just the movement, nothing embarrassing. And we will tag you if you want."

**If yes:** Signal the camera person to start rolling. No microphone needed for the test itself.

**If no:** Respect it immediately. Continue with the test, no camera.

---

### Phase 4 — Run the Test

**Dynamometer (run first):**

Hand them the device with the dial showing. Demonstrate the grip position on yourself first.

> "Dominant hand, elbow at a right angle, squeeze as hard as you can for three seconds. I will count you down: 3, 2, 1, squeeze."

Note the result. Offer a second attempt if they want one. Do not reveal the verdict yet.

> "Good. One more test. This one is physical and most people find it harder than they expect."

**Single-leg balance, eyes closed (run second):**

Guide them to stand on a flat, clear patch of ground.

> "Stand on whichever leg feels stronger, hands on your hips. Close your eyes when you are ready and I will start the timer. Try to stay as still as you can."

Start the timer when their eyes close. Stop when they open their eyes, grab for something, or put the foot down. Note the time. Swap legs and repeat.

> "Okay now the other side."

Take the better of the two results. Do not comment until Phase 5.

---

### Phase 5 — The Reveal

Deliver both results together. Be direct and non-judgemental — you are not shaming them, you are showing them reality. Combine the two scores into a single picture.

**Both results below standard:**

> "So your grip score is [X] kilograms. For a woman your age we want to see at least [Y]. And you held the balance for [X] seconds: the standard for your age is [Y]. Both of those are telling us the same thing — your strength and your neuromuscular control are below where they need to be. That is not a criticism; most women are in this position. What it means is that without addressing it, this does not stay the same. It gets worse."

**Grip below, balance okay:**

> "Your grip is [X] kilograms — that is below the minimum of [Y] for your age. Your balance is actually decent. But grip strength is one of the best predictors of how you age at a cellular level. The number matters, and yours is telling us there is a gap."

**Balance below, grip okay:**

> "Your grip strength is good — [X] kilograms, above the standard. But that balance test? [X] seconds. For your age we want to see at least [Y]. That wobble you felt is a sign that your neuromuscular system — the connection between your brain and your muscles — is not where it needs to be. That is fixable, but only with the right training."

**Both pass:**

> "Good news — you are above the minimum on both. That said, minimum is not the goal. The goal is to be well above this line and to stay there as you get older. There is almost certainly a gap between where you are and where your strength should be. That is what we work on at The Evolved."

---

### Phase 6 — Social Media Clip (If Filming)

If consent was given and the test is on camera, get a brief talking piece after the reveal. Keep it natural and short.

Trainer asks on camera:

> "So [name], you just found out your strength is [below standard / at standard]. What is your reaction to that? Did you expect that?"

Let them respond naturally. 15 seconds is enough.

Then trainer, looking at camera:

> "This is exactly why we do this. Strength is not optional — it is the thing that keeps women independent, healthy, and strong through every stage of life. If you want to know where you sit, link in bio."

---

### Phase 7 — The Close

Do not let the moment cool. Move directly from the reveal to the booking.

> "We run full Strength Assessments at The Evolved — it is a 60-minute session where we go through everything properly, build a picture of where you are, and work out exactly what you need. It is free for your first assessment. Can I book you in now?"

Hand them your phone or turn it to face them with the booking page open:

**`go.theevolvedgym.com.au/strength-assessment`**

> "It takes 30 seconds — just pick a time that works."

If they book: get their name and confirm the time verbally.

> "Perfect, [name]. You will get a confirmation straight away. We are at [address / West End]. I will see you there."

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I do not have time right now" | "The test itself is 2 minutes. What is your name? I can send you the link right now so you have it." |
| "I already go to the gym" | "That is great. This is still worth doing — most gym-goers are surprised by their score. It is free and it is specific to your age." |
| "I am not really into gyms" | "This is not about selling you a gym. It is a free strength check. You can decide what to do with the information." |
| "I will think about it" | "Totally fine. Can I grab your number and send you the link? No follow-up calls — just the link." |
| "Is this a sales thing?" | "It is a free assessment. There is no obligation to join anything. We just think women deserve to know where they actually stand." |

## Social Media Capture Protocol

**Who films:** Second trainer or tripod-mounted phone. The trainer running the test should never be managing the camera.

**Shot list:**

| Shot | What to capture |
|---|---|
| Wide: the approach | Trainer walking up to woman in public space |
| Mid: the test | Grip test in progress, then single-leg balance — timer visible during balance test |
| Close: the reveal | Trainer showing the result on the dynamometer screen, then reacting to the balance time |
| Talking piece | Woman reacting to result (consent confirmed before rolling) |

**Caption direction:**

> Most women have no idea how strong (or weak) they actually are. We are finding out. Tag a woman who needs to take this test. Free strength assessments at The Evolved — link in bio.

**Do not post anyone who declined consent.** If the shot includes background passers-by, check for identifiable faces and crop or blur before posting.

**Tagging:** Offer to tag the participant if they have Instagram. Ask before posting: "What is your Instagram handle?"

## Locations and Timing

**Best locations:**
- Busy pedestrian areas near West End (Boundary Street, Davies Park markets, South Bank)
- Near cafes or brunch spots on weekend mornings (women 25–50, relaxed, time available)
- Near parkrun finish lines (already fitness-adjacent, open to the conversation)
- Local markets

**Best timing:**
- Saturday and Sunday mornings, 7:30am to 11am
- Weekday lunch hours near commercial areas for the 30–45 demographic

**Minimum crew:** Two trainers. One to run the test and script, one to handle camera and logistics.

## Lead Capture (If They Don't Book)

If they will not book on the spot, capture a lead before they leave.

> "No problem. Can I send you the link? What is the best number or Instagram to send it to?"

Log in GHL immediately with:
- First name
- Phone or Instagram handle
- Age bracket (rough)
- Test result
- Source: Street Outreach

Assign to the Street Outreach pipeline in GHL and follow up within 24 hours.

---

## Safety Notes

- Never pressure a woman to continue a test if she expresses discomfort.
- For the single-leg balance, advise anyone with a known ankle or knee injury to skip it. The dynamometer result alone is sufficient.
- Ensure the surface is flat and dry before running the balance test. Do not run it on wet ground or uneven pavement.
- If a woman appears unwell at any point, stop the test.
- Do not run this activity in heat above 30 degrees. Find a shaded location.
