# SOP: Intro Sessions

**Service:** Intro Sessions
**Gym:** The Evolved All Female Gym
**Effective Date:** 20/02/2025
**Prepared By:** Peter Brown
**Version:** 2.5

---

## 1. Purpose

Outline procedures for conducting Intro Sessions for new members. Sessions are designed to assign appropriate exercise progressions across the three main movement patterns, build confidence, introduce key training principles, and set members up for long-term success.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all trainers conducting Intro Sessions for new members.

---

## 3. Objectives

- Assign the member's exercise level for Squat, Deadlift, and Bench Press
- Set up their Trainerize app and establish goals
- Build rapport, confidence, and understanding of training principles
- Record baseline data (reps, load, biometrics) for app aggregation
- Plant the seed for ongoing 1:1 PT

---

## 4. Session Structure

- **Duration:** 30 minutes per session
- **Fit & Flexible members:** no Intro Session
- **Strong, Fit & Flexible Membership members:** 1 Intro Session
- **Fast Track members:** 4 Intro Sessions

Use the end of Session 1 to upsell 1:1 PT — explain that additional sessions allow you to continue this assessment and progression process together.

---

## 5. Session 1 — Movement Assessment

### Purpose

Assign the appropriate exercise for each of the three main movement patterns: **Squat, Deadlift, and Bench Press.**

This session moves quickly. Avoid failure — work to a **7 RPE**. The goal is to identify the exercise in each pattern where the client demonstrates control and can achieve a working load, not to push limits.

### Preparation
- Send a friendly intro text upon receiving the calendar invite
- Send a reminder text the morning of the session
- Arrive 15 minutes early to set up and review client details

### Greeting & Onboarding
- Welcome the client and introduce yourself
- Explain the purpose of intro sessions (assign movement levels, introduce systems, build confidence)
- Check if they received their Welcome Pack — provide link if not

### App Setup: Trainerize
- Help them download, book 2–3 classes, and walk through key features (workouts, progress tracking)
- Complete the baseline measurements and progress photos using the procedure below, then enter or upload everything into Trainerize
- Discuss goals — create 1–3 main goals in Trainerize
- Assign 1–2 Level 1 Habits aligned to their main goal

### Baseline Measurements

Explain that consistent measurements give the member an objective way to see progress. Record every measurement in centimetres to the nearest 0.5 cm.

Ask permission before every individual measurement: for example, *"Is it okay if I measure your waist now?"* A previous yes is not blanket permission for the remaining measurements.

Stand slightly to the client's left when taking front-facing measurements. This protects personal space and avoids standing directly in each other's breathing path.

Use a pen mark or a quick fingertip press that leaves a temporary white spot when a midpoint needs to remain visible. Keep the tape even around the body at every site.

Measure in this order:

1. **Shoulders:** The client stands front-facing while the trainer stands slightly to her left. Wrap the whole tape around the body at the widest part of the shoulders.
2. **Right bicep:** The client stands side-facing with her right arm bent. Identify the acromion, the bony point at the top and outside of the shoulder; measure from there to the elbow, halve that distance, mark the midpoint of the humerus and wrap the tape evenly around the arm at that point.
3. **Waist:** The client stands front-facing while the trainer stands slightly to her left. Ask her to point to her belly button, then wrap the tape evenly around the waist at that level.
4. **Hips:** The client stands side-facing. Wrap the tape evenly around the thickest part of the glutes.
5. **Right quad:** The client stands side-facing. Measure from the front bony point of the iliac crest to the middle of the knee, halve that distance, mark the midpoint of the femur and wrap the tape evenly around the thigh at that point.
6. **Right calf:** The client stands side-facing. Wrap the tape evenly around the thickest part of the lower leg.

### Progress Photos

Ask separately for permission to take progress photos. If the client declines, accept the answer and move on without pressure.

Invite the client to remove loose outer layers and remain in fitted training clothes. Never pressure her to remove clothing or expose more than she is comfortable with.

Use the phone camera rather than Trainerize's in-app camera. Take one front-facing, one side-facing and one rear-facing photo as the client turns between positions.

Frame the full body with an even gap above the head and below the soles of the feet. Keep the distance, camera height, lighting and framing as consistent as practical for future comparisons.

Upload the photos to the client's Trainerize account after taking them. Never share before or after photos unless the client has given explicit permission for that separate use.

### Movement Assessment: Progression Rules

Open the Onboarding Session Workout in Trainerize.

**Progression logic:**
- Apply PRIME to every rep: Posture, Range, Identify, Measure, then Evaluate & Evolve
- Advance only when the client completes 10 pain-free, repeatable reps to PRIME standard and the exercise remains below 7 RPE
- Stop at and assign the highest exercise the client can perform proficiently at about 7 RPE
- If posture, range, control, comfort or safety breaks down, stop and assign the previous proficient exercise
- Never skip a stage or progress solely because 10 reps were completed
- Before progressing from Barbell Romanian Deadlift to Trap Bar Deadlift, require 12 reps at 35 kg total barbell load. Every rep must include a controlled one-second hold with the bar just below mid-knee while the client maintains a PRIME postural position
- Record all reps and load for the app to aggregate for future use
- Leave exercises not completed blank

**Rest:** 30 seconds between sets

**Breathing and bracing:** Teach in all exercises. Begin in a lying position and transfer the knowledge to standing and loaded movements.

---

#### Squat Pattern

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Glute Bridge | 1 × 10 | No load. Move to next exercise if form maintained for all reps |
| Bodyweight Box Squat | 1 × 10 | Use plates to adjust box height — find the height with the most control. No load if form maintained at nexus point |
| Bodyweight Squat | 1 × 10 | No load if form maintained at nexus point |
| Kettlebell Goblet Squat | 1 × 10 | Record reps and weight. If 7 RPE not achieved with a load above 10kg, move to Nexus Point Squat |
| Nexus Point Squat | 1 × 10 | Record reps and weight only if the client maintains an upright torso at the nexus point |

#### Hinge Pattern

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Broomstick Hinge | 1 × 10 | Establish the hinge pattern. Advance only when all reps meet PRIME and remain below 7 RPE |
| Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift | 1 × 10 | Record reps and load. Advance only when all reps meet PRIME and remain below 7 RPE |
| Barbell Romanian Deadlift | 1 × 12 at 35 kg | Hold one second just below mid-knee on every rep. Progress only when all 12 reps maintain a PRIME postural position |
| Trap Bar Deadlift | 1 × 10 | Record reps and load. Advance only when all reps meet PRIME and remain below 7 RPE |
| Barbell Deadlift | 1 × 10 | Record reps and load. Assign only when all reps meet PRIME at about 7 RPE |

#### Bench Pattern

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Dumbbell Bench Press | 1 × 10 | Record reps and weight. If the client maintains scapula retraction, arch, and active hips with a total load above 15kg, move to Barbell Bench Press |
| Barbell Bench Press | 1 × 10 | Record reps and weight only if the client maintains scapula retraction, arch, and active hips throughout |

## 6. Session 2 — First Working Sets

### Purpose

Complete only the 3 exercises assigned in Session 1 — one working set each, 3 total working sets for the session. Leave all other exercises blank.

Today's focus: progressing posture and range of motion, identifying areas of improvement, and teaching the member how to measure and track reps and load.

### Preparation
- Send a reminder text 24 hours before
- Arrive 15 minutes early to review progress notes

### Check-In
- Ask how their week of training has been
- Are there any sore or stiff muscles that need warming up? Take time to warm those areas up. If nothing is sore or stiff, warm up for the exercise selection appropriately.

### RM Education

Teach the client what rep range and RM means:

> *"10–12RM means pick a weight you can lift for 10, 11, or 12 reps — but not 13. You should reach rep 12 and not be able to complete rep 13 with good form. If you can, add 1–2kg. On the contrary, if you choose a weight you can only lift for 6 reps, remove weight until you're within the rep range."*

**Progressive overload logic:**
- Complete 12 reps across all working sets → increase weight by 1–2kg next week
- Complete 10 or 11 reps → aim for one more rep at the same weight next week

### Movement Session

Use the Performance Warm Up and Acclimation Sets in Trainerize. Watch these videos and relay the information to the client.

**RPE target:** 7–8
**Rest:** 2 minutes between sets

Complete only the client's assigned exercises (1 working set each):

| Pattern | Progression Options | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|---|
| **Squat** | Glute Bridge / BW Box Squat / BW Squat / KB Goblet Squat / Nexus Point Squat | 1 × 10–12RM |
| **Hinge** | Broomstick Hinge / Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift / Barbell Romanian Deadlift / Trap Bar Deadlift / Barbell Deadlift | 1 × 10–12RM |
| **Bench** | DB Bench Press / Barbell Bench Press | 1 × 10–12RM |

Teach breathing and bracing in all exercises. Begin in a lying position and transfer to standing and loaded movements.

Record all reps and load for the app.

Retain the Session 1 hinge assignment unless the member demonstrates the next exercise to PRIME standard. Use the same sequence and stop rule whenever reassessing.

The 35 kg Barbell Romanian Deadlift gate remains mandatory in every reassessment: 12 reps with a one-second hold just below mid-knee on every rep in a PRIME postural position.

### Post-Session Tasks
- Confirm availability for the next 2 intro sessions (Fast Track members)
- Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with updates
- Send a post-session text with encouragement

## 7. Session 3 — Progressing Volume

### Purpose

Complete only the exercises the client is currently working on. Use this session to reassess — can they progress to the next exercise in any of their patterns?

Today's focus: 2 working sets per exercise, 6 total working sets for the session.

### Preparation
- Send a reminder text 24 hours before
- Arrive 15 minutes early to review progress notes

### Check-In
- Ask how their week of training has been
- Are there any sore or stiff muscles that need warming up? Warm up accordingly.

### Movement Session

Use the Performance Warm Up and Acclimation Sets. Reinforce RM education and progressive overload logic from Session 2.

**RPE target:** 7–8
**Rest:** 2 minutes between sets

Complete only the client's assigned exercises. Reassess progression opportunity at each pattern:

| Pattern | Progression Options | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|---|
| **Squat** | Glute Bridge / BW Box Squat / BW Squat / KB Goblet Squat / Nexus Point Squat | 2 × 10–12RM |
| **Hinge** | Broomstick Hinge / Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift / Barbell Romanian Deadlift / Trap Bar Deadlift / Barbell Deadlift | 2 × 10–12RM |
| **Bench** | DB Bench Press / Barbell Bench Press | 2 × 10–12RM |

Record all reps and load for the app.

Only progress the hinge assignment when the member demonstrates the next exercise to PRIME standard. Otherwise consolidate the current assignment.

Progress beyond Barbell Romanian Deadlift only after the member passes the 35 kg, 12-rep, one-second-hold PRIME gate.

### Post-Session Tasks
- Confirm availability for Session 4
- Ask if they are interested in continuing 1:1 PT after intros
- Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with updates
- Send a post-session text with encouragement

---

## 8. Session 4 — Consolidation & Close

### Purpose

Retain the two-set structure from Session 3. Complete only the exercises the client is working on, consolidate the highest proficient assignment in each pattern, and complete 6 total working sets.

This is the final intro session — focus on consolidating their exercise assignments and closing for ongoing 1:1 PT.

### Preparation
- Send a reminder text 24 hours before
- Arrive 15 minutes early to review progress notes

### Check-In
- Acknowledge this is the final intro session (if not continuing PT)
- Ask:
  - *"Imagine you've been consistent with 3–4 sessions per week for 6 months — how would you feel?"*
  - *"What benefits do you think you'll experience?"*

### Movement Session

**RPE target:** 7–8
**Rest:** 2 minutes between sets

| Pattern | Progression Options | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|---|
| **Squat** | Glute Bridge / BW Box Squat / BW Squat / KB Goblet Squat / Nexus Point Squat | 2 × 10–12RM |
| **Hinge** | Broomstick Hinge / Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift / Barbell Romanian Deadlift / Trap Bar Deadlift / Barbell Deadlift | 2 × 10–12RM |
| **Bench** | DB Bench Press / Barbell Bench Press | 2 × 10–12RM |

Record all reps and load for the app.

Consolidate Barbell Romanian Deadlift unless the member has passed the 35 kg, 12-rep, one-second-hold PRIME gate.

### Post-Session Outcome

**After the session:**
- Let them know you'll check in monthly — follow the monthly check-in process
- Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with client status
- Send a final post-session text with encouragement

## 9. Roles & Responsibilities

**Trainer:**
- Provide a welcoming, supportive introduction
- Ensure clients understand how to book and track workouts in Trainerize
- Assess movement and assign training levels systematically
- Teach breathing and bracing from Session 1 through to Session 4
- Record all reps and load every session
- Monitor progress and encourage participation

**Client:**
- Attend all assigned intro sessions
- Use Trainerize to book classes and log progress
- Communicate any issues with exercises, soreness, or gym access

---

## 10. Safety Guidelines

- Always check for mobility limitations or injuries before progressing movements
- Emphasise technique over load — never progress an exercise when form breaks down
- Teach breathing and bracing mechanics from Session 1
- Ensure a clear workout space and proper equipment setup before each session

---

- Trainers assessed on member feedback, attendance rates, and SOP adherence
