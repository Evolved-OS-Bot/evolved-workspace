# SOP — Evolved Pilates Session

**Class:** Evolved Pilates Session
**Gym:** The Evolved All Female Gym
**Effective Date:** 20/01/2025
**Prepared By:** Peter & Megan Brown
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Purpose

Outline procedures for conducting Evolved Pilates sessions — improving core strength, flexibility, posture, and overall body awareness through low-impact exercises inspired by traditional Pilates principles.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all employees delivering Evolved Pilates sessions at The Evolved All Female Gym.

---

## 3. Objectives

- Enhance participants' core strength and stability
- Improve posture, flexibility, and muscular endurance
- Promote body awareness and mindful movement
- Provide a calming, supportive environment suitable for all fitness levels

---

## 4. Class Structure

- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Capacity:** 15 members

---

## 4.1 Pre-Session Checklist

**Cleanliness & Hygiene**
- Ensure all equipment is clean and sanitised
- Check floors, mirrors, and workout areas are free of dust, debris, and clutter

**Equipment Setup**
- Arrange equipment according to the session layout
- Confirm all necessary items are available and in good condition
- Test any machines or devices for functionality

**Environment**
- Adjust room temperature (fans as needed)
- Set up music/audio to appropriate volume and playlist
- **Leave main lights off — turn festoon lights on**
- **Move barbells from racks and lay flat on the floor against the back wall, clear of walkways**

**Safety**
- Inspect equipment for damage or wear — replace if necessary
- Confirm walkways and workout areas are clear of obstructions
- Ensure first aid kits and AEDs are accessible and stocked

---

## 4.2 Member Comfort & Experience

**Welcoming Atmosphere**
- Greet members warmly as they arrive and direct them to the workout area
- Provide an overview of the session and address any initial questions

**Accessibility**
- Set up to accommodate varying fitness levels — modifications readily available
- Arrange additional space or equipment for members requiring alternative setups

**Safety Communication**
- Brief members on equipment usage and safety protocols before the session
- Identify emergency exits and highlight the location of first aid kits

---

## 4.3 Warm-Up (5–10 minutes)

**Objective:** Prepare the body for Pilates exercises by focusing on breath, alignment, and gentle mobility.

- Use the prescribed warm-up exercises in Trainerize
- Within your scope, give alternatives where needed to address individual mobility needs
- If unsure of an appropriate alternative: deliver what is prescribed, apologise, and tell the member you'll ask management — then do so as soon as possible (provide client name and area/type of immobility)

---

## 4.4 Main Session (30–40 minutes)

**Focus areas:** Core stability, controlled movement, flexibility, and strength.

- Use the prescribed workout in Trainerize — adhere to sets, timing, and movement patterns outlined
- Within your scope, give progressions or regressions to individualise the workout to each member's needs
- If unsure of an appropriate progression or regression: deliver what is prescribed, apologise, and tell the member you'll ask management — then do so as soon as possible (provide client name and movement pattern requiring progression/regression)

**Equipment required:** Mats, resistance bands, small Pilates balls, light dumbbells (optional)

**Key points:**
- Emphasise controlled, precise movements
- Encourage participants to synchronise breath with movement
- Offer progressions for advanced participants and regressions for beginners

---

## 4.5 Cool-Down (5–10 minutes)

**Objective:** Promote relaxation, enhance flexibility, and transition participants out of the session.

- Using the iPad, access the YouTube video provided at the end of the Pilates workout and play as a guided breathwork exercise
- **Or:** study the YouTube video provided and deliver your own version using its principles

---

## 4.6 Post-Session Responsibilities

**Farewell**
- Acknowledge the effort — Pilates demands mental focus, not just physical
- Tell everyone what a great job they did
- Congratulate 2–3 members individually — identify 1 win or 1 improvement and offer a class recommendation or complimentary 1:1 PT session
- Ask when they're coming in next

**Clean-Up**
- Wipe down and sanitise all equipment used
- Return all items to their designated storage areas
- Check for forgotten personal items — store in lost and found
- Wash cool towels in bacterial wash, then roll and place back in the fridge

**Environment Reset**
- Restore the workout space to its original condition for the next session
- Inspect for damage or hazards — report to management if necessary
- Ensure the toilet is closed after the session

---

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities

**Instructor:**
- Arrive 15 minutes early to set up the studio and greet participants
- Check in with participants to identify any injuries or limitations
- Provide clear instructions, demonstrating proper technique and modifications
- Ensure a calm, supportive, and inclusive class environment throughout

**Participant:**
- Arrive on time wearing comfortable, form-fitting attire suitable for Pilates
- Inform the instructor of any injuries, discomfort, or physical concerns before class begins
- Follow instructions and practise mindful movement — focus on breath and alignment

---

## 6. Safety Guidelines

- Inspect all equipment (mats, resistance bands, etc.) for cleanliness and safety before class
- Monitor participants closely — provide verbal and visual cues to maintain proper form
- Encourage participants to move within their range of motion and avoid overexertion

---

## 7. Session Feedback

- Gather feedback from participants after the session to assess satisfaction and areas for improvement
- Record attendance in Trainerize
- Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with any issues encountered during the session

---

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Issue | Response |
|---|---|
| Participant discomfort | Offer modifications or alternative exercises |
| Equipment limitations | Use bodyweight exercises or substitute available equipment |
| Low energy | Incorporate motivational cues — ensure participants understand the benefits of each exercise |
| No clients show | To be paid, you must be present at the premises completing an approved alternative activity |

---

## 9. Performance Evaluation

- Session delivery assessed through participant feedback and management observation
- Instructors encouraged to attend workshops or training to refine Pilates techniques and delivery
