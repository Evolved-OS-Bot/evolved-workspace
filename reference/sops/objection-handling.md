# SOP — Objection Handling

**Service:** Strength Assessment — Close
**Gym:** The Evolved All Female Gym

---

## Guiding Principle

In sales, our job is to read between the lines and unblock people to allow them to take the actions **they** want to take. Not the actions we want them to take.

---

## Objection 1 — "I don't have the money"

**Step 1 — Test if price is the real objection:**

> *"That's ok [name]. I understand this is a big investment into yourself. Before you go, can I just ask — if price wasn't an issue, would this be a program you could see yourself using to achieve [goal]?"*

---

**If NO** — price isn't the real objection. Find it:

> *"Ok, no worries. What is it about the program that you feel won't help you achieve your goals?"*

Tackle the real objection (e.g. don't see the value, worried they won't follow through).

---

**If YES** — price is the only barrier. Shift from "I can't" to "how can I":

> *"Ok good. These programs are life-changing. What would have to happen inside your life for you to be able to afford to invest this kind of money into achieving [goal] — something you've been thinking about achieving for [timeframe]?"*

Then **stop talking.** Let them speak first. Do not fill the silence — you will disrupt their internal dialogue.

After they answer: using the information gathered in the consultation, confidently suggest redirecting funds away from what is keeping them from their goals and toward the program that will move them closer to them.

---

## Objection 3 — "I need to run this by my partner"

Work through this sequence — ask each question and wait for the answer before continuing:

> *"That's ok [name]. I understand — I'd totally want to know if my partner was about to spend this kind of money on themselves. Earlier you said your partner was supportive. Did they know you'd be coming here today?"*

*(Yes or No)*

> *"And — aside from price — would this be a program they would support you in doing?"*

*(Yes or No)*

> *"And do you see yourself being able to use this program and achieve [goal]?"*

*(Yes or No)*

> *"Ok great. Listen — we have a 48-hour cooling-off period. Would you feel comfortable committing today before you leave? I see hundreds of women just like you every year, and the ones who leave without committing to themselves usually come back 6 or 12 months later worse off than when they first came in.*
>
> *If you go home and get no support from your partner, we'll return your deposit and you won't have lost anything. If you get their support, you'll avoid your own procrastination and be able to dive straight into achieving this goal."*

---

**If YES** — set them up and take their first weekly payment.

**If NO:**

> *"No worries at all. It's my job to help get you started — thank you for allowing me to help with that. Can I give you a call at this time tomorrow to check in and see if you got that support?"*

When you get your first no, remain supportive and positive.

---

## Trainer Notes on the Partner Objection

In most cases, partners are supportive — they want their loved one to feel healthy, strong, and confident because it benefits them too.

Women typically raise this objection when either:
- They genuinely aren't sure if they have their partner's support, **or**
- They don't want to look like they're signing up for another gym they won't use

If this comes up, highlight the parts of the program and approach that help members stay accountable and follow through. Use member stories where relevant to help them connect with other women who felt the same way.
