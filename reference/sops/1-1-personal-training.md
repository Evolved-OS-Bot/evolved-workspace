## 1. Purpose

Outline procedures for conducting 1:1 Personal Training sessions — providing customised fitness programs tailored to the individual goals, needs, and abilities of each member in a safe, supportive environment.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all personal trainers delivering 1:1 sessions at The Evolved All Female Gym.

---

## 3. Objectives

- Provide personalised training to help members achieve their fitness and health goals
- Educate members on proper exercise techniques and safety protocols
- Build rapport and trust to support long-term fitness journeys

---

## 4. Session Structure

- **Duration:** 30–60 minutes (depending on the client's program)

---

## 4.0 Key Performance Habits for PT Clients

This sets the expectation of results — clients must understand what they need to do to achieve them.

### Priority 1 — Training Intensity

Women engaged in PT only or on packages like Fast Track must lift with adequate intensity **2–3x per week** for optimal results.

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Rep range | 6–12RM |
| Intensity | 70–85% of 1RM |
| Total volume | 6–8 sets per session |

### Priority 2 — Movement Pattern Coverage

Sessions must target the **6 foundational movement patterns** at least once per week:

**Squat · Hinge · Lunge · Push · Pull · Carry**

Applying the intensity above to these movement patterns ensures long-term results in strength, body composition, and functional health.

---

## 4.1 Trainer Implementation Logic

Use the correct logic based on the client's membership structure:

**Logic A — PT Package + SGPT Membership**
- The 1:1 session must meet intensity and movement pattern criteria — unless the client has already attended all 3 Sculpt & Strength sessions for the week
- If they haven't attended all 3: use a Sculpt & Strength workout from their training program (Kettlebell icon in Trainerize) as the 1:1 session workout

**Logic B — 2+ PT Sessions Per Week, No SGPT**
- Base sessions on the Evolved All Female Gym 1:1 Periodised Program
- Meet intensity and movement pattern benchmarks each session

**Logic C — 1 PT Session Per Week, No SGPT**
- Follow the 1:1 Periodised Program
- Still hit intensity and movement pattern benchmarks

**Logic D — Client Requests Cardio, HIIT, or Alternate Focus**
- Trainer may adapt the session only after educating the client:
  - Our expertise is in strength-based transformation
  - Long-term health, hormonal balance, and ideal body composition are built on the strength training principles outlined above
  - It is our duty to inform women of the minimum key performance habits needed for longevity and independent living in their final decade — these habits also make them look, feel, and function better in the short term

---

## 4.2 Pre-Session Preparation

- Review the client's goals, progress, and previous session notes in Trainerize
- Periodise a **minimum 12-week program** for the client, broken down against their goals
- Prepare necessary equipment and plan the session layout
- Greet the client promptly and perform a quick verbal check-in — assess current energy levels, mood, and any injuries or concerns

---

## 4.3 Warm-Up (5–10 minutes)

**Objective:** Prepare the body for the workout, reducing the risk of injury.

- Include dynamic stretches, mobility work, activation, and/or light cardio tailored to the client's program
- Engage key muscle groups and assess movement patterns during the warm-up

---

## 4.4 Main Session (30–60 minutes)

**Objective:** Deliver a customised workout in Trainerize based on the client's goals, fitness level, and progress. Record all training sessions at the time of completion.

**Components (as applicable):**
- **Strength training:** Compound and isolation exercises (e.g. squats, deadlifts, bicep curls)
- **Cardio or conditioning:** High or moderate-intensity cardio (e.g. treadmill intervals, kettlebell swings)
- **Mobility and core work:** Functional movement and stability (e.g. planks, hip bridges)

**Key points:**
- Demonstrate each exercise with proper form before the client begins
- Provide real-time feedback and cues to ensure safety and effectiveness
- Modify exercises as needed based on the client's capabilities and response

---

## 4.5 Cool-Down (5–10 minutes)

**Objective:** Support recovery, reduce soreness, and enhance flexibility.

- Include static stretches and breathing exercises
- Recap session highlights and discuss next steps or adjustments for future sessions

---

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities

**Personal Trainer:**
- Arrive prepared, on time, and dressed professionally
- Create a safe and engaging training environment
- Track client progress (training data, biometrics, photos) — note achievements and areas for improvement
- Educate clients on proper form, technique, and the importance of consistency
- Maintain confidentiality and respect the client's personal boundaries

**Client:**
- Arrive on time, ready for the session
- Communicate openly about goals, limitations, or concerns
- Follow instructions and actively participate
- Adhere to gym policies and respect the trainer's guidance

---

## 6. Safety Guidelines

- Perform a pre-session assessment to identify potential risks or limitations
- Monitor the client's form and intensity throughout
- Have a first aid kit accessible and be familiar with gym emergency protocols
- Ensure all equipment is in good working condition and safely positioned

---

## 7. Session Feedback

- Provide constructive feedback and celebrate progress at the end of each session
- Encourage clients to share feedback to refine future programs

---

## 8. Troubleshooting

### Client Issues

| Issue | Response |
|---|---|
| Fatigue or discomfort | Adjust intensity or modify exercises as needed |
| Equipment unavailable | Have alternative exercises prepared |
| Client plateau | Reassess the program and introduce new challenges |
| Client cancels with 24hrs+ notice | Provide a make-up session or credit on account |
| Client needs to reschedule | Redirect to **0483 968 880** |
| No-show or cancellation within 24hrs | Trainer must remain on premises for the full allotted time, completing approved alternative activities. Text **0402 840 247** to notify of no-show and confirm what you'll be completing. |

### Approved Alternative Activities (No-Show)

- Vacuum — pull equipment away from wall and vacuum
- Mop gym floor — under equipment and against walls
- Wipe down all equipment — benches, barbells, dumbbells, racks (remove all chalk)
- Wipe down microwave inside and out
- Wipe down all shelving — move items and wipe under them
- Ensure dumbbells are in order and in their correct spots
- Check liquid chalk and refill
- Check towels are moist, clean, and cold — clean and freshen any used towels
- Water plants
- Tidy storeroom and storage areas
- Ensure equipment is set up for the next class
- If a class is on, step in as a second trainer
- Update Instagram story — tag @the_evolved_allfemalegym with detail and education

### Trainer Cancellation / Rescheduling

- **Notice required:** 24 hours minimum
- **Procedure:** Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au, then text Peter on **0420 863 721** to confirm receipt
- The client's time slot will be prioritised by finding a replacement trainer first
- If no replacement is available, the session is rescheduled with the original trainer within that same week (subject to availability)
