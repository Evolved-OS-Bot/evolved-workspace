# SOP: Post-Sale Member Onboarding

**System:** GHL, Stripe and Trainerize  
**Process Owner:** Assigned consultant  
**Quality Control:** Admin Eve  
**Status:** Live  
**Version:** 1.1  
**Last Updated:** 25/07/2026

---

## Purpose

Complete every new member's post-sale setup accurately before she leaves the consultation. This procedure converts the existing Strength Assessment Trainerize account into the correct membership experience without creating a duplicate client or relying on Admin to finish the sale.

This SOP supersedes historical onboarding checklists that reference ACR, PT Minder, Bronze, Silver, Gold or Limited as current operating instructions.

---

## Scope

Use this SOP immediately after a Strength Assessment prospect accepts Fit & Flexible, Strong, Fit & Flexible Membership or Fast Track and completes the membership checkout.

This SOP covers the initial membership handoff only. Later upgrades, downgrades, holds, cancellations and renewals require their approved service-change procedures.

---

## Completion Gate

Do not begin membership fulfilment until all three items are confirmed:

1. The membership agreement is signed.
2. The upfront payment is successful.
3. The membership start date is recorded in GHL.

If any item is missing, stop. Do not assign a membership product or represent the setup as complete.

---

## Role Ownership

| Role | Responsibility | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Assigned consultant | Complete the full member setup during the post-sale consultation | Before the member leaves |
| Assigned consultant | Complete the GHL fulfilment task and record the setup note | Same appointment; next-day task is fallback only |
| Admin Eve | Independently verify the completed setup and resolve or assign any exception | By day two |

The assigned consultant is the GHL contact's Assigned User at the point of sale. The process must not default every fulfilment task to Admin.

## Procedure

### 1. Confirm the Sale Record

In GHL, confirm the signed agreement, successful upfront payment, exact membership package, recorded membership start date and Assigned User.

Check that the regular weekly debit amount matches the selected package before continuing.

### 2. Reuse the Existing Trainerize Account

Search Trainerize using the assessment client's exact email address. Open the account that was created through the Initial Consultation product.

Do not create a second client, use name-only matching or send a second invitation. If there is no exact account or more than one possible account, stop and assign an Admin Eve exception.

### 3. Assign the Membership Main Product

Assign the exact Main Product from the package matrix. For a membership beginning today, use the day-of-purchase start.

For an explicitly future-dated membership, use the membership start date recorded in GHL. Product Starts applies the access, Owner, location, group and program settings when the product begins.

### 4. Verify the Product Starts Result

Confirm Full Access with 1-way messaging, Owner `Evolved All Female Gym` and location `The Evolved Gym`.

For Strong and Fast Track, confirm The Evolved All Stars and the correct membership program. For Fit & Flexible, confirm there is no All Stars group and no membership program.

### 5. Add Timetable Booking Access

Apply the separate Class Access Add-on Products shown in the package matrix. Confirm the included timetable types show 999 paid credits with `Do not Expire`.

Fit & Flexible receives Cardio and Pilates only. Strong and Fast Track also receive Strength.

### 6. Complete Package-Specific Provisions

For Strong and Fast Track, set up the Smart Meal Plan. Fit & Flexible does not receive a Smart Meal Plan.

Do not create Trainerize personal-training credits for KickStart, onboarding or weekly Fast Track PT.

### 7. Book the First Coaching Appointment

Fit & Flexible requires no onboarding appointment. Strong requires one KickStart session booked within 2 to 3 days.

Fast Track requires session one of the four-session onboarding pathway within 2 to 3 days. Confirm the ongoing weekly 30-minute PT schedule with the member and follow the approved booking process.

### 8. Confirm the Member Experience

Ask the member to open Trainerize and confirm she can sign in. Show her where to find the timetable and how to book the class types included in her membership.

For Strong and Fast Track, confirm the correct program appears on the correct calendar days. A future-dated product may not show its program before its recorded start date.

### 9. Close the GHL Handoff

Complete the assigned GHL fulfilment task. Record a concise note containing the Trainerize account matched, Main Product, start date, Class Access Add-ons, Smart Meal Plan status and first-session booking.

Do not record card details or other unnecessary payment information in the note.

---

## Admin Eve Quality-Control Check

By day two, independently confirm:

- the GHL contact and Trainerize account use the same exact email;
- only one Trainerize account exists for the member;
- the Main Product and start date match the signed package;
- Full Access, 1-way messaging, Owner and location are correct;
- Class Access Add-ons and 999 non-expiring credits match the package;
- All Stars and the membership program are correct;
- Smart Meal Plan is present for Strong and Fast Track only;
- the required first coaching appointment is booked; and
- the consultant's GHL task and setup note are complete.

If any item is wrong, correct it only when the intended state is unambiguous. Otherwise assign the exception to the responsible owner and record what is blocking completion.

---

## Exceptions and Stop Rules

### Existing Main Product

Trainerize membership products use `After current`. During an upgrade, downgrade or return, another active Main Product can delay the new product.

Do not assume the replacement is active. Verify the current Main Product, the approved service-change date and the resulting start date before changing or cancelling anything.

### Future Start

Use the exact GHL membership start date. A missing program before that date is not a failure if the product is correctly pending.

### Fifty-Two-Week Product End

Automatic Membership Control is off and Product Ends contains no deactivation action. Do not deactivate a paying member because the Trainerize product reaches 52 weeks.

Renewal reconciliation is a separate control. GHL remains the lifecycle source, while Stripe is the default payment source and approved legacy PTMinder/EziDebit receipts may evidence legacy payment exceptions.

### Cancellation

Removing a Class Access Add-on does not revoke its 999-credit balance. Deactivation on the verified final service date is the hard stop for app and timetable access.

Follow the cancellation procedure rather than using this SOP to end access.

---

## Definition of Done

The member has one correctly matched Trainerize account, the right Main Product and start date, the right booking access, the right program and group, all included provisions and the first required coaching appointment.

The consultant has completed the GHL task and note, and Admin Eve has completed the independent quality-control check by day two.
