# SOP — Hybrid Fitness Racing

**Class:** Hybrid Fitness Racing
**Gym:** The Evolved All Female Gym
**Effective Date:** 20/01/2025
**Prepared By:** Peter & Megan Brown
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Purpose

Outline procedures for conducting Hybrid Fitness Racing sessions — combining cardio, strength, and endurance elements to simulate fitness racing dynamics and improve overall fitness.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all employees delivering Hybrid Fitness Racing sessions at The Evolved All Female Gym.

---

## 3. Objectives

- Improve members' overall fitness through a balanced mix of cardio, strength, and endurance challenges
- Build confidence in participants for competitive fitness racing or obstacle courses
- Provide an exciting and motivating environment for all fitness levels

---

## 4. Class Structure

- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Capacity:** 15 members

---

## 4.1 Pre-Session Checklist

**Cleanliness & Hygiene**
- Ensure all equipment is clean and sanitised
- Check floors, mirrors, and workout areas are free of dust, debris, and clutter

**Equipment Setup**
- Arrange equipment according to the session layout
- Confirm all necessary items are available and in good condition
- Test machines (e.g. rowing machines, treadmills) for functionality

**Environment**
- Adjust room temperature (fans as needed)
- Set up music/audio to appropriate volume and playlist
- Ensure adequate lighting — no glare or overly dim conditions
- Turn on party lights and set to beat with the music

**Safety**
- Inspect equipment for damage or wear — replace if necessary
- Confirm walkways and workout areas are clear of obstructions
- Ensure first aid kits and AEDs are accessible and stocked

---

## 4.2 Member Comfort & Experience

**Welcoming Atmosphere**
- Greet members warmly as they arrive and direct them to the workout area
- Provide an overview of the session and address any initial questions

**Accessibility**
- Set up to accommodate varying fitness levels — modifications readily available
- Arrange additional space or equipment for members requiring alternative setups

**Safety Communication**
- Brief members on equipment usage and safety protocols before the session
- Identify emergency exits and highlight the location of first aid kits

---

## 4.3 Warm-Up (5–10 minutes)

**Objective:** Prepare the body for high-intensity movements and reduce the risk of injury.

- Use the prescribed warm-up exercises in Trainerize
- Within your scope, give alternatives where needed to address individual mobility needs
- If unsure of an appropriate alternative: deliver what is prescribed, apologise, and tell the member you'll ask management — then do so as soon as possible (provide client name and area/type of immobility)

---

## 4.4 Main Session (30–40 minutes)

**Format:** Circuit-style or race-style layout with stations alternating between cardio bursts, strength exercises, and endurance challenges.

- Use the prescribed workout in Trainerize — adhere to sets, timing, and movement patterns outlined
- Within your scope, give progressions or regressions to individualise the workout to each member's needs
- If unsure of an appropriate progression or regression: deliver what is prescribed, apologise, and tell the member you'll ask management — then do so as soon as possible (provide client name and movement pattern requiring progression/regression)

**Customer service:**
- Provide cool towels to members


**Format:**
- Participants complete stations sequentially or in teams, depending on the session format

**Equipment required:** Rowers, assault bikes, ski erg, dumbbells, kettlebells, plyometric boxes, pull-up bars, cones

**Progressions & regressions:**
- Beginners: modify weights or range of motion
- Advanced: add time-based challenges or heavier loads

---

## 4.5 Cool-Down (5–10 minutes)

**Objective:** Gradually lower heart rate and enhance recovery.

- Use the prescribed cool-down provided in Trainerize

---

## 4.6 Post-Session Responsibilities

**Farewell**
- Tell everyone what a great job they did
- Congratulate 2–3 members individually — identify 1 win or 1 improvement and offer a class recommendation or complimentary 1:1 PT session
- Ask when they're coming in next

**Clean-Up**
- Wipe down and sanitise all equipment used
- Return all items to their designated storage areas
- Check for forgotten personal items — store in lost and found
- Wash cool towels in bacterial wash, then roll and place back in the fridge

**Environment Reset**
- Restore the workout space to its original condition for the next session
- Inspect for damage or hazards — report to management if necessary
- Ensure the toilet is closed after the session

---

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities

**Instructor:**
- Arrive 10–15 minutes early to set up stations and ensure equipment is in working order
- Conduct a pre-class briefing — explain the layout and objectives of the session
- Demonstrate proper technique for each station, including modifications
- Actively monitor participants for safety and motivation throughout

**Participant:**
- Arrive on time, ready to participate in a high-intensity session
- Wear appropriate footwear and attire
- Inform the instructor of any injuries or limitations before the session begins

---

## 6. Safety Guidelines

- Inspect all equipment before use to ensure it is safe and functional
- Clear the workout area of unnecessary hazards
- Monitor participants for signs of overexertion or improper form
- Encourage hydration and rest as needed during the session

---

## 7. Session Feedback

- Gather feedback from participants after the session to assess satisfaction and areas for improvement
- Record attendance in Trainerize
- Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with any issues encountered during the session

---

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Issue | Response |
|---|---|
| Overcrowded stations | Adjust timing or increase the number of stations to balance participants |
| Fatigue or overexertion | Provide active recovery options (e.g. walking, light stretching) between rounds |
| Equipment problems | Have alternative exercises ready to replace equipment-dependent stations |
| No clients show | To be paid, you must be present at the premises completing an approved alternative activity |

---

## 9. Performance Evaluation

- Session delivery evaluated through participant feedback and management observation
- Instructors encouraged to attend workshops or certifications in fitness racing or functional training
