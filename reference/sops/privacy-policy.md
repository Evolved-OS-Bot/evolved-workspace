## Overview

At The Evolved All Female Gym, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information in compliance with the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

---

## 1. Collection of Personal Information

We may collect the following types of personal information:

- **Identity:** Name, date of birth, gender
- **Contact:** Email address, phone number, residential address
- **Health:** Medical history, fitness goals, injuries, or conditions relevant to training (collected with consent)
- **Payment:** Billing details, payment history, receipts
- **Usage:** Gym membership details, attendance, and participation in classes or events

---

## 2. How We Collect Personal Information

- Membership registration forms
- Health and fitness questionnaires
- Online sign-ups via our website and apps
- Email, phone, or in-person communication
- Payment and booking systems

---

## 3. Purpose of Collecting Personal Information

- To provide fitness and wellness services tailored to member needs
- To ensure the safety and appropriateness of exercise programs
- To manage memberships, bookings, and payment processing
- To communicate with members about services, updates, and promotions
- To comply with legal and regulatory requirements

---

## 4. Use and Disclosure of Personal Information

Personal information is used and disclosed only for purposes directly related to membership and our services. Information may be shared with:

- Staff to deliver personalised training and programs
- Third-party service providers (e.g. payment processors, booking systems) under strict confidentiality agreements
- Legal authorities if required by law or to prevent harm

**We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.**

---

## 5. Security of Personal Information

Reasonable steps are taken to protect personal information from unauthorised access, alteration, or misuse:

- Secure storage systems for physical and electronic records
- Restricted access to personal data for authorised personnel only
- Encryption of sensitive information during online transactions

---

## 6. Accessing and Correcting Personal Information

Members have the right to:

- Request access to the personal information we hold about them
- Request corrections to any inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated information

To make a request, contact us using the details below. Identity verification may be required before fulfilling a request.

---

## 7. Retention of Personal Information

Personal information is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. After this period, information will be securely destroyed or de-identified.

---

## 8. Marketing and Communication

- We may use contact information to send updates, promotions, and news about The Evolved All Female Gym
- Members can opt out of marketing communications at any time via the unsubscribe link in emails or by contacting us directly

---

## 9. Use of Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve browsing experience. Cookies are small data files stored on your device. Cookie settings can be managed through your browser.

---

## 10. Overseas Disclosure

We do not typically disclose personal information to entities outside of Australia. If this becomes necessary, compliance with the APPs will be ensured.

---

## 11. Complaints and Queries

If you have concerns about how we handle personal information, contact us using the details below. We will respond promptly and aim to resolve issues within **30 days**.

If not satisfied with our response, you may contact the **Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)**.
