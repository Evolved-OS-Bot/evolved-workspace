## 1. Emergency Procedures

### 1.1 Purpose

To provide clear guidelines for responding to emergencies and protecting the safety and well-being of all members, staff, and visitors.

### 1.2 Emergency Scenarios and Responses

**Fire**
- Call emergency services immediately — **000**
- The fire extinguisher is located near the front entrance — use only on small, contained fires
- Do not attempt to extinguish large fires — focus on safe evacuation
- Guide members to the nearest evacuation exit (see Section 3)

**Medical Emergency**
- Provide first aid as outlined in Section 4
- Call emergency services (**000**) if the situation is critical
- Use the AED if the person is unresponsive and not breathing normally — located at **[confirm AED location]**
- Inform management immediately:
  - **Peter:** 0420 863 721
  - **Megan:** 0402 840 247

**Power Outage**
- Ensure members remain calm
- Suspend all equipment use until power is restored and safety checks are completed

**Severe Weather or Natural Disaster**
- Move members to a designated safe area inside the gym
- Follow local authority instructions

## 2. Incident Reports

### 2.1 Purpose

To document and address incidents to improve safety and operational protocols.

### 2.2 When to Complete an Incident Report

- Injuries to members, staff, or visitors
- Equipment malfunction causing harm or potential harm
- Disruptive behaviour or security issues

### 2.3 Incident Report Procedure

1. Complete the Incident Report Form immediately after the incident
2. Submit the completed form to management within **24 hours**
3. Management will review and implement necessary follow-up actions

## 3. Evacuation Plans

### 3.1 Purpose

To ensure safe and orderly evacuation during emergencies.

### 3.2 Evacuation Routes

- **Primary exit:** Front entrance through the roller door

### 3.3 Evacuation Procedure

1. Alert all members and staff to evacuate immediately
2. Direct individuals to the nearest safe exit
3. Assemble at the designated evacuation point: **street level car park**
4. Conduct a headcount to ensure all individuals are accounted for
5. Inform emergency services upon their arrival

### 3.4 Post-Evacuation Procedure

- Do not re-enter the building until clearance is given by emergency services
- Conduct a debrief to review the evacuation process and identify improvement areas

## 4. First Aid Protocols

### 4.1 Purpose

To provide immediate care for injuries or medical emergencies until professional help arrives.

### 4.2 Equipment Locations

| Equipment | Location |
|---|---|
| First Aid Kit | Bag storage area |
| AED (Automated External Defibrillator) | [confirm location] |
| Fire Extinguisher | Near front entrance |

### 4.3 First Aid Procedure

**Assess the situation first:**
- Ensure the area is safe before approaching the injured person
- Identify the nature of the injury or condition

**Minor injuries** (cuts, bruises):
- Clean the wound, apply a bandage, and monitor

**Severe injuries** (fractures, heavy bleeding):
- Call **000**
- Apply pressure to stop bleeding while waiting for emergency services

**Medical emergencies** (heart attack, unconsciousness, cardiac arrest):
- Call **000** immediately
- Begin CPR if trained and necessary
- Retrieve and use the **AED** — follow the device's voice prompts

**After the situation is under control:**
- Complete an Incident Report Form

### 4.4 First Aid Training Requirements

- All staff must hold current **First Aid** and **CPR** certifications
- **CPR renewal:** every 12 months
- **First Aid renewal:** every 3 years
- Provide copies of current certifications to management upon renewal
