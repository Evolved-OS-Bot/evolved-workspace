# SOP: First-Week Member Reply Follow-Up

**Gym:** The Evolved All Female Gym
**Process owner:** Retention Manager
**System:** GHL workflow `Membership: First 7 Days`
**Access:** Retention Manager and approved management cover only

---

## Purpose

Make sure every new member who replies to a Day 7, Day 8 or Day 9 check-in receives a clear, personal follow-up.

The process separates the written response and system administration from the personal relationship follow-up. This keeps ownership clear and prevents a positive or concerning reply from being missed.

---

## Role Ownership

### Admin Eve

- Reads the member's reply.
- Sends or coordinates the written response.
- Adds a clear internal note for the Retention Manager.
- Keeps the member in the correct review pathway.
- Resolves an unclear reply before handing off a definite positive or negative follow-up.

### Retention Manager

- Reads the member's reply and Admin Eve's note.
- Completes the personal follow-up.
- Records the follow-up and outcome in GHL.
- Completes the GHL task only after the record is updated.

The Retention Manager does not interpret or change the review workflow. If the handoff is unclear, leave the task open and ask Admin Eve or management to clarify it.

---

## When This SOP Applies

Use this SOP when GHL creates a Retention Manager task from a member reply to the Day 7, Day 8 or Day 9 check-in in `Membership: First 7 Days`.

The task should identify whether the follow-up is positive or negative. Do not act from an unclear classification without a clear Admin Eve note.

---

## Positive Reply Follow-Up

1. Read the member's reply and Admin Eve's note.
2. Call the member as soon as possible. Thank her, acknowledge what she shared and ask how her first week has felt.
3. Check whether she has left a Google review. If she has not, answer any questions without pressuring her.
4. If the moment feels natural, say: "We have just had a spot open up for a new member at The Evolved, and I thought you might have a friend or woman in your life who would benefit from getting stronger too."
5. If she has someone in mind, ask for a warm introduction or confirm that the referred woman has agreed to be contacted.
6. Record the call, review status, referral outcome and any next action in GHL.
7. Contact the referred woman promptly once a warm introduction or explicit agreement to be contacted is in place.
8. Complete the task only after the note and any agreed referral follow-up are recorded.

Keep the follow-up warm and specific. The referral invitation is a natural extension of a positive conversation, not a script that must be forced into every call.

Do not record or use a referred woman's contact details merely because the member knows her. A warm introduction or explicit agreement to be contacted is required first.

---

## Negative Reply Follow-Up

1. Read the member's reply and Admin Eve's note.
2. Call the member as soon as possible. Listen carefully, make sure she feels heard and find out what support would genuinely help.
3. Address anything within the Retention Manager's role.
4. If the member cannot be reached, leave a message and continue trying until personal contact is made or the owners agree on the next step.
5. Record the call, outcome and any escalation as an internal note on the member's GHL contact record.
6. Escalate findings promptly to the owners when the issue cannot be remedied without owner intervention.
7. Keep the task open until every escalation has a clear owner and next action.
8. Complete the task only after the GHL note and any required escalation are recorded.

Do not promise a refund, billing change, membership change or other management decision.

---

## Unclear Reply Handoff

Admin Eve reads what the member has shared and decides what she needs next. If things are going well, Admin Eve leaves the Retention Manager a clear note for an encouraging follow-up.

If something is not going well, Admin Eve removes the member from the Google review pathway and asks the Retention Manager to call her promptly. The note must explain what the member said and what needs to happen next.

If Admin Eve is genuinely unsure, the task stays open while management is asked for help. If the Retention Manager receives a handoff that is still unclear, leave it open and request clarification.

---

## Recording Standard

Open the member's GHL contact record, select **Notes** and add an internal note. Do not rely on a task comment or conversation message as the operating record.

Record:

- Date and time of the follow-up.
- Channel: in person, phone call or personal message.
- Whether the member was reached or a message was left.
- What the member was happy about, unhappy about or concerned about.
- What support or solution was discussed.
- The agreed outcome.
- For a positive reply, whether the member has left a review.
- Whether a referral was discussed and the referral outcome.
- The referred woman's name and contact details only when a warm introduction or explicit contact consent is confirmed.
- How consent was confirmed.
- Any next action, its owner and when it is due.
- Any escalation made and the owner it was escalated to.

The task title or completion tick is not enough evidence on its own.

---

## Completion Standard

Complete the GHL task only when:

- The personal follow-up has happened.
- The outcome is recorded on the member's GHL contact record.
- Any required escalation or next action has an owner.

For a positive reply, the task remains incomplete until the member has been called and the outcome is recorded. Any agreed referral follow-up must also have an owner and due date.

For a negative reply, the task remains incomplete until the member has been called and reached, or the owners have agreed on the next step after unsuccessful contact attempts.

---

## GHL Task Timing

All Day 7, Day 8 and Day 9 reply tasks are due in one day at 5:00 pm.

Keep `Skip weekends` enabled.

---

## Absence and Cover

If the assigned Retention Manager is away or cannot complete the follow-up within the required time, management assigns the task to approved cover.

Do not reassign the task to a general trainer who has not been given access to this process.
