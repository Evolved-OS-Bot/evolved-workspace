# Course 9: Intro Sessions

**GHL Title:** Intro Sessions
**Description:** Intro Sessions are the first experience a new member has with their trainer at The Evolved. This course covers all 4 sessions: how to prepare, what to deliver, and how to set members up for long-term success.
**Pass mark:** 80%
**Prerequisite:** Course 7: 1:1 Personal Training

---

## Lessons

### Lesson 1: Overview

Intro Sessions are a structured onboarding and movement assessment process, not a training session.

**Session allocation by membership:**
- **Fit & Flexible members**: no Intro Session
- **Strong, Fit & Flexible Membership members**: 1 Intro Session
- **Fast Track members**: 4 Intro Sessions

Each session is 30 minutes and progresses in complexity. Your job across these sessions is to:
- Assign the member's exercise level for Squat, Deadlift, and Bench Press
- Set up their Trainerize app and establish goals
- Build rapport and confidence
- Record a baseline (reps, load, biometrics) for the app to aggregate
- Plant the seed for ongoing 1:1 PT

Use the end of Session 1 to upsell 1:1 PT: explain that additional sessions allow you to continue this assessment and progression process together.

### Lesson 2: Level 1 Habits

Level 1 Habits are assigned in Session 1 alongside the Trainerize app setup. They come from The Evolved's 28-day Holistic Body Transformation Program for Women and represent the foundational lifestyle habits that support training results: primarily sleep quality, recovery, and nutrition.

**Assign 1–2 habits per member. Do not overwhelm them.**

**How to select:**

Identify the member's gap in a brief conversation during app setup:
- Do they lack energy? → Start with sleep habits
- Do they struggle with what to eat? → Start with the cookbook
- Both? → Buffer Before Bed + Eat From Our Cookbook (the default for most women)

**The Level 1 Habits:**

| # | Habit | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Place a Buffer Before Bed**: 15 minutes screen-free before sleep (read, podcast, breathwork, conversation, meditation) | 6 out of 7 nights |
| 2 | **Eat From Our Cookbook**: use the recipes 80% of the time including snacks and drinks (aside from water) | 80% of meals/snacks |
| 3.1 | **Fall Asleep Before 12am**: maximise sleep during golden hours (10pm–12am) | 6 out of 7 nights |
| 3.2 | **Fall Asleep Before 11pm**: golden hours 9pm–12am | 6 out of 7 nights |
| 3.3 | **Fall Asleep Before 10pm**: golden hours 9pm–12am | 6 out of 7 nights |
| 3.4 | **Fall Asleep Before 9pm**: golden hours 9pm–12am | 6 out of 7 nights |
| 4 | **Replace High-Energy Liquids**: swap calorie-containing drinks for zero-calorie options (water, black coffee, no-sugar cordial, etc.) | Ongoing |
| 5 | **Do the Bedroom Makeover**: add or remove one item from the sleep environment (blackout curtains, remove phone, etc.) | One-time action |
| 6 | **Eat a High-Protein Dessert Before Bed**: meets sweet cravings while providing building blocks for overnight repair | Daily |
| 7 | **Your Repair Ritual**: combines Habits 1, 6, bedtime target, and screen-free sleeping space into a single daily habit | 6 out of 7 nights |

**Selection guidance:**

- **Most members → start with Habits 1 and 2.** Buffer Before Bed is the easiest entry point: nearly everyone can do it and the sleep quality improvement is felt quickly. The cookbook removes decision fatigue around food.
- **Member going to sleep late → pair Habit 1 with the appropriate 3.x.** Choose 3.1, 3.2, 3.3, or 3.4 based on what time they currently go to sleep: set a target that is achievable, not aspirational.
- **Member already sleeping well but eating poorly → Habits 2 and 4.**
- **Habit 7 (Repair Ritual) is not a starting habit**: it is the consolidation of the habits a member has already built over weeks. Do not assign it in Session 1.

**How to assign in Trainerize:** Find the habits section in the member's program and activate the selected habits. Walk through what each one means and confirm the member understands the standard before they leave.

### Lesson 3: Session 1

**Purpose:** Assign the appropriate exercise for each of the three main movement patterns: Squat, Deadlift, and Bench Press.

This session moves quickly. **Avoid failure: work to a 7 RPE.** The goal is to find the exercise in each pattern where the client demonstrates control and can reach a working load.

**Before they arrive:**
- Send a friendly introduction text when you receive the calendar invite
- Send a reminder text the morning of the session
- Arrive 15 minutes early: review their details

**When they walk in:**
- Greet them warmly and introduce yourself
- Explain what Intro Sessions are for: *"We're here to find your starting point, set up your apps, and make sure you feel confident before your first class."*
- Check they've received their Welcome Pack: send the link if not

**App Setup:**
- Trainerize: help them download the app and walk through workouts and progress tracking
- **Schedule their sessions:** Book their group classes and upcoming PT sessions before they leave. This is your responsibility, not theirs.
- The member is paying for accountability, so do not hand her the phone and ask her to choose alone. Review her availability, agree on a weekly schedule and book it with her.
- Create 1–3 goals in Trainerize
- Assign 1–2 Level 1 Habits aligned to their main goal (see Lesson 2)

**Baseline measurements:**

Explain that consistent measurements give the member an objective way to see progress. Record every measurement in centimetres to the nearest 0.5 cm.

Ask permission before every individual measurement: for example, *"Is it okay if I measure your waist now?"* A previous yes is not blanket permission for the remaining measurements.

Stand slightly to the client's left when taking front-facing measurements. This protects personal space and avoids standing directly in each other's breathing path.

Use a pen mark or a quick fingertip press that leaves a temporary white spot when a midpoint needs to remain visible. Keep the tape even around the body at every site.

Take the measurements in this order:

1. **Shoulders:** The client stands front-facing while you stand slightly to her left. Wrap the whole tape around the body at the widest part of the shoulders.
2. **Right bicep:** The client stands side-facing with her right arm bent. Identify the acromion, the bony point at the top and outside of the shoulder; measure from there to the elbow, halve that distance, mark the midpoint of the humerus and wrap the tape evenly around the arm at that point.
3. **Waist:** The client stands front-facing while you stand slightly to her left. Ask her to point to her belly button, then wrap the tape evenly around the waist at that level.
4. **Hips:** The client stands side-facing. Wrap the tape evenly around the thickest part of the glutes.
5. **Right quad:** The client stands side-facing. Measure from the front bony point of the iliac crest to the middle of the knee, halve that distance, mark the midpoint of the femur and wrap the tape evenly around the thigh at that point.
6. **Right calf:** The client stands side-facing. Wrap the tape evenly around the thickest part of the lower leg.

**Progress photos:**

Ask separately for permission to take progress photos. If the client declines, accept the answer and move on without pressure.

Invite her to remove loose outer layers and remain in fitted training clothes. Never pressure her to remove clothing or expose more than she is comfortable with.

Use the phone camera rather than Trainerize's in-app camera. Take one front-facing, one side-facing and one rear-facing photo as the client turns between positions.

Frame the full body with an even gap above the head and below the soles of the feet. Keep the distance, camera height, lighting and framing as consistent as practical for future comparisons.

Upload the photos and enter all six measurements in the client's Trainerize account. Never share before or after photos unless the client has given explicit permission for that separate use.

**Movement Assessment:**

Open the Onboarding Session Workout in Trainerize. Work through the squat, hinge, and bench patterns in sequence.

**Progression rules:**
- Apply PRIME to every rep: Posture, Range, Identify, Measure, then Evaluate & Evolve
- Advance only when the client completes 10 pain-free, repeatable reps to PRIME standard and the exercise remains below 7 RPE
- Stop at and assign the highest exercise the client can perform proficiently at about 7 RPE
- If posture, range, control, comfort or safety breaks down, stop and assign the previous proficient exercise
- Never skip a stage or progress solely because 10 reps were completed
- Before progressing from Barbell Romanian Deadlift to Trap Bar Deadlift, require 12 reps at 35 kg total barbell load. Every rep must include a controlled one-second hold with the bar just below mid-knee while the client maintains a PRIME postural position
- Record all reps and load for every exercise completed
- Leave exercises not completed blank
- Rest 30 seconds between sets

**Breathing and bracing:** Teach in all exercises. Begin in a lying position, then transfer to standing and loaded movements.

**Squat Pattern:**

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Glute Bridge | 1 × 10 | No load. Move to next if form maintained |
| Bodyweight Box Squat | 1 × 10 | Use plates to adjust box height: find the height with the most control. No load if form maintained at nexus point |
| Bodyweight Squat | 1 × 10 | No load if form maintained at nexus point |
| Kettlebell Goblet Squat | 1 × 10 | Record reps and weight. If 7 RPE not achieved with a load above 10kg, move to Nexus Point Squat |
| Nexus Point Squat | 1 × 10 | Record reps and weight only if upright torso is maintained at the nexus point |

**Hinge Pattern:**

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Broomstick Hinge | 1 × 10 | Establish the hinge pattern. Advance only when all reps meet PRIME and remain below 7 RPE |
| Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift | 1 × 10 | Record reps and load. Advance only when all reps meet PRIME and remain below 7 RPE |
| Barbell Romanian Deadlift | 1 × 12 at 35 kg | Hold one second just below mid-knee on every rep. Progress only when all 12 reps maintain a PRIME postural position |
| Trap Bar Deadlift | 1 × 10 | Record reps and load. Advance only when all reps meet PRIME and remain below 7 RPE |
| Barbell Deadlift | 1 × 10 | Record reps and load. Assign only when all reps meet PRIME at about 7 RPE |

**Bench Pattern:**

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Dumbbell Bench Press | 1 × 10 | Record reps and weight. If scapula retraction, arch, and active hips are maintained with total load above 15kg, move to Barbell Bench Press |
| Barbell Bench Press | 1 × 10 | Record reps and weight only if scapula retraction, arch, and active hips are maintained throughout |

**After the session:**
- **Upsell 1:1 PT**: explain that additional sessions allow you to continue this assessment and progression process together
- Fast Track members: confirm their ongoing availability
- Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au if bookings need adjusting
- Send a post-session text highlighting something they did well

**If not continuing PT:**
- Let them know you'll check in monthly
- Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with client status
- Send a warm final encouragement text

### Lesson 4: Session 2

**Purpose:** Complete only the 3 exercises assigned in Session 1: 1 working set each, 3 total working sets. Leave all other exercises blank.

Today's focus: progressing posture and range of motion, identifying areas of improvement, and teaching the member how to measure and track reps and load.

**Preparation:**
- Reminder text 24 hours before
- Arrive 15 minutes early: review progress notes

**Check-in:**
- **KPH accountability review (do this first, every session):**
  - How many sessions did they complete this week?
  - Which KPH targets did they hit: training frequency, habit compliance?
  - What got in the way of anything they missed?
  - What needs to change this week to make sure they hit it?
- Any sore or stiff muscles? Take time to warm those areas up. If nothing is sore, warm up for the exercise selection appropriately.

**RM Education:**

Teach the client what rep range and RM means:

> *"10–12RM means pick a weight you can lift for 10, 11, or 12 reps, but not 13. You should reach rep 12 and not be able to complete rep 13 with good form. If you can, add 1–2kg. If you can only lift for 6 reps, remove weight until you're within the rep range."*

**Progressive overload:**
- Complete 12 reps across all working sets → increase weight by 1–2kg next week
- Complete 10 or 11 reps → aim for one more rep at the same weight next week

**Movement Session:**

Begin with the Performance Warm-Up before the first working set. This is the first session where members learn this protocol: teach it explicitly, not just model it.

**The Performance Warm-Up (main lift only):**

This protocol is validated at Melbourne University by Dr Paul Cribb to increase performance by up to 30% through acclimatising the body and priming the central nervous system.

| Set | Load | Reps |
|---|---|---|
| Warm-Up Set 1 | 20% of working weight | 12 reps |
| Warm-Up Set 2 | 60% of working weight | 6 reps |
| Acclimation Set | 100% of working weight | 1 rep |
| → Working Set | 100% of working weight | 10–12 RM |

*Example: 40kg working weight → 8kg × 12 reps → 24kg × 6 reps → 40kg × 1 rep → 40kg working set.*

Explain to the member: *"These warm-up sets prime your nervous system for the working weight. By the time you get to your working set, it'll feel much more controlled."*

- **RPE target:** 7–8
- **Rest:** 2 minutes between sets
- **Sets:** 1 working set per exercise (assigned exercises only)

| Pattern | Progression Options | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|---|
| Squat | Glute Bridge / BW Box Squat / BW Squat / KB Goblet Squat / Nexus Point Squat | 1 × 10–12RM |
| Hinge | Broomstick Hinge / Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift / Barbell Romanian Deadlift / Trap Bar Deadlift / Barbell Deadlift | 1 × 10–12RM |
| Bench | DB Bench Press / Barbell Bench Press | 1 × 10–12RM |

Teach breathing and bracing in all exercises. Begin in a lying position, transfer to standing and loaded movements. Record all reps and load.

Retain the Session 1 hinge assignment unless the member demonstrates the next exercise to PRIME standard. Use the same sequence and stop rule whenever reassessing.

The 35 kg Barbell Romanian Deadlift gate remains mandatory in every reassessment: 12 reps with a one-second hold just below mid-knee on every rep in a PRIME postural position.

**After the session:**
- Confirm availability for Sessions 3 and 4
- Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with updates
- Send encouragement text

### Lesson 5: Session 3

**Purpose:** Complete only the exercises the client is working on. Reassess: can they progress to the next exercise in any pattern?

Today's focus: 2 working sets per exercise, 6 total working sets.

**Preparation:**
- Reminder text 24 hours before
- Arrive 15 minutes early: review progress notes

**Check-in:**
- **KPH accountability review (do this first):**
  - How many sessions did they complete?
  - Which KPH targets did they hit?
  - What got in the way of anything they missed?
  - What needs to change this week to make it?
- Any sore or stiff muscles? Warm up accordingly.

**Movement Session:**

Use Performance Warm Up and Acclimation Sets. Reinforce RM education and progressive overload logic.

- **RPE target:** 7–8
- **Rest:** 2 minutes between sets
- **Sets:** 2 working sets per exercise

| Pattern | Progression Options | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|---|
| Squat | Glute Bridge / BW Box Squat / BW Squat / KB Goblet Squat / Nexus Point Squat | 2 × 10–12RM |
| Hinge | Broomstick Hinge / Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift / Barbell Romanian Deadlift / Trap Bar Deadlift / Barbell Deadlift | 2 × 10–12RM |
| Bench | DB Bench Press / Barbell Bench Press | 2 × 10–12RM |

Record all reps and load.

Only progress the hinge assignment when the member demonstrates the next exercise to PRIME standard. Otherwise consolidate the current assignment.

Progress beyond Barbell Romanian Deadlift only after the member passes the 35 kg, 12-rep, one-second-hold PRIME gate.

**After the session:**
- Confirm availability for Session 4
- **Ask if they are interested in continuing 1:1 PT after Intro Sessions**
- Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with updates
- Send encouragement text

### Lesson 6: Session 4

**Purpose:** Retain the two-set structure from Session 3. Complete only the exercises the client is working on and consolidate the highest proficient assignment in each pattern, for 6 total working sets.

This is the final Intro Session: focus on consolidating their exercise assignments and closing for ongoing 1:1 PT.

**Preparation:**
- Reminder text 24 hours before
- Arrive 15 minutes early: review progress notes

**Check-in:**
- **KPH accountability review (do this first):**
  - How many sessions did they complete?
  - Which KPH targets did they hit?
  - What got in the way of anything they missed?
  - What needs to change this week to make it?
- Acknowledge this is the final Intro Session (if not continuing PT)
- Ask: *"Imagine you've been consistent with 3–4 sessions per week for 6 months: how would you feel?"*
- Ask: *"What benefits do you think you'll experience?"*

**Movement Session:**

- **RPE target:** 7–8
- **Rest:** 2 minutes between sets
- **Sets:** 2 working sets per exercise

| Pattern | Progression Options | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|---|
| Squat | Glute Bridge / BW Box Squat / BW Squat / KB Goblet Squat / Nexus Point Squat | 2 × 10–12RM |
| Hinge | Broomstick Hinge / Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift / Barbell Romanian Deadlift / Trap Bar Deadlift / Barbell Deadlift | 2 × 10–12RM |
| Bench | DB Bench Press / Barbell Bench Press | 2 × 10–12RM |

Record all reps and load.

Consolidate Barbell Romanian Deadlift unless the member has passed the 35 kg, 12-rep, one-second-hold PRIME gate.

**After the session:**
- Let them know you'll check in monthly
- Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with client status
- Send a warm final encouragement text
