### Lesson 4: Delivering the Session

**Member arrival:**
- Greet every member warmly as they arrive
- Brief overview of the session
- Identify any injuries or concerns before you begin

**Warm-Up (5–10 minutes):**
- Use the prescribed warm-up in Trainerize
- Group members together: highlight the session focus and build rapport
- Have members open Trainerize and identify their working weight for the first exercise
- Group 2–3 people with similar working weights to minimise racking/unracking
- Walk through the Performance Warm-Up before the first working set

**The Performance Warm-Up (applies to the main lift only):**

This protocol is scientifically validated at Melbourne University by Dr Paul Cribb to increase performance by up to 30% through acclimatising the body and priming the central nervous system.

| Set | Load | Reps |
|---|---|---|
| Warm-Up Set 1 | 20% of working weight | 12 reps |
| Warm-Up Set 2 | 60% of working weight | 6 reps |
| Acclimation Set | 100% of working weight | 1 rep |
| → Working Sets begin | | |

The member's target working weight is 100%. Calculate backwards. *Example: 40kg working weight → 8kg × 12 reps → 24kg × 6 reps → 40kg × 1 rep → 40kg working sets.*

These are not working sets: they should not feel like failure. The purpose is neurological priming: by the time the first working set begins, the nervous system has been progressively loaded to handle the weight. Working sets feel controlled, not surprising.

**Main Session (30–40 minutes):**
- Follow the Trainerize workout: adhere to sets, reps, and movement patterns
- Ensure everyone has a spotter (clients spot each other in most situations)
- Have all clients completing working sets in synchronisation: easier to correct form
- **Position yourself so your back is never turned to a member**
- **Provide at least one touch point per client per working set**: each member must receive a cue, adjustment, or acknowledgement at least 8 times per workout
- When giving cues: ask for consent → highlight what they're already doing well → give the cue constructively
- **Do not sit down** at any point during the workout
- **No personal phones during class** except to film and upload Instagram stories; tag @the_evolved_allfemalegym. All other personal phone use is prohibited during class
- Actively preempt space setup as the workout progresses (refer to the laminated diagram)
- You may progress, regress, or change any exercise at any point in the workout: as long as the substitute remains in the same movement pattern and uses an EVOLVE-defined option
- If you change an exercise, swap it in the Trainerize workout at the time of recording; all substitutions must be documented

**Cool-Down (5–10 minutes):**
- Use the prescribed cool-down from Trainerize

### Lesson 5: The EVOLVE System

The EVOLVE System is how we standardise station setup and exercise selection across every class, regardless of which trainer is running it. It removes guesswork from class preparation and ensures every member gets the same quality experience week to week.

**The physical EVOLVE cards live in the back left corner of the gym.** These are laminated floor cards: one per exercise group. Pull the correct card before every class.

**The floor layout:**

The training floor has 6 platforms in 2 rows × 3 columns. Fixed equipment includes the Lat Pulldown machine (top centre), Dumbbell Rack (centre divider), Storage (left), and 2 Cable machines (right wall).

**The weight tier system:**

Before class, assign each member to a tier based on their last recorded working weight in Trainerize:

| Tier | Working Weight |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 | <20kg |
| Tier 2 | 20kg> |
| Tier 3 | 40kg> |

Pre-set all 6 platforms per the EVOLVE floor card for that exercise before members arrive. Equipment is already there when they walk in: no mid-session rearranging.

**The regression/progression spectrums:**

For every exercise, the EVOLVE cards show a horizontal spectrum from easiest (left) to most advanced (right). If a member cannot perform the prescribed exercise with good form, regress left on the spectrum. If they are clearly ready to progress, move right.

Do not improvise regressions or progressions. Use only the EVOLVE-defined options. This is what ensures consistency across all trainers.

**The key rule:** The trainer's job in class is to coach, not to design. Design happens before class via the EVOLVE cards. During class, your attention is on the members.

*Full exercise regression/progression spectrums and tier equipment lists: refer to the physical EVOLVE floor cards in the gym and Section 06 of the Evolved Manual.*

### Lesson 6: Post-Session & Troubleshooting

**Farewell**
- Tell everyone what a great job they did
- Congratulate 2–3 members individually: identify 1 win or 1 improvement and offer a class recommendation or complimentary 1:1 PT session. To action a complimentary session: name one specific time you are already paid to be in the gym and have free time, do not offer to the same member more than once per 6 months, and book it on the spot in Lead Connector
- Ask when they're coming in next

**Low attendance:** Reach out to regular members who were absent to check in on them.

**Injury during class:** Follow emergency procedures. Document the incident immediately.

**Equipment malfunction:** Substitute exercises: notify management immediately.

**No clients show:** To be paid, you must remain on premises for the full session time and complete approved alternative activities. Text 0402 840 247 to notify of the no-show and confirm what you'll be doing. See Course 8, Lesson 6 for the full list of approved activities.

### Lesson 7: Testing Week

Testing Week replaces the regular Sculpt & Strength program for one week each quarter. The session structure is the same: 60 minutes, same capacity, same EVOLVE setup. The content is different: members test their current capability against the Evolved Standards instead of completing a working program.

Testing Week has two purposes. First, it shows members, in objective and measurable terms, how much stronger and more capable they have become.

Second, it gives you data: who is approaching a new standard, who has a gap to close, and who is ready for a programming shift or a 1:1 PT conversation.

**When it runs:** Quarterly. Refer to the current training calendar.

**How to deliver it:**

Testing Week is delivered exactly like any other session: open Trainerize and run the prescribed Testing Week program. The program selects which exercises are tested and how: do not add to or substitute from it. Record all results in Trainerize during the session.

**Finding the estimated 1RM:** Instruct each member to tap on the exercise in Trainerize until it opens to the exercise video. A slider along the bottom shows their Trainerize-estimated 1RM based on their training history, as well as any previous personal best. Members use this number as their starting point.

**Attempts:** Each member has two attempts per exercise. The first attempt is their estimated 1RM. Based on whether they achieve it or not, they increase or decrease the load for their second attempt.

**Rep prescription:** The testing week program prescribes 1–3RM. Beginners should opt for less weight and more reps within that range; intermediate and advanced members should work toward more weight and fewer reps.

**Who should not test:** If a member has had less than 4 weeks of consistent attendance leading up to Testing Week, do not test them. Revert to the previous week's program and treat the session as a normal workout. This applies equally to newer members and to members returning from time away.

- Give every member their result against the standard immediately: name the level they are at, name the next level, and tell them specifically what they need to get there
- Celebrate every standard crossed: call it out by name in front of the group

**The full standards:**

**Category 1: 1RM Strength Standards**

*All loads expressed as % of bodyweight. Source: StrengthLevel.com*

| Exercise | Live | Long | Perform |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deadlift | 50% BW | 125% BW | 250% BW |
| Squat | 50% BW | 125% BW | 200% BW |
| Bench Press | 25% BW | 75% BW | 150% BW |
| Overhead Press | 20% BW | 50% BW | 100% BW |
| Lat Pulldown | 30% BW | 70% BW | 130% BW |
| Push Ups | 5 reps, chest to ground | 15 reps, chest to ground | 30 reps, chest to ground |

**Category 2: Functional Strength Standards**

*All loads expressed as % of bodyweight. Source: Dr John Rusin Functional Strength Standards*

| Exercise | Live | Long | Perform |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barbell Romanian Deadlift | 60% BW | 120% BW | 180% BW |
| Single Arm DB Reverse Lunge | 20% BW | 40% BW | 60% BW |
| Single Arm DB Overhead Press | 10% BW | 20% BW | 30% BW |
| Chest Supported Row | 30% BW | 60% BW | 100% BW |
| Reverse Grip Pull Up | 30% BW | 60% BW | 100% BW |
| DB Farmer Walk (Walk of Independence) | 75% BW for 1:00 | 100% BW for 1:00 | 150% BW for 1:00 |
| Core | Full Plank 2:00 unadjusted | Side Plank 1:00 unadjusted | 10 Strict Toes to Bar |

**Category 3: Functional Fitness Standards**

*Sources: RunningLevel.com; RowingLevel.com; Ben Patrick (ATG)*

| Exercise | Live | Long | Perform |
|---|---|---|---|
| Running | 5km in <30:00 | 10km in <50:00 | 21km in <90:00 |
| Rowing | 1000m in <5:30 | 1000m in <4:30 | 1000m in <3:15 |
| Beep Test | Level 6.0+ | Level 9.0+ | Level 12.0+ |
| Pistol Squat | 1 rep hip crease below parallel | 10 reps hip crease below parallel | 50% BW × 10 reps HCBP |
| Reverse Sled | 50% BW × 40m | 100% BW × 40m | 200% BW × 40m |
| Forward Sled | 50% BW × 40m | 100% BW × 40m | 200% BW × 40m |
| ATG Split Squat | 10 reps, full hamstring-to-calf contact | 50% BW × 10 reps full depth | 100% BW × 10 reps full depth |

*HCBP = hip crease below parallel. Sled loads exclude sled weight.*

**Presenting results:**

Connect the number to something real. Do not just read out percentages.

- *"Your deadlift is now at 95% of your bodyweight. The Long standard is 125%. That means you're about 15kg away from the level where the research shows meaningful protection against the bone and muscle loss that accelerates through menopause."*
- *"You've crossed into Long on the Walk of Independence. That's the standard where the longevity research shows the strongest protection against frailty and loss of independence later in life. You did that."*

Standards create targets that mean something. A member who knows she is 8kg from the Long deadlift standard has a reason to show up next week that goes beyond aesthetics.

**After Testing Week:**

- Review results across the group: identify members approaching a new standard, members with a gap worth addressing in 1:1 PT, and members who may be ready to progress their programming
- Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with a summary of any members flagged for follow-up
