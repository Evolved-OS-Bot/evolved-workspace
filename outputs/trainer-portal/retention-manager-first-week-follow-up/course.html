Know Your Part of the Handoff

The Membership: First 7 Days workflow checks for member replies on Day 7, Day 8 and Day 9. When a reply needs personal follow-up, GHL creates a task for the Retention Manager.

Admin Eve owns the written response, internal handoff note and review-workflow state. The Retention Manager owns the personal relationship follow-up and the record of what happened.

You do not need to interpret the workflow logic or change a member's review status. If a task or note is unclear, leave the task open and ask Admin Eve or management to clarify it.

Positive Replies

Read the member's reply and Admin Eve's note, then call her as soon as possible. Thank her, acknowledge what she shared and ask how her first week has felt.

Check whether she has left a Google review. If the moment feels natural, mention that a spot has opened for a new member and ask whether a friend or woman in her life may benefit from getting stronger too.

If she has someone in mind, ask for a warm introduction or confirm that the referred woman has agreed to be contacted. Record or use referral details only after that consent is confirmed, then get in touch promptly.

Keep the conversation warm and genuine. The referral invitation should build on the rapport in the call, not feel like a forced sales script.

Negative Replies

Read the member's reply and Admin Eve's note, then call her as soon as possible. Listen carefully, make sure she feels heard and find out what support would genuinely help.

Address anything within your role. If the issue cannot be remedied without owner intervention, escalate the findings to the owners promptly.

If you cannot reach the member, leave a message and continue trying until personal contact is made or the owners agree on the next step. Do not promise a refund, billing change, membership change or management decision.

Unclear Replies and Role Boundaries

Admin Eve reads what the member has shared and works out what she needs next. The handoff should tell you whether the member needs encouragement or a prompt support call, with enough context for a human conversation.

If the task still asks you to classify the reply or correct the review workflow, leave it open. Ask Admin Eve or management for a clear handoff before acting.

Record the Outcome and Complete the Task

Open the member's GHL contact record, select Notes and add an internal note. Do not rely on a task comment or conversation message as the operating record.

Record the date and time, contact channel, whether the member was reached, what she shared, the support discussed, the agreed outcome, and every next action with its owner and due date. For a positive reply, also record review status, the referral outcome, consent source and any referral details that may be used.

Complete the task only after the required follow-up, internal note and any escalation are recorded. A completed checkbox without the internal note is not a completed follow-up.

If you are away or cannot complete the follow-up in time, ask management to assign it to approved cover. Do not pass the task to a general trainer who has not been given access to the process.
