### Lesson 2: Professional Standards

**Conduct and Behaviour**

- Treat all members, staff, and team members with respect and courtesy at all times
- Foster an inclusive and supportive environment; free from discrimination, harassment, or bullying
- Arrive on time, appropriately dressed, and prepared for every session

**Boundaries and Respect**

- Respect members' personal space, boundaries, and fitness goals
- Always obtain explicit consent before providing physical adjustments or personal advice

**Presentation**

You are a representative of The Evolved brand the moment you walk through the door. Dress professionally and maintain a positive, energetic presence at all times.

### Lesson 3: Operational Policies

**Programming**

- The Small Group PT program is delivered as structured; do not make major changes to sets, reps, or movement patterns
- You may progress, regress, or change individual exercises at any point in the workout based on a member's strength, fitness, or injury status: as long as the exercise remains in the same movement pattern
- If you change an exercise, update it in the Trainerize workout before or at the time of recording; all substitutions must be documented
- All 1:1 PT clients must have a minimum 12-week program in Trainerize at all times; random checks are conducted

**Scheduling**

- If you are going to be absent, give management at least 24 hours notice: call Peter (0420 863 721) or Megan (0402 840 247)

**1:1 PT session reschedules:** Follow this process in order:
1. Take the client's phone, find their booking link, and reschedule the session for them while they watch: this solves the problem immediately and shows them how to use it themselves next time
2. If the booking link cannot be found: open Lead Connector and find a mutually available time on your calendar to reschedule from there
3. If no mutual time is available on the spot: have them message the gym phone (0483 968 880) with their preferred day and time

**SGPT session bookings:** If a member can't book or cancel a session in the app, help them troubleshoot their app access and walk them through making the change themselves.

**Anything you cannot resolve:** Redirect the member to message the gym phone on 0483 968 880.

**Timesheets & Pay**

- Timesheets are due by EOD on Tuesdays via the app provided by our accountants
- Pay is processed on Wednesdays
- If timesheets aren't submitted on time, pay will be processed the following week

**Injury, Health Conditions, Surgery & Pregnancy**

Training modification is always the starting point. Cessation is a last resort.

When a member presents with a health or injury concern:

1. Ask questions first so you understand the condition and its impact
2. Explore what the member can still do; modified training is usually possible
3. Offer allied health collaboration, with the member's explicit consent
4. If modification is not immediately clear, offer an Injury Triage Session
5. Use a membership hold only when modified training is genuinely not possible
6. Reach cancellation only when the member has been medically advised to cease all physical activity for an extended, unknown period

Notify management about any significant injury, health condition, surgery, or pregnancy. Escalate any uncertainty before proceeding; do not guess.

**Complaints**

Your role is to deliver great training, not to handle complaints. Always redirect complaints to info@theevolvedgym.com.au

### Lesson 4: Privacy Policy

At The Evolved, we take member privacy seriously.

**What we collect:** name, date of birth, contact details, health information, payment details, and attendance records.

**How it's used:** solely for delivering training services, managing memberships, and communicating with members.

**Your obligations as a trainer:**
- Never share, discuss, or disclose any member's personal or health information outside of what is needed to deliver their training
- Health questionnaires, body measurements, and photos are strictly confidential
- Members consent to being photographed or filmed during class for social media use unless they have opted out in writing. Trainers are required to film and upload an Instagram story after every class; tag @the_evolved_allfemalegym. Members who wish to opt out must email info@theevolvedgym.com.au

Breaches of member privacy are treated as serious misconduct.

### Lesson 5: Emergency Procedures

**Key contacts:**
- Emergency services: **000**
- Peter: **0420 863 721**
- Megan: **0402 840 247**

**Fire**
- Call 000
- Use the fire extinguisher (near the bag storage area, below the AED) on small, contained fires only
- Do not fight large fires; evacuate immediately
- Guide members out via the front entrance roller door
- Assemble at the street level car park
- Do not re-enter until emergency services give clearance

**Medical Emergency**
- Call 000 for anything critical
- First aid kit: bag storage area
- AED: confirm location with management on your first shift
- Perform CPR if trained and required
- Notify Peter or Megan immediately

**Power Outage**
- Keep members calm
- Stop all equipment use immediately
- Wait for management instruction before resuming

**Incident Reports**
Every injury, equipment malfunction, or safety issue must be reported. Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with:
- Date, time, and location
- People involved
- What happened
- Actions taken

Submit within 24 hours of the incident.

**First Aid Certification**
You must hold current First Aid and CPR certifications at all times:
- CPR: renew every 12 months
- First Aid: renew every 3 years
- Provide copies to management on renewal
