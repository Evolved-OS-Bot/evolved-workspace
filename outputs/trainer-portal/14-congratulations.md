## Lessons

### Lesson 1: You're Certified

Completing this program means you've done more than tick boxes. You've demonstrated your understanding of The Evolved's standards, philosophy, and delivery across every service we offer.

**What you've proven:**
- You understand what it takes to get results for women, and why
- You can deliver every class and service to the standard The Evolved is built on
- You know how to onboard, assess, and retain members
- You're committed to the culture and expectations of this gym

Download your certificate and keep it. You are now a fully onboarded Evolved trainer.

---

### Lesson 2: Your First Six Months

Your first six months at The Evolved is a probation period. This is standard for all trainers.

During this time, management will be observing your:
- Culture fit: how you interact with members, the team, and the environment
- Delivery standards: whether what you learned in this portal is what you're doing on the floor
- Attitude toward feedback and growth

This is not a period to simply survive: it's a period to prove that the standards you've just demonstrated knowledge of are the standards you actually hold in practice. The best way through probation is to deliver every session as if you're being assessed, because the habits you build in these six months are the ones you'll carry for years.

Management will check in with you during this period. If you have questions about how you're performing, ask: don't wait.

---

### Lesson 3: Ongoing Standards

Completing this portal is not a one-time achievement. These are the ongoing requirements that keep your certification current.

**Certifications**

| Certification | Renewal |
|---|---|
| CPR | Every 12 months |
| First Aid | Every 3 years |

Provide updated copies to management upon renewal. Letting these lapse is not an option: you cannot legally deliver sessions without current certification.

**Portal Updates**

When The Evolved updates its SOPs, standards, or adds new services, you will be notified by email and required to complete or re-complete the relevant module. This keeps every trainer current on the same standard. A notification email means action is required: it is not optional reading.

**The 144 Workout Standard**

The Evolved's training philosophy is built on the belief that you cannot coach what you haven't done yourself. The standard that reflects this is **144 strength workouts recorded in your own Trainerize account per year**: roughly 3 sessions per week, year-round.

This is not a disciplinary requirement. It is a recognition standard. Trainers who meet it demonstrate that they are living the method they are asking their members to live. It informs how we recognise trainers within the team and the opportunities we extend to them.

Track your own training in Trainerize the same way you expect your members to.

### Lesson 4: What This Stands For

The women who walk through this door are trusting you with something important: their health, their confidence, and their time. Most of them have tried other gyms, other programs, other trainers. They are here because something about The Evolved felt different.

Your job is to make sure it is different. Every session, every check-in, every cue, every standard you hold: it all compounds into the experience that either keeps them here and changes their life, or doesn't.

The portal you've just completed is the foundation. What you build on it is up to you.

Welcome to the team.
