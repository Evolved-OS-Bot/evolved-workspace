Open & Close Checklist

OPEN Checklist

Arrive & Prepare

  • Arrive 15 minutes before your shift
  • Leave your problems at the door: put your coaching hat on

Comfort

  • Switch main lights on
  • Switch 'Girl Boss' light on
  • Hot weather: Turn on fans and extractor fan

Create the Vibe

  • Open Spotify on the iPad
  • Connect to the gym speaker
  • Pick an appropriate playlist for class energy

Equipment

  • Check equipment setup is appropriate for your class

Welcome Everyone

  • Open Trainerize
  • Check who has booked in for your class
  • Greet each member in a warm and friendly way
  • Edit class list: remove no-shows
  • Edit class list: add members who haven't booked in
  • Complete this before starting members on warm-up exercises

Promote

  • Take a 5–10 second clip of the room and post as a story; tag @the_evolved_allfemalegym

CLOSE Checklist

During Cool-Down (Last 5 Minutes)

  • Wipe down all equipment
  • Remove chalk from weight plates, dumbbells, barbells, and squat racks
  • Order dumbbells with numbers facing up
  • Make sure weight plates are in the correct place
  • Bring cardio equipment in

After Class

  • Set up the gym for the next class
  • Wash used face towels in bacterial wash
  • Re-roll face towels and place in fridge
  • Make sure iPad and Wi-Fi are on charge
  • Hot weather: Fill evaporative air conditioners with water
  • Leave the gym neat and tidy

As You Leave

  • Empty and replace bin bag
  • Turn lights and fans off
  • Hot weather: Keep extractor fan on at night
  • Lock up

No Show Checklist

If a client no-shows or cancels within 24 hours, complete tasks from this list in order to be paid for the session.

Immediate (First 5 Minutes)

  • Check class bookings and text no-shows
  • Call client if it is a 1:1 PT no-show
  • Text 0402 840 247 to notify management of the no-show

15-Minute Blocks: Complete in Order

Block 1

  • Vacuum: pull equipment away from wall first
  • Tidy storage areas
  • Empty vacuum and put on charge
  • Empty bin and replace bag
  • Mop gym floor: mop under equipment and against walls

Block 2

  • Wipe down benches, barbells, dumbbells, and racks (remove all chalk)
  • Wipe down microwave inside and out
  • Wipe down all shelving: move items and wipe underneath (including supplements)

Block 3

  • Check dumbbells are in order and in the correct spot
  • Check liquid chalk and refill if needed
  • Check towels are moist, clean, and cold: clean and freshen any used towels

Block 4

  • Update Instagram story; tag @the_evolved_allfemalegym with more detail and education

When finished: Text 0402 840 247 with the block numbers completed.

If All Tasks Are Complete for the Day

  • Sweep outdoor area
  • Clear rubbish from table area
  • If a class is running: spot clients and assist the acting trainer
  • Text 0402 840 247 if all tasks are completed and you need something else to do
