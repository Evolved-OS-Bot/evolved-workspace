Practical Sign-Off: Overview

Knowledge without delivery is incomplete. This course confirms that what you have learned in Courses 1–12 translates into real, consistent performance on the floor.

There are 10 blocks. Each block has a specific completion requirement. All sign-offs are with Megan. Do not rush through these; each block is an opportunity to develop, not just demonstrate.

Once all 10 blocks are approved, you will be granted access to your certification course.

Block 1: Verbal Knowledge Assessment

Covers: Welcome & Standards (Course 1), Making Women Strong (Course 2), Member Care (Course 11), General Duties (Course 12)

Format: A verbal Q&A session with Megan, approximately 30 minutes.

What You Will Be Assessed On

  • Professional standards, privacy obligations, and emergency procedures
  • The science of strength training for women and the KPH framework
  • Member check-in, retention, and complaint escalation protocols
  • Gym opening and closing, cleaning, incident reporting, and equipment

Schedule this session with Megan. She will ask questions drawn from the four courses above. You must demonstrate clear, confident knowledge across all four areas.

Sign-off: Megan confirms competency and approves this block in the portal.

Block 8: Intro Sessions

Covers: Course 9: Intro Sessions

Three-Step Requirement

  1. Participate: Megan delivers Sessions 1 and 2 to you as the client; experience the full new member journey from their perspective
  2. Shadow: Observe Megan deliver Sessions 1 and 2 to a new or practice member; note structure, coaching points, and the post-session PT conversation
  3. Supervised Delivery: Deliver Sessions 1 and 2 in full with Megan observing

Sign-Off Criteria

  • Correct session structure followed for both sessions
  • SGPT sessions booked for the member during Session 1
  • Permission requested before every measurement and separately before photos; a declined photo is accepted without pressure
  • Shoulders, right bicep, waist, hips, right quad and right calf measured in the correct order, at the correct sites and recorded to the nearest 0.5 cm
  • Front, side and rear photos taken with consistent full-body framing, uploaded to Trainerize and handled according to the clothing, consent and privacy rules
  • Squat and bench progressions applied correctly
  • Hinge progression follows Broomstick Hinge, Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift, Barbell Romanian Deadlift, Trap Bar Deadlift, then Barbell Deadlift
  • Trainer advances one hinge stage only when 10 pain-free, repeatable reps meet PRIME and remain below 7 RPE, then stops at the highest proficient exercise
  • Trainer requires 12 Barbell Romanian Deadlift reps at 35 kg total barbell load, with a controlled one-second hold just below mid-knee on every rep in a PRIME postural position, before progressing to Trap Bar Deadlift
  • PT conversation timing follows the membership pathway: at the end of the single onboarding session for Strong, Fit & Flexible Membership members, and during the later onboarding sessions for Fast Track members
  • Results recorded in Trainerize

Sign-off: Megan observes both sessions in Step 3 and approves this block.

Block 9: Strength Assessment

Covers: Course 10: Strength Assessment

Three-Step Requirement

  1. Participate: Megan delivers a full Strength Assessment to you; experience the process from the prospect's perspective
  2. Shadow: Observe Megan deliver a full Strength Assessment to a real or practice prospect; note consultation quality, test execution, results presentation, and the close
  3. Supervised Delivery: Deliver a complete Strength Assessment (all 3 parts) with Megan observing

Sign-Off Criteria

  • Pre-qualification notes reviewed; Trainerize set up on the Sales iPad before they arrive
  • Completed pre-qualification injury questioning is not repeated; only PAR-Q or current safety issues are clarified
  • Consultation completed without advice or coaching
  • All 4 physical tests run correctly; results recorded on the Strength Assessment Card
  • Correct life-stage card selected for the prospect
  • Close question delivered word-for-word; silence held
  • At least one objection handled

Sign-off: Megan observes Step 3 and approves this block.

Block 10: 36 Workouts

This block confirms that you have completed 36 workouts as a client and experienced The Evolved product firsthand. By living the member journey yourself, you can understand its value and become a genuine raving fan of what we offer.

Requirements

  1. 36 workouts completed: Complete a combination of Sculpt & Strength and Build & Balance workouts as a client in your own Trainerize account
  2. Evidence of completion: Upload a screenshot or export from your Trainerize account showing 36 completed workouts
  3. Written reflection: Write a minimum 200-word reflection on your growth as a woman across this period, covering what has changed in your strength, confidence, and understanding of the member experience. Explain what was harder than expected and what you still want to develop.

Upload both the evidence and your written reflection as attachments to this block.

Sign-off: Megan reviews your submission and approves this block.
