Class Overview

  • Duration: 60 minutes
  • Capacity: 15 members
  • Focus: Core stability, mobility, controlled movement, and breathwork
  • Equipment: Mats, resistance bands, small Pilates balls, light dumbbells (optional)

Evolved Pilates is calming, precise, and accessible to all fitness levels. The environment is intentionally different from every other class at The Evolved: the setup and your energy reflect that.

Where Evolved Pilates Fits in the Evolved Method

Evolved Pilates sits in The Evolved's Less Intense Activity category: distinct from both strength training (Sculpt & Strength, Build & Balance) and Intense Cardio Exercise (Metabolic Burn, Hybrid Racing).

What This Means

  • Pilates does not meet the strength KPH: it does not generate sufficient progressive loading to drive meaningful muscle mass, bone density, or hormonal adaptation for most women
  • It is not classified as ICE: the intensity is lower and the format is not interval-based
  • Members can outgrow the resistance stimulus in Pilates relatively quickly, at which point the session no longer provides a significant physical adaptation challenge

What Pilates Does Provide

  • Mobility development: improved range of motion and joint control
  • Core strength in prone, supine, and unilateral positions
  • Breathwork: supporting the nervous system and reinforcing the diaphragm-pelvic floor relationship taught in strength sessions
  • Active recovery: a session that supports training without adding to fatigue

Evolved Pilates is most valuable as a complement to strength training, not as a standalone program. If a member attends only Pilates, they are not meeting the strength minimum.

Guide them toward adding strength sessions.

Delivering the Session: Environment and Energy

The environment is the first delivery decision you make in Evolved Pilates. It must be set up correctly or the session fails before it begins.

Your energy must match the room. Evolved Pilates is more soothing and calming than Sculpt & Strength, Build & Balance, Metabolic Burn, or Hybrid Racing.

Lower voice, slower cues, longer pauses. Members who come to this class value that environment: protect it.

Member Arrival

  • Greet members warmly: this class attracts members who value calm and focus
  • Brief overview of the session
  • Identify any injuries or limitations before starting

Warm-Up (5–10 minutes)

  • Use the prescribed warm-up from Trainerize: focused on breath, alignment, and gentle mobility
  • Give alternatives within your scope. If unsure: deliver what's prescribed, apologise, ask management ASAP

Main Session (30–40 minutes)

  • Follow the Trainerize workout: adhere to sets, timing, and movement patterns
  • Controlled, precise movements throughout: this is not a high-intensity session
  • Give progressions and regressions as needed. If unsure: deliver what's prescribed, ask management ASAP

Cool-Down (5–10 minutes)

  • Access the YouTube breathwork video at the end of the Pilates workout on the iPad and play it as a guided cool-down
  • Or: study the video and deliver your own version using its principles
  • This is a mindful transition: take it seriously

Coaching with PRIME in Pilates

The same PRIME coaching lens applies in Evolved Pilates as in every other Evolved class. The framework doesn't change: the pace, intensity, and environment do.

P: Posture: In Pilates, posture begins on the floor. Is the spine neutral in the lying position?

Is the pelvis controlled? Are the shoulders drawing back?

Establish this before any movement begins.

R, Range of Motion: Pilates is an opportunity to expand range of motion in a low-load environment. Work within the member's available range and gently extend it.

Never force, earn it. Breathwork supports range expansion.

I: Identify Areas of Improvement: Observe before you intervene. In a 60-minute Pilates session you have more time per member than in a strength class.

Use it. One well-timed, precise cue is worth ten rushed ones.

M, Measure & Monitor: Members record the reps they achieve within the time frame for each exercise in Trainerize. This gives members who want to improve a clear target to beat next session, and it gives you data to track progression over time.

Encourage members to record during the session, not after.

E, Evolve: Progress load, complexity, or range once the member demonstrates consistent control, from floor-based to standing, from bilateral to unilateral, from bodyweight to light load.

Breathing in Pilates: Breath is not a background detail: it is a coaching cue. Reinforce the diaphragmatic breathing pattern taught in strength sessions.

Members who breathe well in Pilates carry that habit into their deadlifts and squats.

Post-Session & Troubleshooting

Farewell

  • Acknowledge the effort: Pilates demands mental focus, not just physical
  • Tell everyone what a great job they did
  • Congratulate 2–3 members individually: identify 1 win or 1 improvement and offer a class recommendation or complimentary 1:1 PT session. To action a complimentary session: name one specific time you are already paid to be in the gym and have free time, do not offer to the same member more than once per 6 months, and book it on the spot in Lead Connector.
  • Ask when they're coming in next

Clean-Up

  • Wipe down and sanitise all equipment
  • Return mats, bands, and balls to storage
  • Check for forgotten belongings
  • Wash cool towels in bacterial wash: roll and return to fridge
  • Restore barbells to racks
  • Return main lights to normal
  • Ensure toilet is closed
  • Check the timetable and set the gym up for the next class. The last class of the day sets up for the first class of the following morning. There is always a next class.

Admin

  • Record attendance in Trainerize
  • Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with any issues
