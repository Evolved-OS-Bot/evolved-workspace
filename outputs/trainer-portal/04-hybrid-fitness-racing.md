# Course 4: Hybrid Fitness Racing

**GHL Title:** Hybrid Fitness Racing Sessions
**Description:** Hybrid Fitness Racing combines cardio, strength, and endurance in a high-energy, race-style format. This course covers setup, delivery, member experience, and managing the unique demands of this class.
**Pass mark:** 80%
**Prerequisite:** Course 3: Metabolic Burn HIIT

## Lessons

### Lesson 1: Class Overview

- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Capacity:** 15 members
- **Format:** Circuit or race-style: stations alternate between cardio bursts, strength exercises, and endurance challenges
- **Equipment:** Rowers, assault bikes, ski erg, dumbbells, kettlebells, plyometric boxes, pull-up bars, cones

Hybrid Fitness Racing is the most equipment-intensive class at The Evolved. Setup requires more time and attention. Arrive ready.

---

### Lesson 2: Where Hybrid Fitness Racing Fits in the Evolved Method

Hybrid Fitness Racing sits within The Evolved's **Intense Cardio Exercise (ICE)** framework: the same category as Metabolic Burn. ICE is a separate standard from the strength KPH.

**What ICE means:**
- Intensity of RPE 7 or higher
- Work sets at high intensity followed by rest or lower-intensity periods
- Work-to-rest ratio of approximately 1:1 to 1:1.5

**How Hybrid Racing differs from Metabolic Burn:**

Metabolic Burn is designed with a strict interval structure, intensity up, rest, repeat. Hybrid Fitness Racing blurs the lines: it combines cardio machines, strength stations, and endurance challenges in a circuit or race format that is not always structured in clean intervals. For most members it functions as ICE, so it is kept in the same category for simplicity. Be aware that the intensity curve is less predictable, your job is to read the room and keep members working hard throughout.

**ICE is complementary to strength, not a substitute.**

Strength training (Sculpt & Strength, Build & Balance, 1:1 PT) builds muscle mass, bone density, and hormonal health through periodised progressive loading. Hybrid Fitness Racing develops **Work Capacity**, one of the Eight Physical Assets of Longevity, but cannot replicate the strength adaptation. It is most beneficial for members who are already consistently hitting their strength KPH.

If a member attends only Hybrid Racing, they are not meeting the strength minimum. Educate them and guide them toward adding strength sessions.

### Lesson 3: Pre-Session Checklist

Arrive **10–15 minutes early** (slightly longer than other classes due to station setup).

**Equipment Setup:**
- Arrange stations according to the session layout
- Test all machines, rowers, assault bikes, ski erg, for functionality before members arrive
- Confirm all stations are ready before the first member walks in

**Environment:**
- Fans adjusted as needed
- Music at appropriate volume and playlist
- **Party lights on and set to beat with the music**: there are two, one in each corner of the room. The remote is kept underneath the TV: buttons on the remote make the beat mode straightforward to set.
- Adequate lighting

**Safety:**
- Inspect all equipment: especially cardio machines
- Walkways and transition zones clear of obstructions
- First aid and AED accessible and stocked

### Lesson 4: Delivering the Session

**Pre-class briefing:**
Hybrid Fitness Racing requires a clear pre-class brief. Members need to understand the layout, the stations, and the format before the clock starts.

- Explain the session layout and objectives
- Walk through each station and demonstrate proper technique, including modifications
- Cover safety protocols: especially for high-intensity cardio machines

**Warm-Up (5–10 minutes):**
- Use the prescribed warm-up from Trainerize
- Give alternatives within your scope for individual needs
- If unsure: deliver what's prescribed, apologise, ask management ASAP

**Main Session (30–40 minutes):**
- Follow the Trainerize workout: adhere to sets, timing, and movement patterns
- Participants complete stations sequentially or in teams depending on the session format
- Actively monitor all participants for safety and motivation throughout
- Give progressions (heavier loads, time-based challenges) and regressions (reduced range of motion, lighter loads) as needed

**Member experience:**
- Provide **cool towels** to members during the session

**Pushing vs protecting:** Your default position is to push members harder, not hold them back: encouraging language, calling out effort, and leading from the front with your own energy are your primary tools. That said, members can sometimes push themselves too hard. If a member looks faint, pale, or overly distressed: slow them down immediately, get them a glass of water, and offer fast-absorbing carbohydrates like Powerade to support recovery.

When this happens, acknowledge their effort positively: pushing that hard is a sign of real commitment. Reassure them that it doesn't happen every session, and let them know that next session you'll use it as a reference point to calibrate their intensity better. How you handle the moment is what keeps members coming back to this type of training.

**Cool-Down (5–10 minutes):**
- Use the prescribed cool-down from Trainerize

### Lesson 5: Post-Session & Troubleshooting

**Farewell:**
- Tell everyone what a great job they did
- Congratulate 2–3 members individually: identify 1 win or 1 improvement and offer a class recommendation or complimentary 1:1 PT session. To action a complimentary session: name one specific time you are already paid to be in the gym and have free time, do not offer to the same member more than once per 6 months, and book it on the spot in Lead Connector
- Ask when they're coming in next

**Clean-Up:**
- Wipe down and sanitise all equipment: especially cardio machines
- Check the timetable and set the gym up for the next class. The last class of the day sets up for the first class of the following morning. There is always a next class.
- Check for forgotten belongings: store in lost and found
- Wash cool towels in bacterial wash: roll and return to fridge
- Ensure toilet is closed

**Admin:**
- Record attendance in Trainerize
- Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with any issues

**Troubleshooting:**

| Issue | Response |
|---|---|
| Overcrowded stations | Adjust timing or add stations to balance participants |
| Fatigue or overexertion | Active recovery options: walking, light stretching between rounds |
| Equipment problems | Alternative exercises ready for each equipment-dependent station |
| No clients show | Stay on premises, complete approved alternative activities. Text 0402 840 247 to notify of the no-show and confirm what you'll be doing. See Course 8, Lesson 6 for the full list of approved activities |
