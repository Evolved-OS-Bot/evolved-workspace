# Course 5: Evolved Pilates

**GHL Title:** Evolved Pilates Sessions
**Description:** Evolved Pilates is a dynamic, full-body session focused on core strength, flexibility, posture, and mindful movement. The environment and delivery style are distinct from other classes: this course covers everything you need to set it up and deliver it correctly.
**Pass mark:** 80%
**Prerequisite:** Course 4: Hybrid Fitness Racing

---

## Lessons

### Lesson 1: Class Overview

- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Capacity:** 15 members
- **Focus:** Core stability, mobility, controlled movement, and breathwork
- **Equipment:** Mats, resistance bands, small Pilates balls, light dumbbells (optional)

Evolved Pilates is calming, precise, and accessible to all fitness levels. The environment is intentionally different from every other class at The Evolved: the setup and your energy reflect that.

---

### Lesson 2: Where Evolved Pilates Fits in the Evolved Method

Evolved Pilates sits in The Evolved's **Less Intense Activity** category: distinct from both strength training (Sculpt & Strength, Build & Balance) and Intense Cardio Exercise (Metabolic Burn, Hybrid Racing).

**What this means:**
- Pilates does not meet the strength KPH: it does not generate sufficient progressive loading to drive meaningful muscle mass, bone density, or hormonal adaptation for most women
- It is not classified as ICE: the intensity is lower and the format is not interval-based
- Members can outgrow the resistance stimulus in Pilates relatively quickly, at which point the session no longer provides a significant physical adaptation challenge

**What Pilates does provide:**
- Mobility development: improved range of motion and joint control
- Core strength in prone, supine, and unilateral positions
- Breathwork: supporting the nervous system and reinforcing the diaphragm-pelvic floor relationship taught in strength sessions
- Active recovery: a session that supports training without adding to fatigue

Evolved Pilates is most valuable as a complement to strength training, not as a standalone program. If a member attends only Pilates, they are not meeting the strength minimum. Guide them toward adding strength sessions.

### Lesson 3: Pre-Session Checklist

Arrive **15 minutes early**.

**Unique environment requirements for Pilates:**
- **Leave main lights OFF: turn festoon lights ON**
- **Move barbells off racks: lay flat on floor against the back wall, clear of walkways**

These two steps are mandatory. They transform the feel of the space from a strength gym into a calm, focused environment appropriate for Pilates.

**Cleanliness:** Equipment clean. Floors and mirrors clear.

**Equipment:** Mats laid out, resistance bands and Pilates balls available.

**Safety:** First aid and AED accessible. Walkways clear.

**Music:** Low volume, calm playlist appropriate for Pilates.

---

### Lesson 4: Delivering the Session: Environment and Energy

Before the session content: the environment is the first delivery decision you make in Evolved Pilates. It must be set up correctly or the session fails before it begins.

The correct setup (covered in Lesson 3: Pre-Session Checklist):
- Main lights off, festoon lights on
- Barbells moved off racks and laid flat against the back wall
- Low volume, calm playlist

Your energy must match the room. Evolved Pilates is more soothing and calming than Sculpt & Strength, Build & Balance, Metabolic Burn, or Hybrid Racing. Lower voice, slower cues, longer pauses. Members who come to this class value that environment: protect it.

---

### Lesson 4: Coaching with PRIME in Pilates

The same PRIME coaching lens applies in Evolved Pilates as in every other Evolved class. The framework doesn't change: the pace, intensity, and environment do.

**P, Posture:** In Pilates, posture begins on the floor. Is the spine neutral in the lying position? Is the pelvis controlled? Are the shoulders drawing back? Establish this before any movement begins, it is the foundation of everything that follows.

**R, Range of Motion:** Pilates is an opportunity to expand range of motion in a low-load environment. Work within the member's available range and gently extend it. Never force, earn it. Breathwork supports range expansion.

**I: Identify Areas of Improvement:** Observe before you intervene. In a 60-minute Pilates session you have more time per member than in a strength class. Use it. One well-timed, precise cue is worth ten rushed ones.

**M, Measure & Monitor:** Members record the reps they achieve within the time frame for each exercise in Trainerize. This gives members who want to improve a clear target to beat next session, and it gives you data to track progression over time. Encourage members to record during the session, not after.

**E, Evolve:** Progress load, complexity, or range once the member demonstrates consistent control. Pilates movements have meaningful progressions, from floor-based to standing, from bilateral to unilateral, from bodyweight to light load.

**Breathing in Pilates:** Breath is not a background detail: it is a coaching cue. Reinforce the diaphragmatic breathing pattern taught in strength sessions. Members who breathe well in Pilates carry that habit into their deadlifts and squats. Cue breath in the lying warm-up, then transfer it through every movement.

### Lesson 6: Post-Session & Troubleshooting

**Farewell:**
- Acknowledge the effort: Pilates demands mental focus, not just physical
- Tell everyone what a great job they did
- Congratulate 2–3 members individually: identify 1 win or 1 improvement and offer a class recommendation or complimentary 1:1 PT session. To action a complimentary session: name one specific time you are already paid to be in the gym and have free time, do not offer to the same member more than once per 6 months, and book it on the spot in Lead Connector
- Ask when they're coming in next

**Clean-Up:**
- Wipe down and sanitise all equipment
- Return mats, bands, and balls to storage
- Check for forgotten belongings
- Wash cool towels in bacterial wash: roll and return to fridge
- **Restore barbells to racks**
- **Return main lights to normal**
- Ensure toilet is closed
- Check the timetable and set the gym up for the next class. The last class of the day sets up for the first class of the following morning. There is always a next class.

**Admin:**
- Record attendance in Trainerize
- Email info@theevolvedgym.com.au with any issues

**Troubleshooting:**

| Issue | Response |
|---|---|
| Low energy or disengagement | Soften your cues, lower your voice, and slow the pace — match the member's state, then gradually bring them up |
| Injury or discomfort | Stop the exercise for that individual, assess, offer a modification or rest. Follow emergency procedures if needed and document |
| Equipment malfunction | Substitute with bodyweight or band equivalent: notify management immediately |
| No clients show | Stay on premises, complete approved alternative activities. Text 0402 840 247 to notify of the no-show and confirm what you'll be doing. See Course 8, Lesson 6 for the full list of approved activities |
