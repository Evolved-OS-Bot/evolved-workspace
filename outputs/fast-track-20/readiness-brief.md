## Evidence-Based Eligibility

Data creates a review order, not an automatic sales list.

### Mandatory Gates

A member must:

1. be a verified active Strong member;
2. have no current hold, Notice Active, cancellation, unresolved billing, or identity exception;
3. not already receive recurring PT;
4. have completed Strong onboarding and normally have at least 30 days of membership history;
5. have an approved coaching need within trainer scope;
6. state a goal that weekly individual coaching can materially support;
7. accept one exact recurring 30-minute slot for at least 13 weeks;
8. accept the full $149 weekly rate and approved upfront treatment; and
9. have coach and Peter approval before contact.

### Coaching Need Evidence

At least one must be documented:

- a current assessment result below an applicable Live standard;
- a technique or movement-control limitation requiring individual coaching;
- injury or medical-condition adaptation within the trainer's scope;
- stalled progress on a member-prioritised strength outcome;
- incomplete movement-pattern coverage requiring individual work; or
- a specific, time-bound strength or performance goal that group coaching alone cannot efficiently address.

An old or missing assessment record is a prompt to validate, not proof of need. The current performance dataset includes legacy gaps, so a reassessment may be required before the offer.

### Engagement and Readiness

The default gate is at least four completed workouts in the prior 30 days. Lower attendance normally requires an attendance intervention first, unless the coach documents why weekly PT is the appropriate solution.

The current objective screen identified 36 Strong members who are:

- decision-grade active;
- not in Notice Active;
- not already in `Active PT`;
- at full weekly price or recorded PIF;
- active in recent training; and
- supported by current performance data.

The initial 25-member review order is stored privately. Every member still requires coaching-need, goal, schedule, and commercial validation.

### Final Eligibility Score

Use a 15-point coach-reviewed score after mandatory gates:

| Domain | Points | Evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Coaching need | 0–4 | Assessment, technique, condition adaptation, or progression evidence |
| Engagement | 0–3 | Recent completed training and ability to use the service |
| Goal fit | 0–3 | Member-stated goal that weekly PT directly supports |
| Schedule fit | 0–3 | Exact recurring slot plus 13-week commitment |
| Coach confidence | 0–2 | Coach believes weekly PT is the right next service |

Minimum eligibility is 10 out of 15 plus every mandatory gate. A high score cannot override missing capacity or an unsafe coaching situation.

## Economics and Margin

Two upgrades generate $100 of incremental weekly revenue and require one trainer hour. Gross incremental yield is therefore $100 per reserved trainer hour before employment cost.

The contribution calculation is:

```text
Weekly contribution
= validated upgrades × $50
- incremental trainer hours × loaded employment cost per hour
- any incremental payment or delivery overhead
```

No contribution margin is decision-grade until trainer wage and payroll on-cost are supplied by the capacity workstream.

## Stop, Continue, Expand

### Stop

- Any true incumbent has no owned delivery resolution.
- Fewer than ten approved weekly trainer hours exist for the stretch plan.
- An offered member lacks an exact recurring slot.
- A slot compromises SGPT delivery or floor safety.
- The existing-member payment or onboarding path remains ambiguous.

### Continue

- Incumbents have healthy delivery or approved dated exceptions.
- Full-price offers convert without concessions.
- Every accepted member is booked and provisioned correctly.

### Expand

- All first sessions are delivered.
- No unresolved booking, billing, provisioning, floor, or coaching-quality issue remains.
