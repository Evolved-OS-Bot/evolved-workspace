# Fast Track Upgrade System

**Owner decision:** assessment-led upgrade journey; not a pilot and not a direct upsell.  
**Version:** 0.1 — staff-ready design, release blocked.  
**Scope:** current SGPT members only. This document authorises no contact, booking, membership, payment, policy, workflow or course change.

## 1. Purpose and operating principle

The coach’s job is to help an established SGPT member understand her current standards, progress and next six to twelve months of training. Every one of the 20 standards in the canonical framework must be available in the current-member assessment, including Deadlift, Barbell Romanian Deadlift and Squat.

The coach selects one appropriate test per movement pattern or applicable fitness modality from the member’s goals, readiness, safety and evidence. A test that is not appropriate today may remain blank when the coach records a specific `Not tested` reason; blank does not mean failed or incomplete and does not select the member’s track.

After capturing the exact exercise, setup, load or assistance, raw result, form and safety evidence, the coach separately selects the applicable Live, Long or Perform direction. There is no universal composite score or fixed selection algorithm for this assessment.

The default result is a stronger SGPT plan. Fast Track is discussed only where weekly individual coaching is the best-fit route and every delivery, commercial and member-safety condition is true.

This system reuses the canonical three-part Strength Assessment and Evolved Standards Framework. It does **not** create a Fast Track-specific screen, abbreviated assessment, second “Full Foundations Assessment,” new Trainerize profile, prospect pathway, lead qualification, Sales/No-Sale result, or acquisition workflow.

## 2. Non-negotiable release rule

Do not invite, book, offer, accept payment, change service or provision Fast Track until the relevant gate below is passed.

| Gate | Pass condition | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Current-member assessment route | A safe booking, record, attendance and result process is approved without prospect lifecycle or Sales/No-Sale effects. | Owner / operations |
| Incumbent protection | Every current Fast Track member has a verified delivery disposition and protected continuity. | Operations / PT lead |
| Assessment inventory | Named assessment coach, session type/calendar, weekly inventory and cover are approved. | PT lead |
| Fast Track capacity | A named recurring 30-minute trainer slot plus cover exists for the individual member, with the required horizon. | PT lead / operations |
| Commercial path | Existing-member agreement, A$149 weekly payment boundary, service-change and provisioning path are approved. | Owner / admin |
| Member clearance | Lifecycle/payment, consent/suppression, identity, health/PAR-Q and suitability checks pass. | Assigned coach / admin |

Until all relevant gates pass, this is a preparation and assessment design only. No coach may use an assessment to imply an available place or a future Fast Track conversion.

## 4. Who to invite

### 4.1 Qualification screen

An invitation is earned through genuine evidence, never a generic campaign list. The assigned coach checks the protected candidate register and confirms all required conditions.

| Required evidence | Standard |
|---|---|
| Current service | Active SGPT, normally current A$99 Bronze/Strong route; not already receiving active PT/Fast Track. |
| Training consistency | Prefer at least four recorded workouts in the prior 30 days, sustained recent engagement and/or verifiable progress. A lower-consistency case requires a written coach rationale that weekly PT is the right intervention. |
| Progress signal | Recent training, assessment progression, technical bottleneck, reassessment need, coach-observed goal or a member-stated progress need. Trainerize workout logs are supporting evidence, not attendance proof. |
| Coaching fit | A plausible weekly 1:1 focus exists: technique, safe progression, accountability, managed constraint within scope, or a specific goal. |
| Lifecycle/commercial | Identity, active entitlement, payment, cancellation/notice/hold state and current service all reconcile. |
| Member safeguards | No suppression/consent issue; PAR-Q/current health context supports the proposed review, or a clearance/modification route is agreed. |
| Delivery fit | A potential recurring trainer/slot and cover are available before the conversion conversation; never promise it in the invitation. |

### 4.2 Do not invite / defer

Do not invite when the member has active PT/Fast Track; hold, cancellation, notice or payment/identity exception; consent/suppression concern; unresolved health issue; no coach-supported need; poor engagement without a documented rationale; or no credible assessment/ongoing PT capacity.

### 4.3 Evidence log before invitation

Record in the protected register:

- candidate identity and current SGPT service;
- dates/counts of relevant training records and the source;
- prior/current assessment evidence and the specific progress or coaching signal;
- the coach’s one-sentence invite rationale;
- lifecycle/payment/consent/suppression checks and reviewer;
- assessment inventory placeholder; and
- status: approved to invite, defer, or exclude.

## 5. Invitation and booking

### 5.1 Member-facing framing

Call the session a **Full Standards Assessment and Training Plan**. It is a review of progress, current foundations and a six-to-twelve-month training plan. It is not an offer appointment.

**Invitation script**

> Hi [Name] — I’ve been reviewing your recent training and noticed [specific genuine progress/consistency signal]. I’d love to offer you a Full Standards Assessment and Training Plan: we’ll review your current strength foundations, what matters most to you now, and map your next six to twelve months of training. You’ll leave with a clear next focus for your SGPT training. There’s no obligation to change anything; it’s a progress and planning session. Would you like me to look at the available review times?

Use only the actual evidence. Do not say “you’ve been selected,” imply a free PT session, mention Fast Track, or use a generic progress claim.

### 5.2 Booking checklist

Before booking:

- confirm the current-member assessment route and session type are approved;
- confirm coach, duration, location/calendar, required equipment and cover;
- review existing assessment, Trainerize program, goals, notes, current service and safety context;
- confirm the member is permitted to receive the invitation under consent/suppression controls;
- book only through the approved current-member route; and
- record the appointment identifier and assigned coach in the protected register.

Do not use prospect lead qualification, create a new Trainerize account, purchase the Initial Consultation product, or enter prospect Sales/No-Sale outcomes.

## 6. Full Standards Assessment and Training Plan

### 6.1 Prepare

Review known member history before the appointment. Ask only what has changed: goal, why, time horizon, current SGPT experience, barriers, preferred support, availability, injury/condition context and current safety status. Use the PAR-Q/current safety gate, modification, stop and referral rules from the canonical assessment system.

### 6.2 Deliver one assessment, not two

Use the canonical Strength Assessment/Standards Framework:

1. Goals and consultation.
2. Full standards review: make all 20 canonical options available across hinge, squat, lunge, vertical push, horizontal push, vertical pull, horizontal pull, carry, core and fitness. Select one appropriate test per movement pattern or applicable modality; record the coach-selected test and rationale. Do not substitute the intake-only single-leg/grip/spinal assessment.
3. Results, prescription and plan.

Record the exact exercise, setup, load or assistance, bodyweight percentage, raw result, range, form and safety evidence in the existing member’s Trainerize record. Explain Live, Long and Perform as capacity milestones, not a pass/fail result and not a diagnosis.

For a selected main loaded test, complete the canonical Performance Warm-Up once: 20% × 12 reps, 60% × 6 reps and 100% × 1 acclimation rep. Keep the sets controlled and away from failure; reduce the target or stop if the acclimation rep feels extremely hard or movement safety breaks down.

For each selected loaded test, use two 1–3RM attempts. Attempt 1 uses the Trainerize-estimated 1RM and attempt 2 is adjusted up or down from the result; members with fewer than four weeks of consistent attendance do not complete maximal testing.

Do not place the Performance Warm-Up between every option or apply loaded-test attempt logic to Core or fitness modalities. Record the reason whenever readiness makes maximal testing inappropriate.

Modify, omit or stop testing according to the canonical safety rules. An unused option may remain blank only when the coach records its specific `Not tested` reason; it is not a failed result and is separate from the selected test and track.

The standards sources give authoritative tests and thresholds, but not a universal full-assessment test-selection algorithm, composite score or test order across every category/modality. The coach must record the selected test and evidence-led rationale rather than inventing a rule.

**Assessment transition script**

> Thank you — I’m hearing that your priority is [goal] by [time], and that [barrier/support need] matters most. Let’s look at your current foundations and compare them with where you want to go. This is not a pass or fail test; it helps us choose the safest and most useful next progression.

### 6.3 Assessment output

By the end, document:

- member-stated primary goal, why and target timeframe;
- relevant health/safety modification or referral decision;
- one current full-standards evidence set: all 20 options available, selected canonical test per movement pattern or applicable modality, raw result and setup, applicable level or track, form and safety evidence, and explicit `Not tested` reasons where used;
- strengths, limiting factor(s), asymmetry or next progression;
- initial 8–12-week focus;
- six-month and twelve-month direction;
- recommended service outcome: SGPT, Fast Track discussion eligible, or defer/refer; and
- follow-up/reassessment date and owner.

## 7. The six-to-twelve-month training plan

The plan is the product of the session. It must stand on its own if the member stays in SGPT.

| Horizon | Coach and member agree |
|---|---|
| Next 8–12 weeks | One to three measurable training focuses, SGPT weekly rhythm, next exercise/progression, habit/accountability support, and the evidence to review. |
| Months 3–6 | Expected block outcome, standards/progression check, coaching review date and adjustments for barriers. |
| Months 6–12 | Meaningful goal/standards direction, sustainable training rhythm, next reassessment window and any referral/support dependency. |

Do not promise a specific strength result, body-composition outcome, pain outcome or medical benefit. Use realistic progress language anchored in the member’s training history and safe capacity.

## 8. Fit-based Fast Track conversation

### 8.1 Decision rule

The coach may discuss Fast Track only after all are true:

1. The member has stated a specific need for weekly individual coaching.
2. The assessment identifies a concrete focus weekly 30-minute PT can address alongside SGPT.
3. Recent engagement indicates she can use the inclusion.
4. Lifecycle/payment/identity/consent/suppression checks pass.
5. Incumbent Fast Track delivery is protected.
6. A named recurring trainer slot and cover are actually available.
7. The member wants to hear the option and accepts the full-price model.

If any condition fails, do not pitch Fast Track. Keep the SGPT plan, defer pending the missing condition, or refer/escalate as appropriate.

### 8.2 Conversation script

> Your plan can work well in SGPT, and the immediate focus is [8–12-week focus]. You also said [stated weekly coaching need], and today we saw [assessment-derived focus]. One option, if you want more individual support, is Fast Track: it keeps your SGPT membership and adds one scheduled 30-minute 1:1 PT appointment each week. Would you like me to explain it, or would you prefer to continue with the SGPT plan we’ve agreed?

If she wants to hear it:

> Fast Track is A$149 per week. It includes SGPT plus one scheduled 30-minute weekly PT appointment. If you reschedule with at least 24 hours notice, that appointment may be rebooked in the same service week or the following service week only; it cannot accumulate. Changes inside 24 hours and no-shows forfeit that appointment. We only proceed if we can match you with a recurring trainer time that works for you and complete the approved service-change process. How does that fit with what you want?

Keep the language factual. Do not create urgency, make medical claims, discount, or suggest that SGPT is inadequate.

### 8.3 Alternatives

| Member situation | Coach response |
|---|---|
| SGPT is sufficient | Confirm the 8–12-week SGPT plan and review date. |
| Wants individual support but no slot | Do not take payment or promise a place; record interest and continue SGPT plan. |
| Wants more coaching but cannot sustain weekly PT | Keep SGPT plan; explore any separately approved service option only if appropriate. |
| Health/technical issue outside scope | Pause service conversation; follow modification, clearance or referral route. |
| Needs time | Give the written plan. No pressure, no expiry claim, no booking/payment. Record “defer—member considering.” |

## 9. Actions for every outcome

### Yes — member chooses Fast Track

Coach says:

> Great. Before anything changes, we’ll confirm the recurring trainer time, complete the approved agreement and payment steps, and verify your service setup. Your SGPT plan remains in place until those checks are complete.

Then stop the assessment process and complete the post-yes gates in Section 10. An interest or verbal yes is not a conversion, booking, payment authorisation or confirmed service change.

### No — member stays in SGPT

Coach says:

> That makes sense. Your SGPT plan is [focus], and we’ll review [measure] on [date/window]. We can revisit the level of support later if your needs change.

Record the plan and review date. Do not re-pitch.

### Defer — member wants time, capacity is unavailable, or evidence is incomplete

Coach says:

> You do not need to decide today. Let’s keep the training plan we agreed and resolve [specific missing item] before we revisit the support options. I won’t make any change unless it is right for you and the delivery is confirmed.

Record the exact defer reason, owner and review point. Do not promise a time or slot.

### Refer / not suitable now

Coach says:

> Based on what we found today, the right next step is [SGPT modification / clearance / allied-health referral / follow-up] rather than adding weekly PT right now. We’ll keep your plan focused on that.

Record the safe next action and do not offer Fast Track.

## 10. Post-yes gates: capacity, payment, service change and provisioning

All must pass before a conversion is counted or an appointment is booked.

1. **Capacity:** PT lead verifies named trainer, recurring weekday/time, 30-minute duration, cover, floor/calendar conflict, start date and required booking horizon. Confirm incumbent protection first.
2. **Member confirmation:** Coach confirms that the recurring time, trainer and terms are acceptable to the member.
3. **Commercial approval:** Admin applies the owner-approved existing-member A$149/week agreement and payment boundary. Do not invent or reuse new-sale upfront/onboarding treatment.
4. **Lifecycle/service change:** Admin verifies current service, start/effective date, no incompatible hold/cancellation/notice state and the approved service-change workflow.
5. **Provisioning:** Complete the approved SGPT + PT entitlement, Trainerize/program continuity, relevant access, payment and record updates without duplicate identity, profile, product or booking.
6. **Booking and verification:** Create the approved recurring PT arrangement only after the above. Verify the recurring appointment, trainer, cover, service and payment evidence.
7. **First-delivery check:** Count a validated upgrade only after the agreed service is active, evidence is reconciled and the first included PT delivery is completed or an approved exception is recorded.

## 12. Evidence and logging standard

Keep identified member evidence in the protected Fast Track register and governed operating systems. The coach records facts and source/date, not assumptions.

Minimum record:

- invite evidence and script variation used;
- assessment appointment identifier, coach and attendance outcome under the approved current-member route;
- raw standards results, goal and plan;
- Fast Track decision-rule checks;
- member outcome and exact words where relevant;
- capacity/trainer/cover evidence;
- agreement/payment/service-change/provisioning verification; and
- exception, owner, due date and closure evidence.

The reporting sheet is not an authority for entitlement or lifecycle. GHL governs lifecycle, the approved payment source governs entitlement, Trainerize supports training/assessment evidence, and the governed booking record supports delivery.
