## 2. Assessment rule

Every one of the 20 canonical standards below must be available to the coach. Select one appropriate test for each movement pattern or applicable fitness modality using the member's goals, readiness, safety and existing evidence.

An option that is not appropriate today may remain blank. Record `Not tested` and the specific readiness, safety, relevance or evidence reason; a blank or omitted option is not a failed result and does not determine the member's selected track.

For every tested option record:

- exact exercise and variation;
- setup, range and equipment;
- load, bodyweight percentage or assistance;
- raw time, distance, reps or load result;
- form, PRIME and safety evidence;
- side-specific evidence where applicable; and
- highest fully attained Live, Long or Perform standard.

The coach selects the applicable standard or track separately after reviewing the evidence. There is no universal composite score, fixed test-selection algorithm or automatic track assignment for this assessment.

## 2.1 Canonical loaded-test safety protocol

If the selected main test is loaded strength, complete the Performance Warm-Up first. Use the member's target working weight: 20% × 12 reps, 60% × 6 reps, then 100% × 1 acclimation rep.

Warm-up sets are controlled and not taken to failure. If the acclimation rep feels extremely hard, or posture, range or safety breaks down, reduce the target or stop the test.

Use Trainerize exercise history to identify the estimated 1RM for each selected loaded test. Complete two test attempts: attempt 1 at the estimated 1RM, then adjust attempt 2 up or down from the result.

Use a 1–3RM prescription, with more reps and less load for beginners and fewer reps and more load for advanced members. A member with fewer than four weeks of consistent attendance does not complete maximal testing; record the reason and use an appropriate normal session or existing evidence instead.

The Performance Warm-Up is completed once for the selected main loaded test, not between every assessment option. Core and fitness modalities retain their standard-specific test protocol.
