## Trainerize custom exercise descriptions

### Single Arm DB Reverse Lunge

**P | Posture**

Stand tall with ribs stacked over pelvis, pelvis level and the front foot rooted. Hold one dumbbell on the prescribed side and do not lean toward the load.

**R | Range of Motion**

Step back far enough to keep the front shin close to vertical. Lower under control to the deepest safe range with the front knee tracking over the mid-foot, then return without losing balance.

**I | Identify Areas of Improvement**

Watch for torso lean, pelvic rotation, knee collapse, a short step, loss of balance or a meaningful difference between sides.

**M | Measure and Monitor**

Record bodyweight, dumbbell load, loaded side, working leg, raw repetitions on each side, depth, RPE, form and safety evidence. The canonical standards are Live 20% bodyweight, Long 40% and Perform 60%.

**E | Evaluate and Evolve**

Evaluate each side separately. Consolidate or regress when posture, range or control breaks, and progress load or range only when both sides are repeatable and safe.

### Performance Warm-Up

**P | Posture**

Use the exact setup and bracing required for the selected main loaded test. Keep every rep controlled and stop if posture or safety breaks down.

**R | Range of Motion**

Use the same safe range intended for the test. Complete 20% of target working weight × 12 reps, 60% × 6 reps, then 100% × 1 acclimation rep.

**I | Identify Areas of Improvement**

Watch for pain, apprehension, compensation, poor control or an acclimation rep that feels extremely hard. Reduce the target or stop when readiness is not confirmed.

**M | Measure and Monitor**

Record the selected main test, target working weight, all three warm-up loads, completed reps, RPE, form and safety evidence. Warm-up sets are not working sets and are never taken to failure.

**E | Evaluate and Evolve**

Use this protocol once for the selected main loaded test only. If the acclimation rep is controlled, proceed to the test; otherwise adjust or stop.

Do not use this between every exercise or for Core and fitness modalities.

## Video assets to record

Create two final Trainerize-ready MP4 files. Each should be a 45 to 75 second, 1080p, 16:9 landscape master filmed in a quiet, well-lit gym with the full movement visible.

### 1. `single-arm-db-reverse-lunge-prime.mp4`

- Show the start position from the front and a full repetition from a front three-quarter angle.
- Show the side view that confirms step length, a near-vertical front shin and controlled depth.
- Demonstrate both loading sides and explain that each working leg is assessed separately.
- Include brief contrasts for torso lean, pelvic rotation, knee collapse and an excessively short step.
- Finish with the recording prompt: bodyweight, dumbbell load, loaded side, working leg, reps, depth, RPE, form, safety and asymmetry.

### 2. `performance-warm-up-prime.mp4`

- Show one selected main loaded exercise and state the target working weight before the first set.
- Demonstrate 20% × 12 reps, 60% × 6 reps and 100% × 1 acclimation rep with the exact test setup and safe range.
- Explain that every set is controlled and away from failure.
- Show the stop or adjustment trigger: an extremely hard acclimation rep, pain, compensation, or loss of posture, range or safety.
- State that this is completed once for the selected main loaded test only, not between exercises and not for Core or fitness modalities.
- Finish with the recording prompt: selected test, target working weight, all three loads, completed reps, RPE, form and safety.

## Production and upload checks

- Use the exact custom exercise title in Trainerize and in the final filename.
- Keep demonstrations technically clean and use the canonical coaching language.
- Do not add a universal composite score or a fixed track-selection algorithm.
- Upload the video to its matching custom exercise only after the final coaching review.
- Confirm the video, PRIME instructions and title all read back correctly after upload.
