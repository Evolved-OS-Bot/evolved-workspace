# Fast Track 20: assessment-led release design

**Status:** Current-member assessment content corrected. Outreach, booking, membership, payment and service-change release remain governed separately.

**Canonical authority:** `reference/evolved-manual/03b-standards-framework.md`

## Recommendation

Use the dedicated **Women's Full Evolved Standards Assessment** for current-member reassessment. Do not use or alter the acquisition workout **Women's Standard Strength Assessment**.

The current-member assessment is a goals, standards and training-plan session. It is not the four-result acquisition assessment, a Fast Track screen or a prospect sales appointment.

Every one of the 20 canonical standards must be available to the trainer. The trainer selects one appropriate test per movement pattern or applicable fitness modality from the member's goals, readiness, safety and existing evidence.

An option may remain blank when it is not appropriate today. The trainer records a specific `Not tested` reason; blank does not mean failed or incomplete, and omitted tests do not select the member's track.

## Current-member assessment contract

1. Review the existing member's training history, current goals and changed health or safety context.
2. Make the complete canonical inventory available.
3. Select one appropriate test per movement pattern or applicable fitness modality.
4. Record the exact exercise, setup, load or assistance, bodyweight percentage, raw result, range, form and safety evidence.
5. Record left and right evidence separately where applicable.
6. Record a specific reason for each deliberately omitted option.
7. Separately select the applicable Live, Long or Perform direction from goals, readiness, safety and captured evidence.
8. Do not apply a universal composite score or fixed selection algorithm.
9. Produce an 8–12-week focus and a six-to-twelve-month direction that stands on its own in SGPT.
10. Discuss Fast Track only after the assessment and only where it fits.

For a selected main loaded test, use the canonical Performance Warm-Up once: 20% × 12 reps, 60% × 6 reps and 100% × 1 acclimation rep. Warm-up sets are not taken to failure; reduce the target or stop if the acclimation rep feels extremely hard or movement safety breaks down.

For each selected loaded test, use two 1–3RM attempts. Attempt 1 uses the Trainerize-estimated 1RM and attempt 2 is adjusted up or down from the result; members with fewer than four weeks of consistent attendance do not complete maximal testing.

Do not place the Performance Warm-Up between every option or apply loaded-test attempt logic to Core or fitness modalities. Record the reason whenever readiness makes maximal testing inappropriate.

The canonical Future-Proofing Score remains a separate six-standard, complete-only measure. It is not the selection algorithm for this assessment.

## Member journey

1. **Internal readiness check:** confirm current member status, health context, consent and delivery readiness.
2. **Invitation to review:** describe a progress, standards and training-plan session; do not promise Fast Track.
3. **Complete assessment:** use the dedicated current-member Trainerize asset and the full worksheet.
4. **Results and plan:** explain strengths, constraints and next progression; agree the SGPT plan first.
5. **Service decision:** continue SGPT by default; discuss Fast Track only if the member wants weekly individual support and all gates pass.
6. **Separate upgrade step:** agreement, payment, provisioning and booking occur only through the separately approved service-change route.

## Assessment agenda and outputs

| Segment | Content | Required output |
|---|---|---|
| Preparation | Existing results, program, goals, health context and readiness | Safe assessment scope |
| Goals | Goal, why, timeframe, barriers, support and availability | Member-authored direction |
| Full standards | All 20 options available; one appropriate test per pattern or applicable modality | Raw evidence, omitted-test reasons and side-specific results |
| Coach selection | Goals, readiness, safety and evidence | Separately recorded selected test and applicable track |
| Prescription | Strengths, constraint and next progression | 8–12-week focus plus 6–12-month direction |
| Service decision | SGPT-first fit assessment | Continue SGPT, defer or refer, or optional Fast Track discussion |

## Fast Track decision criteria

Discuss Fast Track only when all apply:

- the member wants weekly individual coaching for a specific need;
- the assessment identifies a concrete focus suitable for weekly 30-minute PT alongside SGPT;
- recent engagement supports use of the service;
- a named trainer, recurring slot and cover are genuinely available after incumbent protection;
- identity, lifecycle, payment, consent and safety checks pass; and
- the member wants to hear the full-price option.

If any condition fails, continue the SGPT plan, defer or refer. Completing the assessment never implies an upgrade.

## Release and protection gates

1. Use only the approved current-member booking, attendance and result route.
2. Protect the acquisition assessment and all prospect lifecycle semantics.
3. Keep the current-member Trainerize assessment unassigned until an authorised member process applies.
4. Protect incumbent Fast Track delivery before considering new capacity.
5. Apply the approved commercial and service-change path only after a member independently chooses Fast Track.
6. Record exact build state, asset IDs, assignment state and verification in the Trainerize build record.

## Governed cascade

- this release design;
- the worksheet;
- the coach guide;
- the Fast Track Upgrade System;
- trainer-course Markdown, HTML and affected quizzes;
- the live Trainerize assessment;
- `context/roadmap.md`; and
- `outputs/systems/trainerize-product-automation-audit.md`.

The downstream register and local validation script must pass before the assessment is described as current or trainer-ready.
