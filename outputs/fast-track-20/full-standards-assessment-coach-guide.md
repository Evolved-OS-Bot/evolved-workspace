# Women's Full Evolved Standards Assessment: Coach Guide

**Canonical authority:** `reference/evolved-manual/03b-standards-framework.md`

## Purpose

Use this for an established member's complete reassessment and training plan. Do not substitute the four-test acquisition Strength Assessment, create a new Trainerize profile or use prospect Sales/No-Sale fields.

Every one of the 20 canonical standards must be available as a trainer-selectable option. The coach chooses one appropriate test for each movement pattern or applicable fitness modality from the member's goals, readiness, safety and evidence.

Tests that are not appropriate today may remain blank. Record a specific `Not tested` reason; blank does not mean failed or incomplete, and omitted tests do not select the member's track.

## Before the session

1. Use the approved current-member route.
2. Review prior assessment, Trainerize program and results, current goals and health context.
3. Confirm the session is a Full Evolved Standards Assessment and Training Plan, not a sales appointment.
4. Prepare the worksheet, timer, load-calculation support and required equipment.

## Run the assessment

Open with:

> We are reviewing your progress, current strength foundations and the next six to twelve months of training. You will leave with a clear plan, and there is no obligation to change your membership.

Update only what has changed: goal, why, timeframe, SGPT experience, barriers, support, availability and safety context. Modify, omit or stop for pain, apprehension, restriction, poor control or a managed condition; refer or seek clearance where required.

If the selected main test is loaded strength, begin with the canonical Performance Warm-Up. Use the member's target working weight: 20% × 12 reps, 60% × 6 reps, then 100% × 1 acclimation rep.

Keep every warm-up set controlled and away from failure. If the acclimation rep feels extremely hard, or posture, range or safety breaks down, reduce the target or stop.

For each selected loaded test, use Trainerize exercise history to identify the estimated 1RM. Complete two test attempts: attempt 1 at the estimated 1RM, then adjust attempt 2 up or down from the result.

Use a 1–3RM prescription, with more reps and less load for beginners and fewer reps and more load for advanced members. Do not maximally test a member with fewer than four weeks of consistent attendance; record the reason and use an appropriate normal session or existing evidence.

The Performance Warm-Up is completed once for the selected main loaded test. Do not insert it between every assessment option, and do not apply loaded-test attempt logic to Core or fitness modalities.

For each tested option record the exact variation, setup, load or assistance, bodyweight percentage, raw result, range, form, PRIME and safety evidence. Record sides separately where the standard is unilateral.

## Select the member's direction

After evidence capture, separately record the selected test for each movement pattern or modality and the applicable Live, Long or Perform direction. Base the choice on goals, readiness, safety and captured evidence.

Do not calculate a universal composite score or apply a fixed selection algorithm. The canonical Future-Proofing Score is a separate six-standard, complete-only measure and is not the selection rule for this assessment.

## Write the plan first

Complete an 8–12-week SGPT focus plus the three-to-six-month and six-to-twelve-month direction. The plan must remain useful if the member never considers Fast Track.

Reflect:

> Your current strengths are [strengths]. The next most useful progression is [focus], and we will review [evidence] in [window].

## Fast Track only when it fits

Fast Track is an optional post-assessment discussion. Every condition must pass: stated weekly coaching need, assessment-derived focus, engagement, lifecycle/payment/identity/consent clearance, protected incumbent delivery, an actual recurring trainer slot with cover, and member interest.

No fit, capacity, clearance or interest means continue SGPT, defer or refer. A verbal yes is not an upgrade; approved capacity, payment, service-change and provisioning checks still apply.
