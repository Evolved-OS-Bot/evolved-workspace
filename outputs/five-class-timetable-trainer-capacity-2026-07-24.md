## Executive Decision

Do not approve five live classes yet. Trainer supply, not timetable space, is now the binding constraint.

The intended operating model is now:

- Piper works a 30-hour Monday-to-Friday morning pattern, opening at 5:00am.
- Nora works a 30-hour Monday-to-Friday afternoon and evening pattern, closing at 8:00pm Monday to Thursday and 7:00pm Friday.
- Nora owns Tuesday and Thursday 5:00pm, 6:00pm and 7:00pm, while Monday and Friday provide longer afternoon PT and small-group capacity.
- Piper and Nora have a one-hour Friday crossover for coaching alignment and camaraderie.
- Megan remains outside the recurring delivery roster.
- Leisa may deliver agreed sessions but is not treated as dependable evening cover.
- One dependable casual is recruited for afternoon, evening and weekend support.
- A morning-capable casual or later second part-time trainer is required if the morning PT book and 10:00am class expansion cannot fit sustainably within Piper's 30 hours.

Moving Nora to evenings creates two immediate transfer projects:

1. Nora's morning PT book cannot simply be placed into Piper's calendar. From 28 July to 26 October it contains 71 appointments and 45.5 hours. Only 44 appointments are clear at the same time against Piper's current appointments and class-roster baseline; 27 require a time change or another trainer. Some clients may instead stay with Nora by moving to her longer Monday or Friday afternoon windows.
2. Nora's Tuesday and Thursday 5:00pm PT clients conflict with Nora coaching the new classes. Anika, Kanika and Rose must transfer to the new casual, move time or move into Piper's morning service.

The five target additions remain commercially sound, but the launch sequence is now subordinate to recruitment and client-transfer capacity.

### Thirty-hour anchor roster arithmetic

Two 30-hour trainers provide 60 paid hours per week. Continuous trainer presence from 5:00am to 8:00pm across five weekdays requires 75 coverage hours before crossover, or 80 to 85 trainer-presence hours with one to two hours of crossover every day.

The 30/30 model can therefore create dependable opening and closing anchors, but it cannot provide uninterrupted daily coverage plus daily crossover. Peter has selected Friday as the sole crossover day; casual hours can fill the middle elsewhere only when demand justifies them.

One workable transition pattern is:

| Day | Piper AM span | Piper paid hours | Nora PM span | Nora paid hours | Crossover / gap |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| Monday | 5:00am–11:30am | 6.0 | 2:00pm–8:00pm | 5.5 | 2.5-hour middle gap |
| Tuesday | 5:00am–11:00am | 5.5 | 2:00pm–8:00pm | 5.5 | 3-hour middle gap |
| Wednesday | 5:00am–12:00pm | 6.5 | 1:30pm–8:00pm | 6.0 | 1.5-hour middle gap |
| Thursday | 5:00am–11:00am | 5.5 | 2:00pm–8:00pm | 5.5 | 3-hour middle gap |
| Friday | 5:00am–12:00pm | 6.5 | 11:00am–7:00pm | 7.5 | 1-hour crossover |
| **Total** |  | **30.0** |  | **30.0** | **1 crossover hour** |

Paid-hour figures assume a 30-minute unpaid meal break in the longer spans. Exact breaks, contracted start and finish times, and roster variations require payroll review and written agreement.

This pattern gives Nora Monday and Friday PT/small-group capacity, protects the Tuesday and Thursday evening class blocks, keeps both trainers connected from 11:00am to noon Friday, and leaves only 1.5-to-three-hour middle gaps Monday to Thursday. Nora finishes at 7:00pm Friday and 8:00pm on the other four weekdays.

### Resource-efficiency interpretation

On the audited future book, Piper currently averages about 8.1 PT hours per week. Her seven existing weekly class blocks, the three proposed class consolidations and approximately 1.75 weekly hours of Nora-client transfers would take her to roughly 20 direct-delivery hours inside a 30-hour role.

That is a credible anchor load while retaining about ten hours for breaks, opening, programming, member care, assessments, transitions and administration.

Nora's existing PM appointments average about 3.6 hours per week. Retaining the proposed morning clients in her PM book and owning the intended Tuesday/Thursday 5:00pm–7:00pm plus Friday 5:00pm/6:00pm blocks initially places her around 13 to 15 direct-delivery hours, depending on the confirmed Tuesday class roster and Wednesday handover.

Nora is therefore the larger efficiency risk, not Piper. Do not purchase a broad recurring casual roster while Nora has unused PM capacity. Use one dependable casual only for simultaneous PT, the early classes that clash with Piper's PT, and absence cover; then build Nora toward an explicit client-facing utilisation target before adding another permanent layer.

Moving Tuesday 5:30am and Friday 5:00pm/6:00pm off Jo/Leisa would remove three recurring casual class deliveries from the base roster. At the planning employment cost, that represents approximately $176 per week of avoidable casual coverage if 1.25 paid hours are currently attached to each delivery. Moving Friday 9:00am from Megan also returns one owner hour to higher-value work.

- Existing-member movement is tracked separately from net new members.

- No broad paid acquisition begins until the class date and trainer are approved.

### Stop, continue and expand

- **Stop or retime:** fewer than four average bookings after four weeks, no waitlist relief, or any coaching, payroll or floor breach.
- **Continue:** four to seven average bookings with clean delivery and credible member demand.
- **Expand:** eight or more average bookings, at least one net new member per class, reliable attendance capture, no unowned red flags and the next class's own gates passed.

Do not count an existing member moving from one class to another as new revenue. Track:

1. total bookings;
2. unique members;
3. actual attendance;
4. members displaced from other classes;
5. waitlist and turn-away demand;
6. net new active SGPT members; and
7. weekly revenue and wage contribution.
