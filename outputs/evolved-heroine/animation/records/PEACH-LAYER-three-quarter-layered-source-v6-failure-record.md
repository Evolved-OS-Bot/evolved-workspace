# PEACH-LAYER Three-Quarter Layered Source v6 Failure Record

**Status:** Rejected by articulation-source QA; preserved and never promoted  
**Build date:** 4 August 2026

Version 6 produced a smooth clean body field, retained the exact neutral match,
and passed file-level technical QA. Its controlled articulation sheet then
exposed triangular gaps under the shorts when each leg root rotated 12 degrees
outward.

The cause was a root capsule clipped to the neutral shorts footprint. When the
leg rotated, that clipped footprint rotated away from the leg opening.

Version 7 uses a complete narrow upper-leg capsule centred on each leg root.
The capsule remains behind the shorts at neutral, includes no anatomical thigh
or knee shaping, and rotates with the simple mascot tube.
