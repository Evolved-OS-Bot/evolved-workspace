## How to use this register

This is the permanent governance view for live GHL automation. It groups replicated workflows when the trigger, owner and control model are the same; exceptions are named separately.

- **Operational owner** is the person or role currently receiving the work in GHL.
- **Target owner** is the durable business role that should be accountable after open repairs are approved.
- **Workflow steward** owns configuration review, testing and documentation; it is not automatically the person completing every task.
- A workflow with no recent enrolments is not dormant when it has a valid event trigger and a recognised future use.

## Governance standard

Every Class A or B workflow family must have:

- one durable business owner;
- one workflow steward;
- a canonical workspace document;
- explicit entry, reply, exit and exception rules;
- role-based assignments where practical;
- a review date and evidence of the last live verification.

Names tied to current employees are acceptable only when the person is intentionally the business owner. Administrative routing should use Admin Eve or another durable role wherever GHL permits it.
